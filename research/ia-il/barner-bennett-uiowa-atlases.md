### Follow-up: the county poor farm (2026-11-01)
The story puts the 800-acre Bennett farm "two miles from the city" (Webster City), next to the county poor farm.
- **Poor farm found:** "**Hamilton County Home 80**" on the *Map of Cass Township*, p.18 (node/152720). It is a boxed 80-acre tract at the **S edge of sec 34, east of centre**, with a house mark at its N side. Cass Twp here = **T89N R25W**; Boone Twp (Webster City) is the notch at the SW of the plate.
- **BLM sec 34 T89N R25W:** -93.794 to -93.774, 42.475 to 42.492. That is about 2 miles NE of downtown Webster City, which fits the story.
- **Suggested pin (poor farm): about 42.478, -93.781, r300.** Confidence B: the label is on the plate; the position within the section is read by eye.
- **Neighbours in 1918:**
  - N: F. O. McKee (120 and 160, secs 34/27)
  - W: B. & W. Lyon 80 (sec 33), with Webster City-owned tracts of 155.60 and 240 beyond
  - E: W. E. Banagan[?] 40, then E. Bickford 160 and J. L. Kamrar 312 (sec 35)
  - S (Independence Twp, T88N R25W sec 3/4): H. R. Busing 80, H. D. Blue 80, Webster City 80
- **No Bennett on any adjoining parcel.** None appears on the Cass or Independence plates or in the patrons directory (p.49). By 1918 the Bennett land had passed to other owners. The 800 acres most likely lay among the McKee, Kamrar, Bickford and Lyon tracts around sec 34, but that is a guess without a deed or an older plat.
- Also checked, with no county farm: the Independence plate p.23 (whole), Freedom secs 13/23/24, and the NE of Webster Twp. Boone Twp p.22 (node/869759) is the Webster City limits only.
