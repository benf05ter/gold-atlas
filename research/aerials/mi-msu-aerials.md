# MSU RS&GIS historic aerial archive: Michigan 1930s-40s (2026-11-01)

Site: https://archive.rsgis.msu.edu/ (map application; 186,400 frames, 1935-2001). Viewed only; nothing downloaded or bought.

## Access
- **Browsing works without a login.** The map shows frame centre dots, and you can filter by county and by year (the `?county=...&to=1944` URL is kept). Clicking a dot gives the frame id, year, scale and film type, plus a **219x177 px thumbnail**.
- **Full-resolution images need a purchase ($30 each) or an MSU login.** Downloads are free for MSU users only, for MSU business. Both are off-limits under our rules, so **the site cannot refine a farmstead pin**.
- Non-MSU access "coming soon" per the site.
