Source: WHAIFinder (UW Robinson Map Library), **Crawford Co. BHP-1-41, 13 Oct 1938**, 1:20,000.
- Frame centre (simple centre-point georeference): 43.2877, -90.7038. Viewed only.
- Frame BHP-1-40 (centre 43.3030, -90.7038) covers the north side.

## Georeference
- I tied the photo to today's roads (OSM). The junction where S. Sleepy Hollow Rd (N-S valley road) meets Gobin Lane (the road running NE up the creek valley) is at **43.2847, -90.6973**.
- The ground scale is about 0.82 m per scan pixel, and the photo is close to north-up.
- Positions below are good to about **±100 m**.

## Ground at the combined pin (43.293, -90.697)
- The pin falls on a **wooded bluff ridge**, part of the dissected Kickapoo-side upland.
- There are open contour-strip fields on the ridge just E and SE of the pin (about 43.292,-90.696 and 43.293,-90.693).
- Valleys:
  - **N-S valley road (a lane)** running NW from the junction, up the hollow W of the ridge.
  - **NE-trending creek valley** (Gobin Lane) to the SE.
  - **E-W valley road** to the S (about the T9N/T10N town line area).

## Reading
- In the plat note (horrigan-crawford.md), the Horgen/Horrigan land straddles secs 35/36 T10N R3W and secs 1/2 T9N R3W.
- **Farmstead D (43.2943, -90.7001)** is the best candidate for a Horgen house on the Clayton side. Its position fits the c.1915 plat's sec 35 house dots (about 43.296, -90.702), within about 200 m. Confidence C+.
- **Farmstead C or A** may be the Jno. Horrigan sec 2 (Scott) place. They are unconfirmed and need a plat house-mark tie.
