## 6. GALLAGHER (tw-gallagher-morrison-creek)
- Jackson Co. papers not on newspapers.com; wire 1906-08-15 to 08-20 (~13 WI papers).
- Baraboo News-Republic 1906-08-15 p1, "Gold in Poorhouse: Workmen Find $1,500 in Jackson County Building" (Black River Falls, Aug 15): https://www.newspapers.com/image/867739824/
  - "To find $1,500 in gold in the JACKSON COUNTY POORHOUSE was the experience of workmen engaged in tearing down the structure." Salvage buyers F.B. DELL and SAM LUND vs. county claim. "The money is believed to have been the property of one JAMES GALLAGHER, an inmate of the poorhouse, who died SOME YEARS AGO. 'Dad Gallagher,' as the old man was familiarly called back in the 60's, was an eccentric fellow. He lived on UPPER MORRISON CREEK IN THE WOODS for many years, and was known to have accumulated considerable money."
- RESULT: confirms first name JAMES (matches the 1860 James Gallagher patent candidate, sec 27 T22N R4W), "upper Morrison Creek in the woods", died some years before 1906 as a poorhouse inmate. KEY: the $1,500 cache was FOUND (in the old county poorhouse, 1906) - so the hoard tied to him is RECOVERED, at the poorhouse not the woods home. Death date not given.
