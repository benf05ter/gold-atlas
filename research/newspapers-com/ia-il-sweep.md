Later he returned without the money."
      - "After that whenever he secured a few dollars that he did not need he would go into the timber on the Glen Farm and later return without the money. This practice he kept up for years. It is known that after Mike Hession and his mother had died and Dennis was in California, and Jack was living alone on the farm, he would sell produce and take the money home, and it was never seen again."
      - "Jack Hession is now in an asylum for the insane, and Dennis and Mike and Mrs. Hession have [died]..."
