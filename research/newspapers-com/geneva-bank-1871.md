# Geneva (Lake Geneva, Walworth Co. WI) bank burglary, Nov 1871: newspapers.com check (2026-10-31)

Asked for by the treasure thread via the coordinator. Source to check: History of Walworth County (1882) p.874, which says about $30,000 was taken, "home plunder never recovered", and the robbers confessed in prison.
- leads.tsv / negatives.tsv: no prior Geneva row.

## Main source
**Janesville Weekly Gazette, 1871-11-20 p1**, "Additional Particulars of the Geneva Bank Robbery" (Elkhorn Independent correspondence, Geneva, Wis., Nov. 14, 1871): https://www.newspapers.com/image/73885238/
- **Date:** "On Wednesday night" [8-9 Nov 1871]. The paper says the robbery "doubtless took place in the morning (Thursday)", i.e. **Thu 9 Nov 1871**, early hours.
- **Method:** the burglars got in through an upper window facing Main St. They bored and blew the right door of the large Herring safe with a half-inch drill and powder, then blew the inner drawer.
- **Amount:** "its contents stolen, consisting of over **$20,000 in greenbacks and about $10,000 in bonds**, the private property included." Total about **$30,000**, matching the 1882 history. The books were left.
- **Losses:** Rev. E. G. Miner lost $3,500; Charles Vipham $350. Bonds left in the safe "for present keeping" were lost.
- **Second job:** minutes later they blew the safe at the Gilbert & Barber grist mill and got $8.
- **Bank officer:** the bank's president was **S. D. Richardson**. Business resumed using C. E. Buhre's bookstore safe.
- **Prep:** the gang spent the previous day around town. They had a drill made at a blacksmith's, and bought a tin tube at T. C. Smith's hardware ("for oiling a patent machine") and 25 lbs of flour at the mill.
- **Pursuit:** parties went out on every road; those going toward **HEBRON** (Ill., S of the state line) believed they were on the track. **No arrests as of Tue 14 Nov.**

## Reading
- A real robbery: about $20k cash plus $10k bonds, 9 Nov 1871.
- The only direction given is the flight **south toward Hebron, Ill.**
- No paper names a hiding place. The bonds may have been non-negotiable or registered.
- **Grade: D** (no cache location) unless the confession text gives a spot.
