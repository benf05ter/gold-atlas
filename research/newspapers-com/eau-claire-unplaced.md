## 1. Eau Claire River bathing beach (1900-1940)

**ANSWER: the Second Ward bathing beach, on the south bank of the Eau Claire River just WEST of SUMMER STREET (east side of Eau Claire), on ground the Northwestern Lumber Co. gave the city, with warming houses.**

- **Leader-Telegram 1932-04-24 p2**, "Park Area Consists of Wooded Land Along River / Property Adjoins Second Ward Bathing Beach": https://www.newspapers.com/image/267180168/
  - The Northern States Power Co. offered the city "a strip of wooded land lying along the SOUTH BANK OF THE EAU CLAIRE RIVER FROM SUMMER STREET EAST to a point about fifty feet east of the line of MARGARET STREET extended". The power company reserved flowage rights. The strip is about 1,600 ft long, 150 ft wide at the west end tapering to 50 ft, and over 5 acres.
  - Location sentence: "The west end of the area ... abuts on the city owned area WEST OF SUMMER STREET, which includes the ground on which the SECOND WARD BATHING BEACH and warming houses are located. This was donated to the city a few years ago by the NORTHWESTERN LUMBER COMPANY."
  - (This strip is the later riverside park east of Summer St.)
- **Leader-Telegram 1916-07-28 p8**, "A Bathing Beach at Electric Park": https://www.newspapers.com/image/239455688/
  - "A. R. Manley, district manager for the Wisconsin-Minnesota Light and Power Co. is continually making efforts for the improvement of ELECTRIC PARK ... It was announced yesterday that arrangements were made for the opening of a swimming beach for next [season]."
  - The location of Electric Park is not given in this item. It was the power company's resort park; it may be on the Dells Pond of the Eau Claire River, but that is **unconfirmed**.
  - The same page has the end of an item where "the mayor and councilmen took a trip up the river yesterday afternoon to look the situation over and see if a more suitable point could not be located" (a city swimming place, 1916). Not read in full.
- Noise / not the river:
  - Leader-Telegram 1913-07-04 p5 and 1930-07-12 p2: the HALF MOON LAKE bathing beach (city lake, not the river).
  - 1921-06-22 p4: YMCA pool.

  - "bathing beach" Dells dam: 40 hits, mostly Half Moon.
  - "bathing beach" Altoona river: 182 hits.

## 2. Eau Claire Lumber Co. North Fork camps (1870-1900), first pass

**PARTIAL: no named Eau Claire Lumber Co. camp found yet. Found one dam landmark and one named (non-ECL) camp.**

- **Daily Free Press (Eau Claire) 1878-04-15 p4**, local column: https://www.newspapers.com/image/6619797/
  - "The Company's FLOODING DAM ON THE NORTH FORK OF THE EAU CLAIRE RIVER was damaged last Saturday."
  - In the Eau Claire papers of the 1870s, "the Company" normally means the Eau Claire Lumber Co. This is my reading; the item doesn't name the company. No location beyond "North Fork".
- **Leader (Eau Claire) 1884-12-06 p3**, local column: https://www.newspapers.com/image/239473352/
  - "Jos. STERLING, of THORP, has organized a logging camp on the NORTH FORK OF THE EAU CLAIRE with DAVE LEONARD. Four million feet is the amount of logs calculated to be put in."
  - This is a named camp (Sterling and Leonard, near Thorp), but no company is named and no section is given.
- Noise:
  - Eau Claire News 1879-12-06 p3: "North Side", plus the North Fork of the FLAMBEAU (Calhoun & Bradley camp). The same page has the 1879 mill cut table, with the Eau Claire Lumber Co. at 54.6 million ft.
  - Weekly Leader 1891-12-06 p6 and Eau Claire News 1887-04-30 p4: scattered-word matches.
- Searches run:
  - "North Fork" camp "Eau Claire Lumber" WI 1870-1900: 56 hits (22 Eau Claire Co.).
