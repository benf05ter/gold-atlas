## B11. Dolph / Bellevue, Eaton Co. MI (1913-1915)

  - **Place:** the strangers stayed at the Dyer hotel in Bellevue, and "always went back of the GROHE SWAMP, which is near the river, and on the CHARLES DOLPH FARM, where they had dug up several stumps." Reported to Deputy Sheriff Will Youngs. Sheriff Storrs has a description.
  - **The treasure ("the detective" story):** "about $15,000 in gold which had been stolen before the civil war from either the county treasurer's office in Charlotte or Marshall". Four men robbed the treasurer's office and buried it "on the banks of a river. A map was drawn which one of the men took. The other three were caught and sent to prison", served, went through the Civil War, and later searched but never found it. The fourth went west and gave the map on his deathbed to a chum, who passed it to "the detective".
  - The detective reportedly asked the county officials for permission to dig and to pay the county a percentage; he was told to return when the board of supervisors met, and never came back. "The country at that time was practically a wilderness ... where the loot was buried, people now lived and that a village was located near."
  - "Another Version of Affair" follows (not read).
