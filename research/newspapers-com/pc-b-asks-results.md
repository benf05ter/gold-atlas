# PC (b) newspaper asks, batch 2 (asks 23-26) (2026-11-01)

These checks look for a later recovery of each B-lead. Reads were view-only on newspapers.com.

Search URL format: `/search/results/?region=us-mn&date-start=YYYY&date-end=YYYY&keyword=...`. Background is in deep-dives-pc-job-1.md (Murphy section A, Kuehn section B).

## 23. Murphy, Warsaw Twp, Rice Co. MN (1901-1915)
- **Holdings:** for Rice Co., newspapers.com has only the Faribault Daily News, Lonsdale Area News-Review and Northfield News, and **all three cover 2026 only**. The 1901-1915 Faribault and Northfield papers are **not indexed**, so the local check was skipped.
- **Statewide MN searches, 1901-1915:**
  - "Murphy money Warsaw" gave 1,033 hits, all scattered noise (Duluth, Austin, etc.).
  - Murphy "town of Warsaw" gave 26 hits. The May 1905 cluster (Kiester, Dodge Center) is a syndicated "Milk Saloons of Warsaw" filler about Warsaw, Poland. Others are Irish Standard society items and noise.
- **Result:** no find, search or dispute report for 1901-1915. Nothing changes the 1920 $6,000 find as the only recovery.

## 24. Kuehn, Sigel Twp, Brown Co. MN (1906-1915)
- **Holdings:** New Ulm Review (1878-1922), New Ulm Post (1914-22) and Der Fortschritt (1891-97). **The Sleepy Eye Herald is not on newspapers.com.**
- **Searches:**
  - "Kuehn money Sigel" gave 35 hits. The April 1906 hits are the known plum-tree jar wire story.
  - New Ulm Review 1912-01-24 p9 is a tax list ("Kuehn, John"). The 1915 hits are the same kind of lists.
  - "Kuehn estate" gave 1 hit, the known 1907-12-25 p6 Klossner land-sale item.
  - "Kuehn buried" gave 95 hits.
- **New item:** New Ulm Review **1906-05-16 p9**, "Wills Do Not Satisfy Heirs ... Sperl Will to be Retried and Kuehn Will Dissolved". https://www.newspapers.com/image/81040063/
  - The May district-court term lists "the Kuehn will that the son is attempting to dissolve claiming that he was not treated fairly for the reason that his father was not in his right mind at the time of his death".
  - The calendar entry reads: "In the matter of the estate of Fred. Kuehn, deceased, Fred. Kuehn, appellant and contestant, vs. Otto Schell, proponent and respondent. Jury."
  - This fills the "no will contest" gap. The will was produced (whether the buried papers were found is not stated) and the son contested it in May 1906.
- **Result:** no further money find reported 1906-1915. The contest's outcome was not checked in this pass.

## 25. Keeler (Jackson Co.) and Agard (Van Buren Co.) MI
**Keeler** (MI, 1892-1902):
- "Noah Keeler" gave 18 hits; "Keeler farm" gave 8; "Keeler hidden Liberty Jackson" gave 50; "Keeler money Liberty" gave 1,676 (noise).
- **No money-found report.**
- New context:
  - Jackson Citizen Patriot **1893-06-12 p3**: a farmers' club is to meet "with Mr. and Mrs. Eli Shafer on the Noah Keeler farm", so the Shafers occupied the farm by mid-1893. https://www.newspapers.com/image/1162641343/
  - JCP **1894-08-10 p7**, Probate Court: "estate of Noah Keeler, deceased, Lorenzo M. Rhodes, administrator, filed his final account and asked to be discharged. Hearing September 1." https://www.newspapers.com/image/1162466364/
  - JCP 1897-09-18 p6 is the obituary of Mrs. Sarah Keeler, a Jackson pioneer. Her relation to Noah is unconfirmed.

**Agard** (MI, 1930-1946; the 1835 event predates papers):
- No find report.
- **Placement detail:** Grand Rapids Press **1938-03-07 p4**, "Group of Rocks in Paw Paw River All That's Left of Old Indian Trading Post of '30's" (photo). https://www.newspapers.com/image/1112771189/
  - The landing stones stand "on the bank of the Paw Paw river a quarter of a mile below Maple lake". That is just downstream of Maple Lake at Paw Paw village.
  - Agard built the post (8-10 log cabins) and a canoe landing "of stones and split logs" in the stream.
  - "There is a legend that Agard kept a cache of gold hidden nearby, but it was never found"; he died fall 1835 and "was buried at the post".
  - The same feature ran in the Ann Arbor News 1938-03-08 p10 and the Bay City Times 1938-03-10 p12.

## 26. Bergblom, Mulchrone, Hoffman Orchard, Roland Glencoe
- **Bergblom** (MN, 1930-40): "Bergblom" gave 39 hits; "Bergblom gold" gave 12.

  - No later find.
- **Mulchrone** (WI, 1900-1910): 9 hits.
  - Feb 1900 reprints of the foundation tear-down search (Milwaukee Journal Sentinel 1900-02-24 p10; Epitaph-News 1900-02-23 p8; Eau Claire Leader 1900-02-25 p6).
  - **No report of the outcome.** Later hits are personals.
- **Hoffman, Nordman Hill** (WI, 1908-1918): 26 hits, all Jan-Feb 1908 state-news reprints of the original find (Baraboo News Republic 1908-01-30 p1 is the earliest).
  - 1909-18 "Hoffman Nordman" gave 331 hits, all noise.
  - No later find.
- **Roland, Glencoe (Buffalo Co.)** (WI, 1893-1903): **FIND REPORT → F.** Milwaukee Journal Sentinel **1893-11-21 p10**, "Murdered for Money: Patrick Roland Was the Victim of Assassins". https://www.newspapers.com/image/1297950383/
  - ARCADIA, Nov 20: the inquest jury returned a murder verdict, "and a search of his premises resulted in the finding of $537 in gold and a 'blind' bridle stowed away in a wagon load of rubbish in the garret of his residence."
  - Subhead: "The Murderers Overlooked $400 in Gold, Which Was Hidden in a Blind Bridle".
  - The murderers were thought to have taken about $1,000 in greenbacks carried in a belt. Mice destroyed about $400 in his hut a few years earlier.
  - He was buried "in the Glencoe Lutheran cemetery".
  - So the hidden gold was recovered by the searchers in Nov 1893.
