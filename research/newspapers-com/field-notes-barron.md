Page images are in `field-notes/` next to this file. Distances are in chains (1 chain = 66 ft = 100 links) measured from the start of the line.

## T27N R9W (downtown Eau Claire, the Chippewa / Eau Claire river confluence)
Sources: plat UWDC AINLXUYOXQNROO8Q (township lines surveyed by George O. Harrick, Sept–Oct 1848; subdivisions by Alexander Anderson, July–Aug 1849; approved Dubuque, 11 Dec 1849; image `field-notes/plat_T27N-R9W.jpg`). Interior notes ALQYM26QNL6UWQ8E (A. Anderson, 1849).
- **The plat** shows a mill and buildings ("Saw Mill" with houses) on the north bank of the Eau Claire River at the mill pond in secs 16/21, just above the confluence. A **"Road to Pinery"** runs east from the mill along the south side of the Eau Claire River through secs 22–24. Another road runs north from the mill through secs 9/4 toward Chippewa Falls ("Road to Chippewa Falls"), and a road heads southwest toward "Prairie/Menomonie" at the west edge. The mill is in sec 16, the same place as the 1849 note below.
- **West corrected between sections 16 & 21** (variation 7°55′ E): 0.70 "Bayou or Pond"; 19.10 "L'Eau Claire river at Mill Pond"; **68.36 "Intersect the centre of Stones Swimmer's & Co's mill"** (as written; probably the Stone/Swim mill partnership); **79.91 "Section corner on bank of river, a fine mill site where the mill stands."**
- **North between sections 20 & 21** (variation 9° E): 29.00 leave bottom E & W, enter prairie; 40.00 ¼ post (B. Oak 10 N 77½ E 33; Spruce Pine 12 S 58 W 27); 60.00 enter bottom SW; **66.00 "Road West"**; 74.78 left bank of L'Eau Claire R., meander post; 80.00 over river, corner to secs 16, 17, 20, 21.
- **North between sections 21 & 22**: **40.00 "Set qr section post in a Mound, pit East 8 links"** (the post was set in a mound; possibly just the survey mound, but worth checking against effigy-mound records); 50.00 enter bottom west; **67.00 "road N W & enter timber NW"**; 77.10 stream 200 links wide, west (the Eau Claire River); 80.00 corner to secs 15, 16, 21, 22. Level 2nd-rate prairie.
- West corrected between 22 & 27: 36.20 stream 30 links wide NW; 45.00 enter prairie.
- North between 16 & 17: 64.84 left bank of the Chippewa (W. Walnut 24 in. bearing tree); West corrected between 9 & 16: **33.52 "Road crosses"**.
- **For the app:** an 1849 sawmill and mill dam on the Eau Claire River at the sec 16/21 line, about 68 chains (≈4,500 ft) west of the 16/17/20/21 corner, which lands near today's downtown, Barstow St and the Eau Claire River. Also 1849 roads: to the Pinery (east), to Chippewa Falls (north) and west. Old sawmill sites are prime spots for lost tools, coins and early artifacts.

## T28N R9W (Chippewa River between Eau Claire and Chippewa Falls; Lake Hallie / Wissota area), 1849
Sources: plat AIBCUPKR66J4H48E (image `field-notes/plat_T28N-R9W.jpg`); interior notes AYZFEZIOHWUB7P8C (George R. Stuntz, August 1849; chainmen John Chisholm and Saml H. Allison, axeman Wm B. Cantwell). Page images T28N-R9W_*.jpg.
- **Plat features:**
  - **"J. C. Thomas' Saw Mill"** with buildings on the left (east) bank of the Chippewa at the sec 27/22 line, just above a **"Mill Pond"** on the creek entering from the east.
  - **"Field"** and **"Cabin"** on the north bank in sec 28 (west of the mill).
  - **"Trail from Menomonie Mills to Falls of Chippewa"**, running diagonally NE from sec 20 through secs 21/16/15.
  - **"Road from Falls of Chippewa to L'Eau Claire Mills"**, running SW from sec 13 through 23 and 27.
  - **"Spring"** labels in secs 29 and 31/32, and "Rapids" in sec 32.
- **West corrected between sections 27 & 34**: **41.00 "Road from the Falls of Chippewa River to the L'Eau Claire Mills."**
- North between 27 & 28: 57.00 enter bottom; 63.00 left bank of Chippewa, meander post (Butternut 8 S44½W 16; Lind. 14 S35E 13); 71.67 over river, meander post right bank; 80.00 corner 21/22/27/28.
- North between 28 & 29: 60.50 enter bottom; 67.10 Chippewa, meander post left bank; 76.00 meander post right bank; 80.00 corner 20/21/28/29.
- West corrected 21 & 28: 11.50 right bank of Chippewa, meander post; 40.30 ¼ post in river; 60.60 right bank again.
- North between 21 & 22: 20.50 enter swamp; 56.50 leave white cedar swamp, ascend bluff; 70.00 enter prairie; **80.00 "Set post in mound, pit 8 links S."**
- West corrected 22 & 27: 15.00 left bank of Chippewa (river 8 chains wide); 23.00 over river.
- **General Description:** the land east of the Chippewa is very sandy; "Chippewa River is navigable for Keel Boats at all seasons of the year but the rapids in Sections 15 & 22 and in Section 32 render the navigation difficult." It names no settlers; the mill, cabin and field appear only on the plat.
- **For the app:** the J. C. Thomas sawmill and mill pond (sec 27, by the 22/27 line on the east bank), the cabin and field in sec 28, and two 1849 travel routes, the Menomonie Mills–Chippewa Falls trail and the Chippewa Falls–Eau Claire Mills road. The springs in secs 29/31 make good water-stop pins.

The northern part is rough & broken, timber… Oaks."

  - "Cabins" at the SW edge of sec 35 by the Chippewa.

  - "L'Eau Galle Mills" and a "Mill Pond" just west of the township line (in T26N R14W, next township).
  - A "Sinks" label in sec 21 and a "Tamarac Swamp" in sec 15.
- **West corrected between 14 & 23** (variation 8°34′ E): **2.50 "Enter A. Coleburn's field NW"**; **8.85 "Leave Coleburn's field S20W, contains about 40 acres"**; 24.50 enter wet bottom; 39.72 ¼ post (Tamarack 12 S75W 30 lks; Tamarack S65E 57); 47.00 leave bottom.
- **North between 23 & 24** (variation 8°43′ E): 9.16 W. Pine 24 in.; 21.00 enter prairie; **24.70 "Road runs NW"**; 40.00 ¼ post (Br-Oak 10 N87W 1.25; Br-Oak 9 S84½E 4.13); 49.50 enter timber; **69.00 "Enter field, runs S20W"**; **79.00 "Leave same, N70W"**; 80.00 corner 13/14/23/24 (Br-Oak 12 S80E 48 lks; Br-Oak 14 N11½W 91).
- **North between 22 & 23**: 25.00 enter prairie; **29.00 "Road bears NW"**; 62.00 enter timber.
- **For the app:** A. Colburn (spelled Coleburn in the notes), 1849 settler on the Red Cedar (present Dunnville area). His ~40-acre field straddles the 14/23 line in the NE of sec 23, and his house is on the Red Cedar bank. Also the early SE–NW road, and cabins on the Chippewa in sec 35. I haven't yet read the sec 35 lines for the cabins.

## T30N R7W (Jim Falls / Chippewa River above Chippewa Falls), October 1849
Sources: interior notes AYL5UFMWM5W2K68Y (George R. Stuntz, finished 20 Oct 1849; chainmen John Chisholm and William H. Burks, axeman Saml H. Allison). The plat link ATMGWTLNOQXTPQ8E turned out to be a 1971 BLM Willow Island resurvey; the 1850 original plat is presumably A4BYQPNNT2ZSDP9A (not yet read).
- **General Description:** "There are six dwelling Houses in this Township:
  - one on the N.W.¼ of the N.W.¼ of Section 2;
  - one on the S.W.¼ of N.W.¼ of Section 2 belonging to **Daniel McCann**;
  - one in Section 20 belonging to **Donaldson & Co** on the East side of the river;
  - one on the West side of the River owned by **Gauthier**;
  - one on the West side of the River in Section 30;
  - and a farm in Sections 30 and 31 on the West side of the River belonging to **James Armitage**, who lives on the N.E.¼ of the N.W.¼ of Section No. 30."
- "The rapids in this Township in Section 16 fall about 10 feet in one fourth of a mile and offer every facility for mill builders. Also the rapids in Section 30, called **Armitage's falls**, afford water power to any amount, the river falling about 50 feet in ¾ of a mile."
- **North between sections 20 & 21** (variation 10° E): 18.50 stream 12 links wide; 40.00 ¼ post (Birch 12 N42W 11 lks; Ash 8 S42E 35 lks); 46.50 stream 8 links runs W; **47.00 "Enter Sugar camp S.E."**; 69.00 stream 8 links runs W; **"Leave Sugar camp bears E."**; 80.00 corner 16/17/20/21 (Sugar 7 S61E 31 lks; Birch 10 N12E 33 lks). Timber: sugar, linden, elm, birch, white pine.
- West corrected 16 & 21: 28.00–41.20 marsh; 40.14 ¼ post (White Oak 8 N59E 58; White Pine 8 S85W 125).
- North between 21 & 22, and 22/27: streams and swamp only, nothing cultural.
- **For the app:** six named 1849 homesteads around Jim Falls: Daniel McCann (SW¼ NW¼ sec 2), an unnamed house (NW¼ NW¼ sec 2), Donaldson & Co (sec 20, east side), Gauthier (west side), and James Armitage's farm and house (NE¼ NW¼ sec 30, farm across secs 30/31). There's also a maple sugar camp on the 20/21 line from about 47 to 69 chains north of the 20/21/28/29 corner. "Armitage's falls" is the old name for the sec 30 rapids, i.e. Jim Falls.

## T28N R13W (Menomonie mills on the Red Cedar), October 1849
Sources: plat AD5MXYQG2P2ITA9C (image `field-notes/plat_T28N-R13W.jpg`); interior notes AX2GHH2YILALCU8M (Jesse S. Jarrett, finished 24 Oct 1849).
- **Plat features:**
  - **"Black & Wilson's Mill"** with buildings on the Red Cedar at the sec 26/23/27/22 corner area (the bend by the mouth of Wilson Creek). This is the original Menomonie mill.
  - A second **"Mill"** labelled **"Gilbert"** on a creek mill pond at the west edge of sec 34 / east edge of sec 33 (near the 27/28/33/34 corner).
  - A **"Road to Red River"** (Hay River?) running NW from the mill through sec 23.
  - Roads NE (toward Chippewa Falls) and SW along the river.
- **General Description (partial):** "This Township is level on East side of river, on the West side high rolling; soil ranging from 1st to 2nd rate sandy. There has been considerable good Pine timber in this Township but it has all been cut out or destroyed by fire. Hard wood in the N.W. part is pretty good. Undergrowth very thick on west side of river. No land that will sell soon except what the mill…" (continues on the next page, not yet fetched).
- **North between sections 26 & 27** (variation 9°45′ E): 4.50 enter river bottom; 40.00 ¼ post (Bur Oak 11 N18W 496 lks; Blk Oak 14 S48E 149); 58.00 Red Cedar left bank, runs SW, meander post (W Oak 13 S71E 72; Birch 12 S54½W 42); 62.12 right bank, meander post (Lind 26 N88½W 28; Walnut 10 N21E 20); **62.45 "Enter pasture field"**; 68.40 leave river bottom NE; **75.00 "Leave field bears N.E."**; **76.00 "Road bears N.E."**; 80.00 corner 22/23/26/27.
- **North between 27 & 28** (variation 9° E): **0.22 "Leave Mill Pond bears N.W."** (this is Gilbert's mill pond at the 27/28/33/34 corner); 18.00–39.00 tamarack swamp; 80.00 corner 21/22/27/28.
- North between 25 & 26: 75.00 enter river bottom SW; 80.00 corner 23/24/25/26.
- **For the app:** Black & Wilson's 1849 mill (the origin of Menomonie) with its pasture field across the 26/27 line about 62–75 chains north of the 26/27/34/35 corner, plus a road heading NE. Gilbert's mill and mill pond sit at the 27/28/33/34 corner. Also a 1849 road toward the Hay River.

  - **15.50 "trail N.W."**
  - **31.00 "Ledge of rock 25 feet perpendicular, S.E."**
  - **32.00 "Creek 25 links wide, East"**, with the footnote: "Herd's mill on this creek is a small affair, one saw, and very little timber around."
  - **34.29 "a house N 70½ W 14.00 [chains]"** and **"a mill S 89½ W 16.00 [chains]"**
  - 40.00 ¼ post (W Pine 9 N86W 27; Sugar 12 N58E 81)
  - 54.00 leave bottom NE; **57.00 "trail NE"**; 80.00 corner 3/4/9/10
- West corrected between 10 & 15: 27.50 left bank of the Red Cedar, meander post; 33.31 right bank, meander post; **35.00 "trail N.W."**.

The house lies 14.00 chains away at N 70½ W and the mill 16.00 chains at S 89½ W, which puts both in sec 9 on the creek near its mouth. There's also a rock ledge 25 ft high at 31 chains, and an old trail along the east bank of the Red Cedar crossing the 9/10, 10/11, 10/15 and 23/26 lines.
