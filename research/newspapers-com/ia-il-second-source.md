## 1. James Webster, Eldora (Hardin Co. IA), 1893-97
- **(a) Local second account: YES.** **Steamboat Rock Echo (Hardin Co.), 1897-05-20 p3**, "Buried his Money": https://www.newspapers.com/image/14772299/
  - "It is currently reported that some years ago James Webster, of CLAY TOWNSHIP, received something like a $1,000 pension money. Being a man of an eccentric nature, rather than trust the banks, he buried it, and persistently refused to tell his friends or even the family its secret hiding place. To this he has added accumulations until the sum total is several hundred more... But now comes the rub... he has forgotten the place where he hid the treasure. - **(c) Later find:** none seen in 1897-99 Hardin hits.
- Grade: C- (a lost cache the owner himself forgot). The amount is likely small.
