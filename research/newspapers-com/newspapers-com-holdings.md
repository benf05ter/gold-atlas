Practical upshot: most of the open leads' home papers (Rice Lake, Burnett, Jackson, Pierce, Sherburne, Iron River, Sawyer, and the 1880s Ashland and Bayfield papers apart from the Ashland Weekly News) are **not** on newspapers.com. Those need microfilm (WHS / ARCs), the MN Hub, or Chronicling America.
