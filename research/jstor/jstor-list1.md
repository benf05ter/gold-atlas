   - **LEAD:** "Mrs. Talitha Catherine Dartt, Illinois' Only Real Daughter of the American Revolution", *Journal of the Illinois State Historical Society* 21:4 (Jan 1929), pp.573-578, FREE, https://www.jstor.org/stable/40187592. **p.576:** her brother **Joel [Harrell Jr.] "was somewhat of a miser.
