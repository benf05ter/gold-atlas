# Amil Markee, Apple River Twp, Polk Co. WI (d. 24 Aug 1936; buried money found by boys, Dec 1936): census and death (Ancestry, 2026-11-01)

Viewed only. (Polk Co. probate is not in Ancestry's WI collection.)

- **Death:** *Wisconsin Death Records* and Find a Grave give **Amil Markee, b. 8 May 1870, Polk Co. WI; d. 24 Aug 1936, Amery, Polk Co.** The ELCA Swedish church records give the same death date at Amery.
- **1930 census, Apple River Twp, Polk Co.:** ED 48-3, sheet 13-A, enumerated 8 Apr 1930 (image 4547573_00603; record 58031030).
  - **Dwelling 16 / family 17: Amil Markee, head, 59, single, owns home, lives on a farm, farmer on own account.** Born WI; father b. Sweden, mother b. Michigan. He lived alone, which fits the "bachelor" of the 1936 story.
  - **Neighbours, in order on the sheet:**
    - Mrs. Chas. Davidson (10/11, farm owner, retired)
    - T. Lokhamming [?] (11/12, carpenter)
    - George Paulson (12/13, dairy farmer), with father Ole Paulson
    - Bror E. Nelson (13/14, dairy and poultry farmer)
    - Fred Brusberg [?] (14/15, farm labourer)
    - Oscar Olin [?] (15/16, dairy farmer)
    - **Amil Markee (16/17)**
    - Victor E. Carlson (17/18, farmer)
    - James [?] (18/19, dairy farmer, b. Scotland)
    - Carl Hartsey [?] (19/20, dairy farmer)
    - Alfred Gunnarson (20/21, dairy farmer)
    - John Kraus (21/22, dairy farmer)
    - Lyle [?] Weikert (22/23, dairy farmer)
    - Wayne Smith (23/24, feed-mill labourer)
- **Brother Oscar Markee** (who received the found money in 1936) is also in **Apple River Twp** in 1930, with wife Ellen and children Edith, Lawrence, Clarence, Leroy, Oliver and Violet.
- **Use:** match these neighbours on a 1930s Polk plat (Apple River Twp) to place Amil's farm.
