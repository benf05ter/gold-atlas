## Use
- In 1850 Paddock lived beside the Murdocks, which supports the sec 11 placement.
- Matching these names on the **1860 Geil map** places the neighbourhood: sec 11 in 1860 shows the Wells family, Harding, Towland, Logan, Lamb, Hannover, Pennell and Baker. Of the 1850 names, only "M. Murdock" appears on that map, in the S of the township. So by 1860 most of these families had moved.
- 1860 and 1870 census pages were not yet checked.
