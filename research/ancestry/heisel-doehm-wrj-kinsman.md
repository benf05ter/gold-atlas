## Heisel (Pound, Marinette Co. WI; Mrs. John Heisel, Mar 1906)
- Marinette Co. is **not** in Ancestry's WI probate collection.
- **1900 census, Coleman, Marinette Co.** (Coleman village and town adjoin Pound). Three Heisel households, all German-born:
  - **Ludwig Heisel** (b. Apr 1848 Germany), wife Augusta (b. 1856), children Amelia, Herman, Rudolph, Arthur, **John (b. Mar 1891)** and Edward
  - **Gustave Heisel** (b. 1873), wife Minnie
  - **Fredric[k] Heisel** (b. 1853, Poland/Russia), wife Carolina
- **No John Heisel as head of household in 1900.** "Mrs. John Heisel" of 1906 could be the widow of a John not enumerated, or one of these families. Sheet and neighbours not opened.
