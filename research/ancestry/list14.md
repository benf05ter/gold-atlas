- 15. **Cadillac is not a GB city** (the URL fell back to all of Michigan). MI statewide "Larson gold Jennings", 1922-24, gives 38 hits, clustered in **June 1923** (Flint Journal 17 Jun, Coldwater Daily Reporter 9 Jun, Ann Arbor News 8 Jun) = an AP state story. Read: **Ann Arbor Times News, Fri 8 Jun 1923, p.15, "Hunt Treasure on Site of Old Town / Hermit's Gold is Sought as Last Vestige of Jennings Goes":**
  - Jennings (about 1,200 people; the Mitchell Bros saw mill and chemical plants) was being moved by motor truck and trailer to **Cadillac** as its timber ran out.
  - "A weird treasure hunt is being conducted in the vicinity of Jennings. The hunt is on for a treasure which is variously estimated at from **$10,000 to $75,000** supposed to have been buried somewhere in the vicinity by **John Larson**, formerly a woodcutter and for many years prior to his death **a hermit living near one of the outlying lakes**."
  - Larson's wife died years before; after his son **John Larson Jr.** disappeared, he became a hermit and died "a few years ago". The son reportedly saw a newsreel of Jennings being moved, came back, found the home deserted, and searched quietly for several days (rumours conflict on success).
  - "Rumor says that the money was in gold" brought from **Sweden** about 30+ years earlier and turned into American gold, "of which no trace has been found". Old residents search on Saturday afternoons and Sundays.
  - The **church** (70 ft high, the village's last building) had just been moved on a giant trailer. "Larson was at one time one of the prominent church workers of the village and there is a possibility that his gold might have been buried beneath it."
  - Fit with Ancestry: **Jens Larson, d. 9 Jan 1916, Lake Twp** (death index) = "John Larson", a few years before 1923.
  - Places: (a) the hermit's home "near one of the outlying lakes" around Jennings (Crooked Lake / Lake Twp lakes; not named), and (b) the **old Jennings church site** (the church was moved to Cadillac in 1923). Grade C pending the house site.
