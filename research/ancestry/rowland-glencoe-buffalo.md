# Patrick Rowland (Roland), Glencoe, Buffalo Co. WI (murdered Nov 1893): probate (Ancestry, 2026-11-01)

**Collection:** *Wisconsin, U.S., Wills and Probate Records, 1800-1987* (coll. 9088), Buffalo Co., "Probate Files, No 882-922".
- **Case no. 891**; Ancestry record 2243080; 123 images, starting at image 007704347_00256.
- Index probate date 19 Jun 1894. Viewed only.

## Petition for administration (image 00263)
- Petition of **D. J. Nally of Glencoe**, Buffalo Co., a neighbour.
- Patrick Rowland "departed this life at **Glencoe** in said county on the **18th day of November 1893**, without leaving any last will".
- Personal property not exceeding **$600**; real estate about **$1,000**.
- Surviving: **Mary Rowland, his niece, residing at Stratford, Canada**, and probably other relatives unknown. Dated 21 Nov 1893.

## Inventory and appraisal (images 00259-00262)
- By D. J. Nally, administrator, with appraisers Robert Ashton and John Bichsel, up to 19 Dec 1893. Filed and allowed **17 June 1894** (R. Lees, county judge).

## Significance
- **Placement:** his land lies in **secs 2, 11 and 12, T21N R10W** (Glencoe Twp). His house was most likely on the 160 acres in secs 11/12.
