## 10. Conrad May, Jamestown (Grant Co WI): DONE, confirmed for 1895
- **Ancestry 1127, Grant 1895, viewer p.60 of 74 (image 31617_072128-00173), "Plat of Jamestown", T1N R2W.**
- **"Conrad May 521"** fills **sec 12** S of **Louisburg P.O.** (the village is on the sec 12 N line; Jos. Kemp, P. Bench and Edward Barnes are around it). The tract runs S into the next tier.
- So May held the sec 12 land in 1895, two years before the 1897 story. The c.1915 Hixson house mark (plats/may-dickeyville-grant.md, pin 42.5745,-90.5570) was on May's own farm. Pin unchanged; it now rests on an 1895 owner.
- **1900 census (7602):** Conrad May with wife Louisa, Jamestown, Grant Co (the index shows the household; the image was not opened).
