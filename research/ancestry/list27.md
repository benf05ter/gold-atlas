## 4. Kelly's Bluff reservoir, Dubuque (Out Lot 732)
- **Trade journals (HathiTrust):**
  - *Municipal Journal* v.37 (1914), p.160: "Dubuque, Ia. Bids for construction of large water reservoir on **Kelly's bluff, south of West Third St.**, will be opened by city water works department on July 29th ... Reservoir will about double city's water pressure."
  - *Municipal Journal* p.300 and *Engineering Record* v.70 (1914), p.105: contract for the reservoir on Kelly's Bluff **awarded to Midwest Constr. Co., Kansas City, at $66,000** (bids opened 30 July 1914); engineer **Dabney H. Maury**, Chicago.
- So the reservoir was **built from Aug 1914 into 1915**, on Out Lot 732, **S of W. Third St** on the bluff. That fits the round structure noted in list23 #14 at **about 42.4944, -90.6726** (S of W. 3rd, W of St Raphael's Cathedral).
- **Out Lot 732 itself is not found** in any OCR text (HathiTrust: only an unrelated Ohio hit). The Dubuque city GIS server did not respond (connection reset).
- **Pin: 42.4944, -90.6726, r100, B** (reservoir site on the bluff S of W. 3rd St, 1914-15; the $500 workmen's find). Confirm the round reservoir on a modern aerial; Ground check.
