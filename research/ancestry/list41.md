### New / useful
1. **FORT SNELLING, Angell's buried gold, FOUND.** MN Hist. Coll. (Mrs. Adams' Reminiscences, p.~100-101): a man named **Angell**, from Red River, buried "a considerable quantity of gold" at the fort and died before he could tell where. It "slept in the ground for over fifty years" and was "discovered not long ago by some laborers digging for foundations of the new buildings for the post". **A FOUND item.** Fort Snelling about 44.893,-93.181; D (colour/found). Grep for an existing pin first.
2. **BOGUS CREEK mouth (Pepin Co WI), fort site gives a fix.** WI Hist. Coll. v.10 p.~360s and Wis. Mag. Hist. 10 (1927) p.286:
   - "the old French fort" (Fort St. Antoine?) is on the Lake Pepin shore "**in Section 20, T. 2[3] N. of Range 15 W.**" (OCR reads "33"; Pepin is T23N R15W), "about 130 or 140 rods above the mouth of **Bogus creek**".
   - This anchors **Bogus Creek's mouth** (list39 #5 counterfeiters' bars) just below sec 20, E shore of Lake Pepin, about 6 mi above the outlet.
   - **Pin aid: Bogus Creek mouth, Pepin Twp, about 44.47,-92.17 (needs a BLM/OSM check), D.**
3. **McGREGOR IA, Spanish coin 1787.** Iowa J. H&P v.13 (1915) p.~143: "the recent finding of a Spanish coin, bearing the date 1787, near McGregor". It is a find, not a cache. Colour, D.
4. **PRAIRIE DU CHIEN, Spanish silver in a mound.** WI Hist. Coll. v.9 p.~121-122: about 20 years before, Austin Birge, digging in a mound at **lower Prairie du Chien**, found a large silver coin (2⅛ in., 776 grains) with bones and pottery; it is now with the WHS. Colour.
5. **MADISON WI, Sorenson's garden coins.** WI Hist. Coll. v.10 p.~(Madison chapter): an American cent of 1798 and several Spanish silver coins picked up in 1880 in Sorenson's garden, possibly at the site of **Armel's** trading "grog-shop" (the first building at Madison). Colour; urban.
6. **WATERTOWN WI.** Wis. Mag. Hist. 4 (1921): Watertown (Johnson's Rapids) was "notorious as the headquarters of counterfeiters and horse thieves" in its first two decades. Colour.
7. **COUNCIL BLUFFS 1875, $350 in gold** found near an old tree on the D. B. Clark trail (Samuels murder money; Iowa J. H&P v.10 p.~172). Out of region (SW Iowa), so held.
