### 1900 census and Sanborn (2026-11-02)
- **1900 census:** "**William Trommler**", **Fergus Falls, Ward 1**, ED 169, sheet 9, dwelling 151 (Ancestry 7602, record 26843493).
  - Age 73, b. Sep 1826 Germany, **single, day labourer**.
  - **Owns his house free of mortgage.**
  - **Street: "Mill Street", house number 11.** The census does not say N. or S.
- **LoC Sanborn, Fergus Falls:**
  - **1903 (sanborn04297_004).** The index lists N. Mill 100-226 / 101-225 (sheets 7 and 6) and 300+ (sheets 2-3); S. Mill 100-124 (sheet 6) and 101-125 (sheet 7). So **the 1903 numbering starts at 100: there is no "11" on the 1903 maps.** The city renumbered between 1900 and 1903. S. Mill 101-125 on sheet 7 is the downtown commercial row (block 66: Journal Printing, confectionery, saloons, offices), not houses.
  - **1896 (sanborn04297_003).** The index lists **N. Mill 1-223 (sheets 4 and 3)** and **S. Mill 1-224 (sheet 7)**, so the old scheme did use low numbers. On 1896 sheet 7, S. Mill runs S from the river footbridge; the first frontage at E. Bismarck Ave is no. 93 (the Odd Fellows/dentist block), with 106-110 and the Columbia Hotel south of Bismarck. **No "11" was found on the S. Mill frontage of sheet 7.** In the 1896 scheme no. 11 is most likely on **N. Mill** (sheet 4, north of the river), not S. Mill.
- **1896 sheet 4 (N. Mill, read later):** N. Mill numbers run 201-215 at E. Lincoln Ave (Picket Blk, Williams Blk) and fall to 101/91/89/87/85 going S to the footbridge and the Otter Tail flour mill (block 67). **No numbers below about 85 appear on N. Mill**, so "11" is not on N. Mill near the river either. The 1896 low numbers (1-84) are not on the downtown sheets, which suggests no. 11 lay on an outer residential stretch that was unmapped or not numbered.
- **Result: not placed to a lot.** Next step: read 1896 sheet 4 (N. Mill 1-223) for a small dwelling at no. 11. If the story insists on S. Mill, the 1903 renumbering means 11 = about 1xx; check the 1909 Sanborn against a city directory.
