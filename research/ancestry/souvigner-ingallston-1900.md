# John Souvigner (Souvignier), Arthur Bay / Ingallston, Menominee Co. MI: 1900 census (2026-11-01)

Viewed only.

- **1900 census, Ingallston [Twp], Menominee Co. MI**, ED 136, sheet 8, dwelling 196 / family 143 (Ancestry 7602, record 25172183; indexed "Johen Souvignier", alt. "Johen Sourrgnier").
  - Age 51, **b. Aug 1848 Belgium**, immigrated 1870, naturalized, **single**, **day labourer**, **rents**, house (not farm). Lives alone.
  - This fits the 1907 wire: "no relatives in this country". He was a renter, which explains why he is on no 1912 plat.
- **Neighbours on the same sheet, in order around him:**
  - Before him: the Williams families (Edward, Bourth, Seme W., Joesg, Mode, Paurk Williams), then Souvignier.
  - After him: **Abner Backer [Baker]** and family (Rabel, Wille C., Lowee), **Wm E. Williams**, and Albert Jrenier [Grenier?].
  - Earlier on the sheet: Linard and Joseph Williams, **William Belhie [Beattie]**, Hurjan, Will England, **Stin Van Paten [Van Patten]**, Joeff Kory, Johen Lwobe, Johen Farther, **Charles Baly [Bailey]**, Johen Renard, Gugens Nichols, Joheg Petraveth.
- **Placement:** Van Patten, Bailey, Williams and Beattie are the same names as the shore holders on the 1912 Ogle atlas, **leaf 32 (Fract. T33N R25-26W, Ingallston)**; see research/souvigner-plat.md. So the cabin was among the Williams, Baker and Van Patten holdings on the Ingallston shore (Arthur Bay vicinity).
## Leaf 32 read at full resolution (1912 Ogle atlas, archive.org page n31, Fract. T33N R25-26W)
The NE corner is the shore cluster that matches the 1900 census sheet. The BLM sections fall in R26W (PLSSID MI190330N0260W0).
- **Sec 1** (BLM -87.4728..-87.4520, 45.2736..45.2873): **S. R. Van Patten** 59.00 (shore lot) and **69.00 with "Res."** at the shore; **Adelbert Bailey 40**; Hugo Johnson 40 (Res.); Val. Johnson 40; John Bloomquist? (shore); Menominee Abstract & Land Co.
- **Sec 12** (fractional shore strip, BLM -87.4710..-87.4665, 45.2649..45.2737): **H. Williams 33.80**, **"Res. J.? Williams" / Mrs. Williams** with a house mark, a **School** mark, and **L. Williams 39.60**.
- **Sec 11**, E side: **Wm. Beattie 40**, Leonard Williams 40 and 160, Eli Williams 40, Aug. Garrops 80 (Res.), **Bailey & Beattie**, C. W. Chase.
- **Sec 14**, shore: **A. Bailey 57.00**, Nat. Bailey, Jos. Schwab 77, R. E. Miller, D. C. Miller, Mrs. J. Samuelson, Emil Odean.
- **No Baker or Backer tract anywhere on the plate** (the shore from sec 1 down to sec 22 was read). Abner Baker had evidently gone by 1912, and as a renter he may never have owned land.
- **Placement:** Souvigner was enumerated within the Williams run (Edward, then Souvignier, then Baker, then Wm E. Williams). In 1912 the Williams houses and the school sit in **sec 12 and the adjoining SE part of sec 1 / E part of sec 11**, about 2.5 km SW of the Arthur Bay P.O. (sec 30 T34N R25W).
- **Suggested pin (cabin area): about 45.274, -87.468, r600**, at the Williams shore cluster at the sec 1/11/12 corner on the Green Bay shore. **Confidence C:** this is the census neighbourhood plus plat, not a parcel. It replaces the D fallback at the Arthur Bay hamlet.
