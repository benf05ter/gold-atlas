# Ole Johnson (Carney, Menominee MI) and Nicholas Koster (Garden, Delta MI): census and death (Ancestry, 2026-11-01)

Viewed only.

## Ole Johnson: 1900 census, Nadeau Twp, Menominee Co.
- **Carney Village**, ED 148, sheet 1-A, enumerated 1 June 1900 by Carl R. Cramer [?] (image 4120243_00693; record 25190044).
- **Dwelling 4: Ole Johnson, head, b. Oct 1844 Sweden**, immigrated 1870, naturalized, **farmer, owns farm free of mortgage**. Married 1878 to **Annie** (b. Sept 1859 Sweden, 40; no children). **No nephew Emil** in the household.
- Neighbours in order:
  - Herman Marks (1, RR section foreman)
  - Peter Riddes (2, farmer, Holland)
  - Joseph Farley (3, farmer)
  - **Ole Johnson (4)**
  - Anton Archer (5, farmer, with servants Nellie and Henry Rutter)
  - John Plankey (6, mill labourer)
- **Placement:** the page header is "Nadeau Township ... Carney Village", but he is listed as a farmer among the first dwellings. That fits a farm at the edge of Carney rather than in the village grid. Sec 30 T37N R26W (the 1885 patent) is about 1 mi from Carney village, so the census is **consistent with** the patent 80 being his home farm in 1900. The census does not prove it.
- **1910:** no Ole Johnson indexed in Menominee Co. He may have died or moved between 1900 and 1910.

## Nicholas Koster: 1900 census and death
- **1900 census, Sack Bay Twp, Delta Co.** (indexed "Nicklos Coster"): ED 45, sheet 6-B, enumerated 4-5 June 1900 by Edwin J. Byrne (image 4120220_00685; record 64340224).
  - **Dwelling 15: Nicklos Koster [Coster], head, b. Dec 1827 Germany, single, immigrated 1852, farmer, owns farm.** He lived alone, which fits the miser story.
  - Neighbours in order:
    - Mary A. Kynaston (10)
    - Edwin J. Byrne (11, farmer, the enumerator)
    - Burton Shipman
    - Gunder Fathe [?] (12)
    - Robert Fagan [?] (13)
    - Joseph McGregor (14)
    - **Koster (15)**
    - Peter Byrnes (16)
    - John Watchorn (17, farmer)
    - Felix Ronzy [?] (18)
    - Joseph Beaudry (19, farmer)
- **Death:** *Michigan Death Records* gives **Nickolas Koester, b. 1826, d. 3 Feb 1909, Fairbanks Twp, Delta Co.** (record 2315882). That matches the c.1909 miser death.
  - Sack Bay Twp lay on the Garden Peninsula; Fairbanks Twp is its southern part. Township lines were reorganised around then, so the two need not be different places.
- **Use:** match Kynaston, Byrne, McGregor, Byrnes, Watchorn, Ronzy and Beaudry on a c.1900-1910 Delta Co. plat of the Garden Peninsula to place Koster's farm.
