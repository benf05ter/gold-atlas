# Julius Behr, Town of Sheboygan WI (d. Feb 1930): Ancestry checks (2026-11-01)

Viewed only. The story ("$4,500 in gold and silver ... in an iron box", Behr house in Lyman's Addition, Town of Sheboygan) is in research/newspapers-com/pc-a-asks-results.md.

- **Probate:** Sheboygan Co. is **not in** Ancestry's WI Wills and Probate collection (9088). The browse list has 38 counties and no Sheboygan. A Behr/Bahr/Baehr search for 1930 finds only other counties. **No inventory available.** The estate (executor or administrator Bruns, final-account hearing Jan 1931, per the Sheboygan Press) would need the Sheboygan Co. Register in Probate.
- **1910 census:** "Julious Behr", **Sheboygan city**, ED 122, sheet 18A (Ancestry 7884, record 31182350).
  - Age 43, b. 1867 Austria, immigrated 1867, naturalized, **single**, lives alone.
  - **Cabinet maker, furniture factory**, wage earner. **Owns his house, mortgaged.**
  - The street is on the sheet image (not opened); the neighbours were not read.
- **1920 census:** no Julius Behr indexed in Sheboygan Co.; probably mis-indexed.
- **1926 Sheboygan city directory, rural section:** "**Behr Julius**" listed under **SHEBOYGAN Route 3** (between Begemann Adolph and Beiersdorf Albert). There is no street number. This fits "late of the Town of Sheboygan" (outside city limits, Lyman flats area).
- **Result:** no money figure. The confirmed profile is a single, Austrian-born cabinet maker who owned his own house and was on R.R. 3 by 1926. The exact lot needs a Lyman's Addition plat (Town of Sheboygan) or the 1910 sheet's street column.
