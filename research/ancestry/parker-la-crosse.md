# Mary Ann Parker, City of La Crosse WI (d. 25 Aug 1901): will and contest (Ancestry, 2026-11-01)

**Collection:** *Wisconsin, U.S., Wills and Probate Records, 1800-1987* (coll. 9088), La Crosse Co., "Probate Case Files, 2961-3043, 1901-1902". Viewed only.
- Ancestry record 1788583; 244 images, starting at image 007083867_01359.
- Index: will date 26 Sep 1900 (also a 7 May 1901 instrument); probate 28 Oct 1901.
- Related records: 1788358 (guardianship and administration, 10 Oct 1901) and 2239218.

## Read so far
- **Image 01359, notice of application:** "The application of **Thomas Shimmin** that an instrument in writing **dated May 7th, 1901**, purporting to be the last will and testament of Mary A. Parker, deceased, late of the **City and County of La Crosse** ... be proven." Heard at the 1st Tuesday of October 1901.
- **Image 01366:** "Proof of will not contested", filed 26 Nov 1901, John Brindley [?], county judge.
- **Images 01367-01368, bill of costs (Oct 1902):** "In the matter of the Last Will and Testament of Mary Ann Parker".
  - A **3-day trial**, with depositions of Mr. and Mrs. Harry W. Reed.
  - Proponents' witnesses: F. P. Hixon, E. E. Ferris, Minnie Ferris, Ellen J. Lush, Geo. B. Marvin, Dr. A. A. Maurer, Mrs. A. A. Maurer, G. M. Woodward, Thomas Shimmin.
  - Attorneys: McConnell & Schweizer (proponents); **Doherty & Baldwin (contestants)**.
- **So the will was contested and tried in 1902.**

## General inventory (images 01428-01430; filed 17 Nov 1902, John Brindley, county judge)
- By **Thomas Shimmin Jr., executor**, up to 15 Oct 1902.
- **Real estate:** "All of **Lots numbered six (6) and seven (7) of Block number three (3) of J. Burns, G. Farnam and P. Burns' Addition** to the Village (now city) of La Crosse, according to the plat of said addition of record in the office of the register of deeds for La Crosse County, **except the south fifty five (55) feet of said lots six (6) and seven (7)**, heretofore conveyed to the Trustees of the Baptist Church of La Crosse, all in La Crosse, La Crosse County, Wisconsin."
  - Lot 6: $1,200. Lot 7: $1,500.
- **Personal estate: blank.** No cash or money found is inventoried.
- So the house lot = **Lots 6-7, Block 3, Burns, Farnam & Burns' Addition, La Crosse**, the N part, beside the Baptist church. A La Crosse plat or Sanborn would put coordinates on it.
