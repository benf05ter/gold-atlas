## 3. VOLLMER Sanborn: 305 S. Main is in the GRAND OPERA HOUSE block (1891)
- **LoC Sanborn Stillwater, Aug 1891 (sanborn04395_00x; images labelled 04395_1891-00NN), sheet 9.** The index has S. Main 201-442 on sheet 9.
- **E side of S. Main between E. Olive and Nelson:** the block is numbered 233-327 northward to southward.
  - **"GRAND OPERA HO." occupies 303-311** (3-storey brick): a saloon / office 2nd floor, a grocery, and a furniture store on the 1st and 2nd floors, with the opera stage behind.
  - Then 315 (crockery and glassware), 319 (furniture), and 323-327 (Gazette Printing 2nd floor, cabinet shop).
- **So in 1891 "305 S. Main" is a storefront in the Grand Opera House block, not a detached dwelling.** In 1900 Margaret Volmer (79) and her niece were at 305, most likely living in rooms above or behind a store there. That fits a downtown merchant family. (The W side across the street is the Union Block, 304-314.)
- The 1904/1910 sheets were not read. (see below for #4)

## 15. KEARNEY homestead, Putnam Twp (Livingston MI): NOT on the plats
- **1915 plate (image 31617_072122-00752, viewer p.27), all 36 sections read at 2x: no Kearney / Kearny / Carney.** Note the nearby Kennedy family (W. Kennedy 137 ac in sec 12 in 1875).
- **1875 Beers atlas (Livingston 1875, viewer p.44 of 53, image 31617_072122-00715, "Putnam", T1N R4E), read at 2x: also no Kearney.** There is "P. Kennedy 100A" in sec 30 W and "E. W. Kennedy 137A" in sec 12 NE; neither is Kearney.
- **Census (indexes):**
  - 1870 and 1880: **John M. Kearney (b. about 1827 NY) and Margaret (b. about 1828 NY), Putnam**, with son Edward T. (b. 1861). Their son **Emmet Kearney (b. 1854), wife Anna E. and son Lee C.** are on the 1880 sheet among Pinckney village households (merchants, postmaster, blacksmiths; image 4241732-00412).
  - 1900: Margaret Kearney (b. Jul 1824 NY), head, Putnam.
  - **1910: Margaret Kearney, 85, head, in PINCKNEY VILLAGE** (ED 112, sheet 5A, image 31111_4330158-00645).
- **So the Kearneys show up as Pinckney village people in 1880 and 1910, and no farm is under their name in 1875 or 1915.** The "Kearney homestead" may have been a village house or lot, or land held under another name (a son-in-law, or a Dillon purchase from the heirs after 1917). **Not placed.** The next steps would be a probate (John M. or Margaret Kearney, Livingston) or the deed index, which go on the Sunday list.

## 4. RACINE 320 Fifth St: a DWELLING in 1908
- **LoC Sanborn Racine 1908 (sanborn09676_004), sheet 3 (09676_1908-0003)**, S edge, N side of FIFTH St between College Ave (W) and Main (E).
- The frontage numbers run 322 (corner, "School of Commerce", 2-storey brick) / **320 = a 2-storey frame DWELLING ("D", with a 1-storey rear wing)** / 316 / 312 (Fair Mfg Co.) / 310-308 (The Times Printing) / 306-304 (Air Dome summer vaudeville theatre, no roof) / 302-300 (Citizens Tel. Co.).
- **So yes: in 1908, 320 Fifth was an old frame house, between the School of Commerce and a livery behind.** That fits the story's "old house" before the K of C (1931 address). Later Sanborns (1920s) were not read.
- **Pin: N side of Fifth St, second lot E of College Ave, Racine** (urban, look-only). Renumbering is possible, but the 1891 and 1900 numbers agree in range. **Pin: Grand Opera House block, E side of S. Main between Olive and Nelson, Stillwater** (look-only, downtown).
