## 2. Census placements (1900/1910)
- **SWEM / Swen, D. W. (Galien, Berrien MI):** 1900 census indexed as "**Daniel W. Swern**", b. Apr 1843 OH, wife Corintha, sons Elmo and Columbus C. **Galien [village/Twp], ED 64, sheet 2, dwelling 26. MERCHANT, owns a house (not a farm), free.** Neighbours: James Edgin, Henry Currlain, Delbert Prince, Fred Berger, James H. Morse, Francis Burris, Joseph C. Harris, Isaac Horner. Also in Galien: Harry Swem (b. 1870), and William Swern (b. 1835, wife Lucretia), who is likely the "Wm. F. Swem" of the 1887 plat. So by 1900 D. W. lived in Galien village as a merchant; the farm land in secs 15-16 was William's.
- **PADDOCK, Mathew (Weesaw):** no Mathew Paddock in Berrien in 1900 (he was b. about 1804). The only Paddock is Ervin (b. 1830 NY), New Buffalo Twp. Placement stays on the 1850 census (paddock-weesaw-census.md).
- **SOUVIGNER:** already placed: Ingallston Twp, 1900, ED 136 sh 8 (souvigner-ingallston-1900.md).
- **HARVEY (Butler Co. IA, 1910/1920):** there is **no Harvey household head in Butler Co.** in either census. In 1910 there are only Ferd Harvey (servant, Albion Twp) and Loyd T. Harvey (stepson, Coldwater Twp). A widow "Mrs. Harvey" may be indexed under another surname, or arrived after 1920.
- **BROWN, Joseph (Boone IA 1910):** already placed: Dodge Twp, ED 13, sh 2A, owns farm free (iowa-town-leads-plats.md).
- **RANDALL, David (Jackson Co. WI, 1900):** **Melrose [village], ED 54, sheet 10, dwelling 104.** Age 67, b. Jan 1832 NY, **single, jeweler**, in a house. North Bend is in the adjoining Town of Melrose, so he lived in **Melrose village**, not on a North Bend farm, in 1900.
