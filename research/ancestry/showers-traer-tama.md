# Isaiah Showers (Shower), Tama Co. IA (d. 1904; $2,500 gold under the woodshed) (PC b, 2026-11-02)

Viewed only (Ancestry 7602 and 9064).

## 1900 census
- "**Isiah Showers**", **Perry Twp, Tama Co.** [Perry Twp contains the town of **Traer**], ED 141, sheet 1, dwelling 234 (record 16178300).
  - Age 71, b. 1829 Ohio, **single**, occupation "**capitalist**". **Owns a house** (not a farm).
  - The neighbouring entries (Schram, Truesdell, Atkins, Ziebell, Pollock, Brubaker, Pritchard, Kershaw, Greelis, Glosser, Kleespie, Green) look like a **Traer town** street sequence. No street or house number is indexed for him.
- **Place correction:** the will (below) calls him "**a resident of Traer**". The lead's "Toledo" (the county seat) is probably wrong. The woodshed would be at his **house in Traer**.

## Probate (Iowa Wills & Probate, Tama Co. *Will Records, 1854-1913*, Vol 4-5, 1892-1913)
- **Will** (index date 1 Sep 1899; image 007592590_00246; record 1904979). There is also an earlier will/codicil entry (record 1904731, image 00021, not opened).
  - Earlier will: farm in trust to the **First Methodist Episcopal Church of Traer** (he had long supported it). Land: **NE¼ and E½ of NW¼, sec 24, T85N R14W**, and **W 80 ac of NW¼, sec 19, T85N R13W**, "together with all the buildings". He provided for a monument of at least $500 on his burial lot in **Buckingham Cemetery**, with the rest of the income to the church.
  - Later will: **S. M. Endicott of Traer**, executor. Debts and funeral paid from personal estate. To **C. A. Pratt** as trustee: **S 120 ac of SE¼ sec 2, T85N R14W**, rents to the **Iowa Children's Home Society**; **SW¼ sec 24, T85N R14W**, rents to the **Iowa Humane Society**. Para. 5 confirms the sec 19 and sec 24 lands for the Traer M.E. Church, with a monument of not less than $1,000.
- **No inventory or town-lot description** in the will book. His **Traer house lot is not named** (he left only farm lands by description). The 1904 gold under the woodshed would be at the Traer residence. It needs the Tama Co. probate case file, or a 1900s Traer directory or Sanborn for his street.
- The farm sections (all T85N, near Traer) are recorded for reference only. The story points to the town house, not these farms.
