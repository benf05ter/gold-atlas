## 1. PINE RUN cave, Brandon Twp (Jackson Co IA): Cooley tract found, creek unlabelled
- **Ancestry 1127, Jackson 1893, viewer p.20 of 52 (image 31617_072143-00804), "Brandon", T85N R1E, atlas p.23.** Read at 2x.
- **"Isaiah Cooley 160" is in the N half of sec 27**, just S of Emeline P.O. (sec 22 S edge). Neighbours: James Swift (strip, sec 27 W / 22), L. Nagele and Levi Wagoner (sec 26 NW), William Brady (sec 27 S), James Johnson Est. (sec 26 SW).
- **No stream is labelled "Pine Run" on the plate.** Two unnamed creeks run S from the Emeline area:
  - one along the sec 26/27 line, past Cooley's E side;
  - one through secs 22/27/28 with "Springs" and "Sp." marks (D. V. Miller 190, sec 28), dropping SW past Brandon School (sec 33 NW) into the South Fork Maquoketa.
- The SW creek through D. V. Miller's springs and the Brandon School ravine (secs 28/33) is the one that cuts into the bluffs. **Inference: Pine Run is one of these two creeks; which one is not proven.**
- **Pin suggestion: Cooley 160, N½ sec 27 T85N R1E, about 0.4 mi S of Emeline (Emeline 42.155,-90.831) → about 42.149,-90.830, r800, D.** It needs a USGS/GNIS name check for "Pine Run" (I did not run a coordinate tool for this list).

## 2. WHITFORD cabin / Page block, Fergus Falls: Page's street address found
- **1900 census (7602): Henry G. Page, b. Mar 1832 NH, head, wife Alice H. (b. 1849 NY), son Henry G. Jr. (b. 1879), Fergus Falls, at 219 WHITFORD STREET.**
- **LoC Sanborn Fergus Falls, Jan 1903, sheet 7 (04297_1903-0007).** The index puts N. Whitford 100-227 on sheet 7.
  - **N. Whitford runs N-S between E. Summit Av (N) and E. Cavour Av.**
  - **The E side (block 64) backs onto the Red River.** Its house numbers are new even / old odd in brackets: 200 (201), 204 (205), 208/208½, 212 (a church "to be vacated"), 222 (223) at the N end near E. Summit. Below them are the St. Fergus Iron Works and the Fergus Bottling Works.
  - The W side is the Fergus Casket Co. (201-203) and a vacant block.
- **So 219 is old numbering on the E (river) side, between the 212 church and 222 (223), in block 64, the N half, near E. Summit Ave.** That is the block "inside Page's fence" where the 1916 history puts the Whitford cabin cellar, on the river side of N. Whitford, which fits a first-settler cabin by the falls.
- Other Sanborn years: 1884, 1890, 1896, 1909, 1915 and 1924 are online (sanborn04297_001-007). Not read.
- **Pin: E side of N. Whitford St, N of E. Cavour Ave toward E. Summit, river side, Fergus Falls, r100, D** (the street centroid 46.2776,-96.0706 is close; urban, look-only).

## 8. CARRIER cave, Black Hammer Twp (Houston Co MN): Carrier farms found

## 4. OSSIAN (Winneshiek 1877, pp.131-132): building names found
- J. O. (John Ossian) Porter's 18x20 frame house was built "**near the site of the old Quarter House**" (used on the Fort Crawford to Fort Atkinson road). It became the first tavern and a stage station for the McGregor stages.
- Capt. Caleb Brooks built a stone building used for years as a hotel.
- The original town plat (1855, 14 lots) was on the "**southeast corner of the section**".
- The counterfeiter paragraph names **no person and no building**. The plat inset was not opened.
- Pin stays village-level D. The Porter tavern / Quarter House site is a better colour spot if a plat names Porter's lot.

## 5. LYONS den (Ionia-Montcalm 1881 p.240): re-read, no location
- The full passage gives no bank, cabin or owner: a "counterfeiters' den" was found near Lyons in 1835 or 1836, the gang fled, and their tools and dies were later found in the river. Nothing more to place. The 1875 Ionia plat was not opened.

## 7. SARANAC (Ionia-Montcalm 1881 ~p.206): re-read, no name
- Pre-1857 bogus Mexican dollars made "in the village". The makers were later arrested as housebreakers. **No person, building or farm is named.** It stays a leads.tsv row.

## 9. MORRIS smithy (Clayton 1916, Price letter, p.~428-429): re-read
- The text says only "two upon the north side [of the Turkey River], both older than the Henderson shop … one by a manufacturer of counterfeit coin in the fall of 1834; this was the first shop in the county", and names William Morris "(bogus)".
- **No township or section.** The Henderson shop (1836) was on the S bank near the mouth of the Henderson branch, NW corner of Millville Twp, so the Morris shop was somewhere N of the Turkey, probably the same lower-Turkey neighbourhood. Not placeable. It stays a lead.
