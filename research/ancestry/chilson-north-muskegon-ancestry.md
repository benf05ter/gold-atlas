# Harvey Chilson, North Muskegon MI: dates and directory entries (Ancestry, 2026-11-01)

Viewed only.

- **Find a Grave index:** Harvey Chilson, **b. 1825 Whitehall, Washington Co. NY; d. 12 Jun 1897, North Muskegon**, Muskegon Co.
- **Because he died in 1897 there is no 1900 or 1910 census for him.** The 1900/1910 street-and-number request can't be met for Harvey himself.
- **Marriage:** 23 Jan 1881, Dalton Twp, Muskegon Co.; residence then Fruitland Twp.
- **Polk's Muskegon city directory, 1895**, North Muskegon section, p.400 (U.S. City Directories image 8848932):
  - "**Chilson Harvey, poultry, res Center.**" That is, he was a poultry dealer living on **Center Street**, North Muskegon. No house number is given; the North Muskegon section lists streets only.
- **1897 directory**, North Muskegon section (image 7140329): "Chilson Harvey, died March, 1897".
  - The month conflicts with Find a Grave's 12 June. The 1897 directory was probably compiled in spring, so March is likely.
- An 1891 directory hit also exists (residence North Muskegon); not opened.
- **Placement:** residence on **Center St., North Muskegon**. That fits the plat note (chilson-north-muskegon.md). No lot number.
