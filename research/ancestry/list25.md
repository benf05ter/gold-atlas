## 22. Wabasis "Tower caves" lidar check (USGS 3DEP hillshade, view only via the elevation.nationalmap.gov ImageServer exportImage; nothing downloaded)
- **BLM:** sec 29 T9N R9W (MI19) = 43.1328-43.1473 N, 85.4117-85.3918 W; sec 28 lies E of it (85.3919-85.3721 W).
- **Views:**
  - A wide view: bbox -85.4060,43.1380 to -85.3880,43.1490.
  - A close view: bbox -85.3990,43.1385 to -85.3910,43.1435.
- **What shows:**
  - A **straight modern road runs NE-SW** down the E side of sec 29 to the NW shore of the W lobe of Wabasis Lake. At its N end it meets the N-S road on the 28/29 line, which matches the 1894 plat road by the C. O. Tower 35 tract.
  - Just E of the modern road a **second, older road trace** curves alongside it for about 250 m. It is a sunken bed with a small bank, roughly 43.1405-43.1425, -85.3945 to -85.3935: probably the 1890s-1910s lake road, bypassed by the straight road.
  - **Between the roads and the lake is a steep, continuous cut bank / scarp, about 300 m long**, running SW from about 43.1412, -85.3938 to 43.1395, -85.3960. Its centre is about **43.140, -85.395**. The face is irregular, with a few small notches or hollows at about 43.1399, -85.3952, but at 1 m resolution **no distinct cave mouths or pit clusters** can be confirmed. Hollows of a metre or so would not show.
  - Also: an **oval raised enclosure with hummocky pits inside**, about 60 × 35 m, in the field W of the road at **about 43.1419, -85.3979** (NE¼ sec 29, roughly on the C. O. / M. A. Tower land). It could be an old farmstead or foundation and dump area. Worth a look on the 1938 aerial.
- **Verdict:** a real road-cut bank along the lake road exists where the 1916 article and the 1894 plat put the Tower farm. It is **suitable for a second pin at about 43.140, -85.395, r150, grade C** ("caves in the road bank", searched site, nothing found). Lidar alone does not prove the caves. Most of the shore is now county-park / private land, so ground check only with permission.
