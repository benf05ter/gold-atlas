## #105 Walter Terrell, Johnson Co.
- **Johnson Co. will book pp. 252-253** (image 007592127_00159; record 1081668). Index probate date 9 Apr 1885.
- "I, Walter Terrell of Johnson County, State of Iowa ..."
  - To his wife **Jane J. Terrell:** "my timber lot of land, known as the NW¼ of the NE¼ of Sec. 4, Township 79 North of Range 6 West, containing about 40 acres; also the SW fractional ¼ of Section 3 of said Township and Range, lying west of the Iowa River, 77 or 78 acres"; also lots 6 and 7 in section 22, [T?] N R 6 W of the 5th P.M.; land in ___ County, Illinois, near Carlyle, Illinois; "**Three Thousand Dollars in Cash ($3000.00)** and Fifty Shares of Stock in the **Iowa City National Bank**".
  - To wife and daughter **Mary A. Terrell** jointly: "**the Homestead upon which I now reside, being about 40 acres in the west end of Lots 3 and 4, in Section 3, Township 79, north Range 6, West of the 5th P.M.**", plus household furniture and books.
  - All other property ("lands, money, rights and credits ... wherever the same may be") to daughter Mary A.
  - Executors: wife Jane J. and daughter Mary A. **"it is my wish that they shall not be required ... to file an inventory, nor make reports"**, so no court inventory would exist.
- **Year check:** the index's 9 Apr 1885 is probably the will date or the recording date. The neighbouring entry on p.252 shows a court term of 1887 (Priscilla ___, recorded 9 Mar 1887). That fits the list's "died 1887". Not resolved from this page.
- **Placement:** the **homestead = W end of Lots 3-4, sec 3, T79N R6W** (Johnson Co., by the Iowa River, north of Iowa City). That is the house where any money "found 1906" would likely be.
