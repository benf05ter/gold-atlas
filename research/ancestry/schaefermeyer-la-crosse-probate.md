# C. Henry (Conrad Henry) Schaefermeyer, La Crosse Co. WI (d. 1 Mar 1903): probate (2026-11-01)

Viewed only. Source: *WI Wills and Probate* (coll. 9088), La Crosse **Probate Case Files 3227-3297, 1903**. The file starts at about **image 699 of 2173** (cover image 700, "Henry C. Schafemeyer, Decd", Vol 28 p.301, filed 14 Mar 1903). The Ancestry index is misaligned for this film: image 700 is indexed under a "24 Oct 1917" record, so the name search finds nothing.

- **Administration:** intestate. **Jacob (J.) Hahn** of La Crosse was administrator (letters by early Apr 1903). Appraisers were Henry Niebuhr and W. J. Hickisch, under a warrant dated 8 Apr 1903 (img 745). Notice to creditors 8 Apr 1903, with claims heard 3 Nov 1903 (img 760). County Judge John Brindley.
- **Account (img 742-743):** "To amount as per inventory: **Real Estate $8,800.00; Personal Property $3,323.17**; total $12,123.17."
  - Receipts 1903-04 are rents on a **State St. property** (La Crosse city) and on "12 acres Geo. Fisher", plus notes and interest (Henry Ritter, Miller, Anton Wecker, J. B. Cappellen mortgage) and the sale of hay, an old cutter and a wagon.
  - **No line for cash or gold found on the premises.** The personal estate was mostly notes and mortgages.
  - Payments include the Dr Smith services, C. H. Parry funeral expenses, messages to Texas and St. Louis (relatives), and claims by Mrs Otto and others. An heir, **Anna Bartchenfeldt**, objected to charges in the final account (img 747).
- **Land (from a later corrective petition, img 708, c.1917, "estate of Conrad H. Schaefermeyer").** In T16N R7W:
  - (c) **133.67 acres in secs 14 and 15:** all of Lot 3 [sec 14] except the railroad right of way; 2 acres of SW¼ SW¼ sec 14; the E 25 acres of Lot 9 and the E 14 acres of the NE¼ [SE¼?] of sec 15.
  - As corrected: the part of SW¼ SW¼ sec 14 north of the **Bostwick Valley Road** (about ½ acre); **NW¼ SW¼ sec 14**; Lot 3 sec 14 less the railroad; and the E 30.28 acres of Lot 9 and NE¼ SE¼ sec 15, running to the La Crosse River.
  - (d) **Lot 6, Block 6, Burns, Farnam & Burns Addition**, La Crosse city.
  - (e) **Lot 11, Block 1, Park Addition**, La Crosse city.
- **Placement check:** this confirms the 1874 plat strip in W sec 14 T16N R7W (research/plats/schaeffermeyer-la-crosse.md, pin 43.864,-91.1876). The farm house is most likely on NW¼ SW¼ or Lot 3 of sec 14, near the Bostwick Valley Road. Confidence rises from B- to B.
