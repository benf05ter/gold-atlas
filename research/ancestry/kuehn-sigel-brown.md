## 2. Will record
- **Same collection, Brown County, "Wills, Vol 39c, 1903-1909"**, images 176-177 of 321 (image ids 004406667_00176-00177), printed pp. 463-464.
- **The will is dated 3 March 1906**, one day before his death. He signed with his mark ("Fred X Kuehn").
- **Witnesses:** Hub. A. Hillesheim of Sigel, Brown Co., and Jos. A. Eckstein of New Ulm.
- **Admitted to probate on the 19th day of April 1906**, by S. A. George, Judge of Probate. That was about a week after the 13-14 Apr 1906 jar find.
- **Plain transcription of the key clauses:**
  - "I, Fred Kuehn of the Town of Sigel in the County of Brown and State of Minnesota ... do make, publish and declare this to be my Last Will and Testament."
  - First: debts and funeral expenses.
  - "Second ... I give, devise and bequeath to my son Friedrich Kuehn the sum of five hundred dollars, to be held in trust however by my friend Ferdinand Crone of New Ulm, Minn. The annual interest on said sum or any part of the principal may be paid over by said trustee to my said son Friedrich Kuehn at such times and in such amounts as my said designated trustee may determine, and he alone shall be the sole judge in that matter, without interference by anyone. If on the death of my said son Friedrich Kuehn, there should be any balance of said trust fund on hand, the same shall be paid over to my sister in law Dorothea Kuehn as a slight reward for what she has done for me. I do not want to leave any more to my son Friedrich Kuehn, as he does not deserve it, and even the amount I do leave him by this will shall and will be well guarded by the trustee herein named as otherwise the bad company kept by my son Friedrich would improperly deprive him thereof."
  - "Third. All my property shall be reduced to cash by my executor, who is hereby empowered to sell the whole thereof at public or private sale. After the foregoing legacy to my son is paid over ... the balance of my property shall be divided ... to my nieces in equal shares ..." The names begin "Emma Fritsche"; the rest are at the foot of p.463 and were not fully read.
  - "Fourth. No other person shall have or receive any part of my estate or greater share than above set out."
  - "Lastly, I make, constitute and appoint **Otto Schell of New Ulm**, Minn. to be Executor ..."

## What this settles
- **The "will buried in the yard" was found** (or a copy existed). A will dated 3 Mar 1906 was proved on 19 Apr 1906, with Otto Schell as executor. That matches the special administrator named in the April 1906 wire story.
- **The will contest:** the son Friedrich received only $500 in trust; the residue went to nieces. This is the will the son tried to "dissolve" in May 1906 (New Ulm Review 1906-05-16 p9). The outcome is not in this volume. The case file (no. 975) is not in the Ancestry Brown Co. case-file volumes, which stop at no. 398.
- No money inventory is in this record.
