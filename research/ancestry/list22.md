## 14. Canaris Phillips: GB and the Bronson Twp plat
- GB "Canary Phillips" 1895-1930: 0. "Canaris Phillips": 3 hits. **Coldwater Daily Reporter, 8 Mar 1897, p.3, Real Estate Sales: "Canaris N. Phillips to David and M. M. Warner, 20 acres on sec. 11, Bronson, $900."** Sec 11 T7S R8W is at Bronson village itself (the village straddles secs 11-14), so this was a separate small lot, sold in 1897.
- **1915 Branch atlas, Bronson Twp T7S R8W (atlas p.53, viewer p.28, image 31617_072119-00298):** **"C. N. Phillips 86.56"** is a N-S tract in the **N part of sec 5** (E of "F. Rickards et al 80"; Frank Coward 40/196 S and E of it; Bronson village at the E edge, secs 11-14).
- **So the Phillips farm straddles the Matteson/Bronson line:** about 86.56 ac in N sec 5 (Bronson Twp) plus the 40 in S sec 32 (Matteson). Sec 5 = 41.8834-41.8976 N, 85.2723-85.2529 W; sec 32 Matteson = 41.8976-41.9123 N. Bronson village is about 41.87,-85.195, so the farm lies **about 3.5-4 mi W/NW** of the village. This fits "four miles northwest".
- **Pin refinement: about 41.896, -85.259, r350, grade B** (on the township line between the sec 5 tract and the sec 32 strip). The house is most likely on the larger 86.56 in sec 5. "Two miles east" is not supported by any Phillips land (the sec 11 village lot was sold in 1897).

## 8. Knaggs Bridge plat
- Ancestry 1127 has no 1895 Shiawassee atlas (only 1875 and 1915). **1915 Ogle Shiawassee atlas, inset "NAGG'S BRIDGE, located in the E½ Sec. 6, Burns Twp." (atlas p.28-29, viewer p.16, image 31617_072125-00275):**
  - The **Shiawassee River** runs along the W edge; **"Grand River Road"** crosses the river at the S part of the plat (the bridge).
  - The **"Consumers Power Co."** holds two parcels on the river at the crossing, which suggests a power dam/pond there by 1915. A small village grid (Warren, Florence and Gaylord Sts) sits E of the road.
  - Other owners: **C. J. Wooden 20** (with a residence, N); **H. J. Gulick** (centre); **C. Cole 48** (S); Albert Marks (E edge); a **cemetery** on Warren St.
  - **No "Indian Rock" or rapids label.** Note for Ground check: the Consumers Power holding means the river at the old post may have been dammed or flooded after about 1910, so "Indian Rock" may be under a millpond. The Knaggs pin (Phillips 44 ac, N sec 6) is consistent; the bridge village is in E½ sec 6.
