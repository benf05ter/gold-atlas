- **Sec 30, W tier:** E. Bruns, W. Sachtjen, J. Schlimgen, **S. Roth 38**, Mrs. McKenna.
- **Sec 31, NW corner:** **S. Root [Roth] 14** and **M. Roth 80**, with a house mark on the W line by the Madison town line. E. Scholtz is to the SW.

The 1904 plate is closer to the 1907 death and confirms **Town of Burke**. BLM sec 31 T8N R10E: -89.3641..-89.3452, 43.1067..43.1215. **No pin change.**

## City directories (Ancestry coll. 2469)
- **Fergus Falls 1902:** "Trommler Frederick W, res **s end Mill st**" (p.116). F. W. Trombler lived at the S end of (S.) Mill St, with no number. See trombler-jacobson-otter-tail.md.
- **Niles 1904** (St. Joseph-Benton Harbor directory, Niles section): "**Andrew Jackson**, spouse **Flora**, **399 N. 8th**" (AI-indexed; image not opened). On the 1914 Sanborn, N. 8th St numbers in the 200s-300s run N from Ferry, so 399 N. 8th would be just N of the Ferry/8th corner. **Caveat:** our Andrew Jackson died June 1902, so a 1904 entry may be a stale listing, or a son of the same name with wife Flora (the 1902 probate petitioners were William Jackson and Mary McCown). There is also an Andrew E. Jackson, labourer, 1311 N. 12th.
- **Muskegon 1902:** the Ole Larson entries are a cabinetmaker on Denmark Ave, Ole H. Larson (sander/labourer) at 13 Walnut, and an Ole Larson at Fruitport. **No wood-turning shop entry or shop street found.** The existing light-plant pin stands.
- **Lansing 1896 Franklin House:** already placed from the 1898 Sanborn (414 Franklin St E, plats/franklin-house-lansing.md, B+). Not repeated.
- **Sheboygan:**
  - **1904: "Julius Behr, carp[enter], Lyman's Add[ition]"**, which confirms the Lyman's Addition home from the story.
  - **1924:** Julius Behr, labourer, Dillingham Mfg Co. (address field garbled: "Lester Hendershot").
  - **1926:** Route 3 (see behr-sheboygan.md).
  - **No 1930 directory entry found** (he died Feb 1930).

## Branch Co. MI 1915, Bronson Twp (bronson-gold-farm card)
- *Standard Atlas of Branch County*, 1915, p.53 "Map of Bronson Township", **Township 7 South, Range 8 West** (image 28 of 88).
- **"C. N. Phillips Est. 89"**: a N-S strip in the **N part of sec 5**, beside F. H. Rickards et al. 80 (W), Frank Coward 196 and 40 (S), and John Shoffmaster 150.63 (sec 4). This is **Canaris (Canary) Phillips's estate**, the "old Canary Phillips farm" of the 1939 find, still held as an estate in 1915.
- No Kregger yet in 1915 (he bought later).
- **Note:** the plate puts Bronson village in secs 11-12, so this farm is about **2 mi W/NW** of the village, not "2 mi east" as the 1939 article says. The existing pin (N½ sec 5 T7S R8W, 41.8940,-85.2600) **is confirmed** by the plate.
