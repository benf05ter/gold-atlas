## (a) GARNETT, Rivoli Twp, Mercer Co. IL: GARRETT found
- 1914 Mercer & Henderson plat book, image 8 of 61, p.4 "Map of Rivoli Township", **Township 14 North, Range 1 West of the 4th P.M.**
- In **sec 16, N half**, three adjoining N-S 80-acre strips just S of the railroad (Hopewell / "Switch" station): **J. A. Garrett 80**, **E. G. Garrett 80** and **Mrs. E. H. Garrett 80**. Brooks 80 and Lulu McIntyre are E; Elmer Peterson's "Maple Grove" 78 and A. M. Peterson's "Elm Lawn" are S.
- The spelling on the plate is **Garrett**, not Garnett. This is probably the same family (check the story's spelling).
- BLM sec 16 T14N R1W (4th PM): -90.5131..-90.4939, 41.1955..41.2103.
- **Suggested pin: N-centre of sec 16, about 41.206, -90.506, r400.** Confidence B- (surname variant).
