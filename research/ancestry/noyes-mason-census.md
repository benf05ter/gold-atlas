# Frank Noyes household, Mason Co. MI: 1880 and 1900 census (Ancestry, 2026-11-01)

Viewed only.

## 1900 U.S. Census: Custer Twp, Mason Co., MI
- ED 40, sheet 15, enumerated 27-28 June 1900 by John H. Bryant (image 4120242_00096; record 64644172).
- **Dwelling 325: Frank Noyes, head, b. Feb 1860 MI**, father b. NH, mother b. NY. Farmer, **owns farm, mortgaged**. Married 1895 to **Frances L.** (b. May 1874).
  - Daughters Leona B. (b. 1885), Winnie E. (1887), Rita F. (1891).
  - Also in the house: his **mother Sarah J. Packard** (b. May 1821 NY, widowed), boarder Burton Cole (farm labourer) and boarder James Gallagher (b. Ireland, farm labourer).
- **Neighbours on the same sheet, in order:**
  - Elmer Mallory [?] (dwelling 323, farmer)
  - Fred [?] Mills / Miller (324, farmer, b. Sweden)
  - **Frank Noyes (325)**
  - George Wolf (326, farmer)
  - Charles Wolf (327, farmer)
  - Claude C. Foster (farm labourer)
  - Daniel Van Buren (328, farmer)
  - Jay Hager [?] (329, farmer)
  - Scott Ames (330, farmer)
  - Azro [?] French (331)
  - Harry Eastman [?] (332, farmer)
- **Note:** this places the Frank Noyes farm in **Custer Twp** (T18N R16W), not Eden (T17N R16W). The two townships adjoin N-S. Match these neighbours on the 1904 atlas Custer plate: Wolf, Van Buren, Hager, Ames, French, Eastman.

## Addendum: 1910, 1920, 1930 census (index search, Mason Co., 2026-11-01)
| Year | Frank A. Noyes (b. 1860) | Fred S. Noyes (b. c.1871) |
|---|---|---|
| 1910 | **Custer Twp**, head; wife Francis [Frances], daughters Winnie C. and Rita F. | **Eden Twp**, head (indexed "Noyer"); **father Samuel J.** in the household |
| 1920 | not found in Mason Co. under Noyes | **Eden Twp**, head (indexed "Noies"), wife **Rosetta K.** |
| 1930 | not found in Mason Co. | **Ludington**, head, wife Rosetta |

- **Result: "Fred Noyes of Ludington" (1935) is FRED S. Noyes (b. c.1871), not Frank.**
  - Fred was an **Eden Twp** farmer in 1910 and 1920, living with his father **Samuel J.** in 1910. The 1880 "Samuel S. Noyes, b. 1848, Eden" is probably the same Samuel. Fred moved to **Ludington by 1930**, which fits "left the farm about 1925".
  - **So the Eden Twp Noyes family is Samuel J. and his son Fred S.**, distinct from Frank A. Noyes of Custer Twp (b. 1860, mother Sarah J. Packard).
  - "S. J. Noyes" on the 1904 Eden plate is more likely **Samuel J. Noyes**, Fred's father, than Sarah J.
- The 1910/1920 Eden census pages (neighbours, farm owned or rented) were not opened. They can be, to match the 1904 plat.

## 1880 U.S. Census: Eden Twp, Mason Co.
- ED 170, p.19 (475a), 11 June 1880 (image 4241734-00414; record 31473861).
- **Samuel S. Noyes**, 32, b. NY (parents b. NH), divorced, brick mason. Dwelling 135, the first entry on the page.
  - Next comes Henry [?] Ober and a long list of boarders (a lumber or shingle camp crew).
- No Frank Noyes appears in Mason Co. in 1880. Frank (b. 1860) would then be 20; not found under Noyes in the county.
- Samuel S. is probably a brother or relative: both have a father born in NH.
