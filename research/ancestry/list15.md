13. **GB "Heckes", MN, Jun-Dec 1905:** 2 hits, both **Minneapolis Journal, Tue 20 Jun 1905, p.12** (the same story, which appears to be the original rather than a follow-up). "Haunted House at Byronville the Scene of the Operations / Visitor Expected to Find $15,000 Which, by the Theory of an Old Resident, May Have Been Cached in Early Days by Thieves." New Richland, 20 Jun: a stranger from **Indiana** boarded at **H. Heckes'** farm near **Byronville** and dug for treasure "near a vacant house upon one of Mr. Heckes' farms" (the house had a ghost legend). He dug for half a day with "peculiar signs", hole after hole within a few rods, "but **nothing that resembled a treasure was unearthed**"; several more days of searching gave no different result. **Outcome: not found.** No later follow-up in Jun-Dec 1905.
14. **GB "Wabeno bank recovered", WI, Jul 1921 - Dec 1922:** 39 hits, mostly noise (ads; a drowning). Useful: **Milwaukee Evening Sentinel, Wed 24 Aug 1921, p.2**, "Cashier Identifies Yaeger as Man Who Robbed Wabeno Bank" (Crandon): Chalmer Yaeger and Lawrence Lesperance faced preliminary trial "charged with robbing the **Wabeno State bank of $32,000 in cash and securities** in July". Bail was set at $20,000 each; Willis Lesperance was in jail and "Frenchie" Yaeger was out on $2,500 bail as an accessory; cashier **Ross Richardson** identified Yaeger. **No recovery amount found**; the top hit (Northern Wis. Advertiser, 21 Jul 1922 p.2) is only a bank ad and a drowning item. The remaining hits (Eagle River Review 5 Aug and 30 Dec 1921; Kenosha 25 Aug 1921; Advertiser 13 Jan and 29 Dec 1922) were not opened.

17. **Allouez sundial-compass:** *Collections of the State Historical Society of Wisconsin* v.16 (1902; HathiTrust uva.x030227625), plate facing p.64 (scan #95): "**Combined Bronze Sun-Dial and Compass.
