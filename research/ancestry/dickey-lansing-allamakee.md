# Mrs. Evelina M. ("Pike") Dickey, Lansing, Allamakee Co. IA (found dead Jan 1902): will (Ancestry, 2026-11-01)

**Collection:** *Iowa, U.S., Wills and Probate Records, 1758-1997* (coll. 9064), Allamakee County, "Will Record, 1855-1932". Viewed only.
- Index (image 007594022_00202): "Dickey, Evelina M. 238-9".
- **Will record pp. 238-239** (image 007594022_00456; Ancestry record 1383378).

## The will (typed transcript in the will book)
- **Dated 2 December 1893.** "I, Mrs. Evelina M. Dickey, of the City of Lansing and county of Allamakee and State of Iowa ..."
- **Admitted to probate 15 April 1902** (Ellison Orr, clerk; J. H. Ludeking, deputy).
- **Key clauses** (plain transcription; some words faint):
  - First: debts.
  - Second: the residue of the estate, real and personal, "as follows".
  - Third: "I give to my husband **Jacob Dickey** now in the **Soldiers Home in Milwaukee** Wisconsin the sum of One dollar ..."
  - "... to the **Methodist Church** situated Lot __, Block 20 in the city of Lansing ... to be used solely for repairing and decorating the interior of said church."
  - "2nd. The next One Hundred dollars to be given to **Aaron Young** sexton of our family graveyard ... to keep the wall of said graveyard in good repair which is situated on **Aaron Young's Farm** which is situated about **two and one half miles from Ft. Atkinson, Iowa**."
  - "3rd. I give to my **neice Grace Hoover**, Aaron Young's daughter, all my silver ware and fifty dollars in money."
  - "4th. I give devise and bequeath to the **Good Templars Lodge of Iowa** ... all of my **certain farm consisting of more than eighty acres situated in Wellington Illinois** and recorded in Iroquois [?] Ill. to E. M. Pike."
  - "5th. All the rest residue and remainder ... to my **nephew John Young, son of Aaron Young, now of New Hampton Iowa**, and certain things in the house to be given to the persons that have names marked thereon ..."
  - A clause about "Uncle Pember [?] Conkling of the state of New Hampshire".
  - "Fifth. I hereby appoint my **nephew Frank Young of Ft. Atkinson, Winneshiek Co., Iowa** to be the executor ..."
  - Witnesses: G. L. Page and Herman Haas, of Lansing.
  - **Codicil** (signed E. M. Dickey): revokes [the gift?] of "One Hundred Dollars I gave to the Methodist ... Lansing".

## Significance
- This confirms "Mrs. Pike Dickey" is **Evelina M. Dickey** (née Pike? The Illinois land is "recorded ... to E. M. Pike"). She lived in the City of Lansing.
- The will names no money box. The estate (and the "box containing a large sum of money" found near her body, Jan 1902) went to the residuary heir, nephew John Young, through executor Frank Young.
- The estate file (inventory) is in the Allamakee district court probate files. It is not indexed here; order it by the will-record citation (Will Record p.238-9, admitted 15 Apr 1902).
