# George Vollmer, Stillwater, Washington Co. MN (d. Oct 1899; will contest): will (Ancestry, 2026-11-01)

**Collection:** *Minnesota, U.S., Wills and Probate Records, 1801-1925* (coll. 9070), Washington Co., "Wills, Vol C, 1892-1901", p.506 (image 004415925_00220; record 2103461). Viewed only.

- **Will dated 21 Dec 1898.** "I, George Vollmer of the City of Stillwater, Washington County, Minnesota ..."
- **Second:** five dollars each to his daughters:
  - Dorothea Anna Catharine Christianson
  - Sophia Getznick [?]
  - Elizabeth Steier [?]
  - Annie Gabel
  - Mary Young [?]
- **Third:** "unto my sons Emanuel Vollmer and George D. J. Vollmer, share and share alike, all of the rest, residue and remainder of my estate, whether personal, real estate or mixed property ..."
- **Fourth:** son George D. J. Vollmer is executor.
- **Significance:** daughters cut to $5 each, with everything to the two sons. That explains the will contest after his death in Oct 1899.
- No house address or inventory on this page. The Stillwater house lot needs the probate file or a city directory.
