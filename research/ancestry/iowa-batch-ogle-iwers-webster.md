## #83 Henry Iwers, Cedar Co. (d. 1935)
- **Probate no. 6598**, Cedar Co. Will Record p.358 (record 1952918, image 007598412_00779).
- "I, Henry Iwers, residing in **Inland Township, Cedar County**, Iowa ..."
- "All remaining property, wherever located ... real, personal or mixed, I give and bequeath to **Louis Voss and Margaret Voss, his wife**." Louis Voss is executor.
- Made at Davenport in 1935. The will was opened and filed 4 June 1936 and admitted to probate 21 Jan 1937.
- Also found: **Hans D. Iwers** (probate 30 Jun 1898, Cedar), with a will, administration, inventory and petition (record 1016756), probably the father.
- **Not read:** the inventory, land description and any John Iwers record.

- **Ogle:** the Warren Co. probate card index (Abel-Putz, images around 1891-1896) has cards for Henry Ogle (estate and will, 1894, case 1405), Nillie and Valentine Ogle (guardianship, 1872) and others, but **no card for F. M. Ogle, case 2387**, in the cards read.
  - The 1907 case-file box (around "Box 93o-") is unindexed; a page sample in Box 91n-93o was older files.
  - So the case 2387 inventory was not reached. **To order:** Warren Co. probate case 2387 (1907).
- **Webster (Hardin):** image 007595057_01078 is the **administrator's bond**, 26 Aug 1897. C. McKeen Duren is administrator of **James H. Webster, late of Hardin County, deceased, who died intestate**. Bond $6,000, with sureties W. J. Moir, Ezra Nuckolls and Chas. Moser.
  - A $6,000 bond implies an estate worth about $3,000.
  - The inventory and land are not in this bond book.
- **Hans D. Iwers** (Cedar, record 1016756, 34 images). His will (images 01050-01052) reads:
  - "I, Hans D. Iwers of **Farmington Township**, Cedar County, Iowa ..."
  - Part III: to his wife, **$4,000 in cash or its equivalent**, plus land including the NW¼ of sec 35 and the SW¼ of [sec ...].
  - **Part IV, to son Henry Iwers:** the NW¼ of **sec 36, T80N R1W** 5th P.M., and the NE¼ of the SE¼ of the SE¼ of sec 27, T80N R2W.
  - **Part V, to son William:** the SW¼ of sec 35 and the S½ SE¼ of sec 27, T80N R1W; also the W½ of the NE¼ SW¼ of sec 27, T80N R2W.
  - **Part VI, to son John Iwers:** the SE¼ of section [continued on the next page, not read].
  - The notice of probate was published in the *Tipton Advertiser*, Sept-Oct 1898. Special administrators: John Iwers and O. H. Snoke.
  - The inventory (1898) is the folded sheet at images 01069-01070; its list was not legible in this pass.
  - **Placement:** in 1898 **Henry Iwers held the NW¼ of sec 36, T80N R1W** (the Farmington/Inland area, Cedar Co.). That is the likely Iwers brothers' farm of the 1935-36 story.
