## #89 Christopher Meyer, Allamakee Co. (legend farm W of New Albin)
- **Will:** Allamakee Will Record pp. 45-46 (image 007594022_00799; record 1384370). Index probate date 26 Mar 1914.
- "I, Christopher Meyer of **Union City Township**, Allamakee County, Iowa ..."
  - The executor is to sell all real estate at public auction and convert everything to cash.
  - The legatee list continues on p.46 (not read). The index names children Catherina Meyer, Dorothea Kohlmeier and Margaretha Meyer; sister Dorothea Kuhl (husband John Kuhl); nephew Heinrich Kruse; niece Dorothea Meyer and Herman Meyer.
- **No land description** on p.45. That confirms the township (Union City Twp, the next township W of New Albin's Iowa Twp) but not the section.
