## FRENCH LAKE: the text and the plat both point to SPRING GROVE (T1N R9E), not Jefferson
- **History of Green County (1884) p.1050:** the French Lake passage sits under **"CHAPTER XLI. TOWN OF SPRING GROVE"**. That is the last chapter heading before the passage, and no other town heading comes between them. Quote: "French Lake was born in Virginia, in 1807 … and settled in this town on section 7, in 1839, having selected and bought 760 acres".
- **Ancestry 1127, Green 1873, viewer p.9 (image 31617_072128-00300), "Spring Grove, Township 1 North, Range 9 East":**
  - **"F. Lake 400" fills sec 8 W / NW.**
  - **"Res. F. Lake" (his residence) is marked at the S edge of sec 7/8** by the creek.
  - **"F. Lake 83.74"** is in N sec 5.
  - That totals about 480+ ac in the 1873 name, consistent with his 760 ac.
- **In Jefferson Twp sec 7 (list41), the 1873 owners included no Lake.** So the sec 7 reading belongs to Spring Grove, not Jefferson. The ruling "Juda lies in Jefferson Twp" should be re-checked; Juda village is in Spring Grove.
- **BLM: sec 7 T1N R9E = 42.5595-42.5745 N, -89.4846 to -89.4650 W; sec 8 = 42.5594-42.5741 N, -89.4656 to -89.4459 W.**
