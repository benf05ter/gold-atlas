## #14 GALLUP red oak (Washington Co IA): wording and plat
- **Burrell 1909, CLAY chapter, p.~139-140:** "George Gallup … sold his place in Clay … on the gallows said he had buried five thousand dollars at the foot of a red oak tree in the northwest corner of section 16 in Iowa".
  - "In Iowa" most likely means the state, with Clay Twp implied by the chapter. **Iowa Twp** (T77N R5-7W, TOC) is the other reading.
- **Ancestry 1127, Washington 1906, viewer p.21 of 126 (image 31617_072148-00043), "Clay", T74N R9W, atlas p.39.** The Skunk River forms the N edge.
  - **Sec 16** is crowded with small lots in the "Black Hawk" school-district area.
  - **The NW part of sec 16 is "Morgan" land (… Morgan; C. B. Morgan nearby)**, with Cyrus Morgan and others; Freeman / Gowey / Alwood are along the river.
- **BLM (via the Clay Twp centroid): sec 16 T74N R9W = 41.2061-41.2211 N, -91.9071 to -91.8876 W.**
- **Pin: NW corner of sec 16, Clay Twp, about 41.2211,-91.9071, r200, D.**

## #19 KNAGGS BRIDGE: ROBERTS mill found (1875)
- Knaggs Bridge geocodes to **sec 6 T5N R4E, Burns Twp, Shiawassee Co (42.861,-84.031)**. In 1875 this was the hamlet **"BURNS" P.O.**, where Grand River St crosses the Shiawassee.
- **Ancestry 1127, Shiawassee 1875:** Burns Twp plate at viewer p.54 (image 31617_072125-00250, atlas p.95); "Burns" village plat at viewer p.56 (image 31617_072125-00252, atlas p.98).
- **The village plat shows a DAM across the Shiawassee just N (upstream) of the Grand River St bridge.** On the **NE bank above the dam it shows "Saw Mill" and "G. Mill" (grist mill), labelled "ROBERTS & MARSHALL".** J. J. Gaylord is on the SE bank below the bridge; L. Newman, A. Drum, E. B. Allen and A. Bigelow are on the W side; N. G. Philips 44.10 a. is N.
- That matches the Owosso Times 1897-12-31 note ("Roberts Bros. … mill property at Knaggs Bridge").
- **Pin: mill site, NE bank just above the Grand River (Cole Rd) bridge, about 42.861,-84.031, r150, B** for the mill site. The trading-post site S of the bridge is not labelled on the 1875 plat.

## #17 HECKES / PHILBROOK farm, Byron Twp (Waseca): PLACED (1896)
- **Ancestry 1127, Waseca 1896, viewer p.15 of 55 (image 31617_072139-00037), "Byron Township", T105N R23W, atlas p.29.**
- **"Roscoe Philbrook 160" = NE¼ sec 11**, with a building mark. Neighbours: Delbert Weed (sec 12 NW), Frank Winegar (sec 11 SE), William Brisbane (sec 11 SW), R. Stewart (sec 10 E).

- Per the New Richland Star 1905-06-22 (via PC a), Heckes then "owns the Philbrook farm", with the dig in the grove near the old house. So the dig site is the **Philbrook quarter, NE¼ sec 11**, adjoining Heckes's own NE¼ sec 14.
- **BLM: sec 11 T105N R23W = 43.9063-43.9208 N, -93.5670 to -93.5466 W; sec 14 = 43.8918-43.9063 N.**
- **Pin: Philbrook farm, NE¼ sec 11, about 43.917,-93.552, r300, B** (the house mark lies within the quarter). Alternative: Heckes home quarter, NE¼ sec 14, about 43.903,-93.552.

## #11 BRANSTAD homestead, Leland (Winnebago IA 1913): PLACED
- **Ancestry 1127, Winnebago 1913 (Anderson), viewer p.18 of 45 (image 31617_072148-00311), "Forest" Twp, T98N R24W, atlas p.23.**
- The Leland village plat (hatched) is in the NE of the township, on the sec 1 / sec 2 line. The Minneapolis & St. Louis RR and the creek run through it.
- **"E. F. Branstad 120" is in the E part of sec 1**, between the village and the Mount Valley/Forest City Twp line (R23W), E of the creek.

- This matches the Des Moines Register 1922-01-08 note (homestead "about a mile east of Leland", gold in an old shed).
- **BLM: sec 1 T98N R24W = 43.3280-43.3423 N, -93.6370 to -93.6171 W** (Leland's geocode 43.337,-93.635 is on its W edge).
- **Pin: E. F. Branstad 120, E½ sec 1, about 43.335,-93.622, r300, B.**
