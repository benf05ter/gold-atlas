# HECKES, Byronville (Waseca Co. MN, dig 1905): Ancestry census (PC b, 2026-11-02)

Lead: an Indiana stranger boarded with **Herman Heckes**, "northwest of town", who "owns the Philbrook farm". The stranger dug in the grove near the "haunted" vacant house on one of Heckes' farms in the Byronville neighbourhood, looking for $15,000. Source notes: research/newspapers-com/treasure-list-t13-t21.md and pc-a-asks-results.md. Viewed only.

## 1900 census
- **Herman "Hecker"** [= Heckes], **Byron Twp, Waseca Co.**, ED 161, sheet 2, dwelling 32 (Ancestry 7602, record 27349808).
  - Age 40, b. Sep 1859 Wisconsin. Wife Anna (b. 1865 IL) and children George A., William I., Florence M., Ruth M., Alice V., Almer F., Esther E. and Senard C. (b. 1885-1899, all MN).
  - **Farmer, OWNS his farm, mortgaged.**
  - **Neighbours, in order on the sheet:** Henry C. Siffing, William F. Winnegs, John P. Brisband, then **Herman Hecker**, then Walter Brisband, Fred Wymmner, Winfield Mimer [Miner?], Frank W. Winnegar, Rudolph M. Brese, Frank Weekworth, Herman Hass and John Hinken.
- There is also **Henryjette Heckes** (b. May 1836 Germany, head), New Richland, Waseca Co., possibly his mother.

## 1896 Standard Atlas of Waseca County (UMedia, read 2026-11-02)
- Source: UMedia "Minnesota County Maps, Plat Books, and Atlases", *Standard Atlas of Waseca County* (1896), **p.29 "Map of Byron Township"**. The IIIF image is https://cdm16022.contentdm.oclc.org/iiif/2/p16022coll231:1111/full/1500,/0/default.jpg (compound object 1150). It is reachable view-only, with no wall.
- **Caption: "Township 105 North ... Range 23 West of the 5th P.M."** BLM T105N R23W matches the real Byron Twp (Nominatim centroid 43.8916, -93.5871).
- **"H. Heckes 160"** is in the **NE¼ of sec 14**, with a **house mark at the NW corner of the tract** (and a second building dot near the "14" at the section centre).
- **"Roscoe Philbrook 160"** is in the **SE¼ of sec 14**, directly S of Heckes and adjoining. A second **Roscoe Philbrook 160** is in the **NE¼ of sec 11**, with Frank Winegar 160 (SE¼ sec 11) between them.
- **Neighbours match the 1900 census:** John Brisbane 160 and W. S. Miner 80 (W½ sec 14), William Brisbane 80 and Chas. S. Jones (sec 11), W. R. Brisbane (sec 2), Frank Winegar (sec 11), B. F. Weed and F. A. Johnston (sec 13), Asa Francis (S sec 13).
- **BLM sec 14 T105N R23W:** -93.5673..-93.5467, 43.8918..43.9063. Sec 11: -93.5670..-93.5466, 43.9063..43.9208.
- **Reading:** by 1905 Heckes "owns the Philbrook farm". The adjoining Philbrook SE¼ sec 14 is the obvious candidate: a second farm with its own (later vacant) house and grove, next to his home quarter.
- **Suggested pins:**
  - **Philbrook farm (dig site candidate):** SE¼ sec 14, about **43.8955, -93.5520, r400**. Confidence C (plat plus census; which Philbrook quarter is inferred).
  - **Heckes home quarter:** house about 43.9055, -93.5560 (NW corner of NE¼ sec 14).
  - Alternative: the Philbrook NE¼ sec 11, about 43.9170, -93.5520.

## Placeable?
- **Township yes: Byron Twp** (the Byronville neighbourhood). The farm can be placed on a 1890s-1910s Waseca plat by Hecke(s), Philbrook or Brisband in Byron Twp.
- **No Waseca county atlas with owners is on archive.org.** There are only the 1874 Andreas state-atlas sheets (a county map and village plans). UMedia and other plat sources are outside today's allowed sites, so this goes to the **Sunday list**: read the Byron Twp plate (c.1896 or 1906 Waseca plat book) for Heckes or Philbrook land, a second farm with a vacant house, and the Brisband/Winnegar neighbours.
- "Northwest of town" in the story is relative to Waseca city or Janesville. Byron Twp is in the NE of Waseca Co., so "town" may be Waseca. Check against the plate.
