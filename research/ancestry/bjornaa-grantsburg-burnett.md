# Ole J. Johnson (Bjornaa), Grantsburg, Burnett Co. WI (d. 3 Feb 1906): probate (Ancestry, 2026-11-01)

**Collection:** *Wisconsin, U.S., Wills and Probate Records, 1800-1987* (coll. 9088), Burnett County, "Probate Estate Files, 85-139, 1905-1907".
- **Case no. 110.** Ancestry record 2189746; 111 images, starting at image 007354810_01041.
- A second index entry (record 2190705, 29 Dec 1906) covers the closing and guardianship papers.
- Viewed only.

## Administration
- **Order for hearing** (image 01044, 3 Apr 1906):
  - Petition of **Martin Johnson, a son**: Ole J. Johnson, inhabitant of the Town of Grantsburg, residing at the town of Grantsburg, **died intestate on 3 February 1906**.
  - Administration was asked for **Andrew Paulson**. The hearing was set for the 1st Tuesday of May 1906, with notice in the *Burnett County Sentinel*.
  - Judge F. G. Dahlberg.
- **Petition** filed 4 Mar 1906 (image 01043), recorded Vol 3 p.163.

## INVENTORY: cash found hidden in two houses (images 01064-01066)
- Inventory and appraisal by **Andrew Paulson, administrator**, with appraisers Christian Olson and Elias Peterson. Dated **21 May 1906**, filed 21 May 1906, recorded Vol 3 pp.168-9.
- **"Town of Grantsburg, Section 32, Town 38, Range 18"** (the cabin where he lived):
  - tools, axes, stove, dishes, clothing and "one piece of buckskin"
  - **"Cash found in a Pocket Book $2.03"**
  - **"Cash found in an Envelope in the Bed $8.00"**
  - **"Cash found in a small tin box under the stove $30.00"**
- **"Town of Anderson, Section 1-37-19"** (the farm, with a dwelling house and granary):
  - **"Cash found in a Trunk upstairs in dwelling house $70.00"**
  - **"Cash found in a small blue trunk upstairs ... in the Granary $675.00"**
  - 3 silver spoons
  - **"Cash found in [a box?] upstairs ... the granary $472.00"**
  - **"Total cash found Twelve Hundred Fifty Seven 03/100 $1257.03"**
- Page 2 (image 01065), **Town of Anderson Sec. 1-37-19, in the dwelling house and granary:** furniture, a spinning wheel, 76 bu. wheat, 75 bu. oats, farm tools and harness. Notes due from Elias Peterson and Jonas Trolin. The running total reaches about $1,378.
- **Distribution:** receipt dated Grantsburg, 29 Dec 1906 (image 01068). Ida Johnson, "one of the heirs", received $345.84 as her full share of the personal property.

## Significance
- **The hidden money was found and inventoried: $1,257.03 in cash**, mostly in trunks and a box in the **granary and upstairs of the Anderson farm house**, with small sums at the Grantsburg cabin.
- **Two places:**
  - **Grantsburg Twp, sec 32 T38N R18W** (his residence and cabin)
  - **Anderson Twp, sec 1 T37N R19W** (farm, dwelling and granary)
- Real estate is not listed on these pages; images 31-111 (accounts and orders) were not read.
