# Michael Tweit (Twite), Vivian Twp, Waseca Co. MN (d. 12 Jun 1927): family probate (Ancestry, 2026-11-01)

**Collection:** *Minnesota, U.S., Wills and Probate Records, 1801-1925* (coll. 9070). Viewed only.
- **Michael's own 1927 estate is outside this collection**, which ends in 1925. A Waseca Co. Tweit name search found none.
- The Tweit entries are all Blue Earth Co., for the parents Thor (probate 26 Dec 1901) and Kristie Tweit (will 1905, probate Mar 1906), plus Belle (1910).

## Kristie Tweit's will (Blue Earth Co. Wills Vol C, 1899-1906, p.571; image 004406601_00265)
- Dated 11 Aug 1905; **admitted to probate 10 Mar 1906**.
- "I, Kristie Tweit of the town of Medo, Blue Earth County, Minn., being now of the age of 77 years ..."
- **2nd:** "to the following of my children **Michael Tweit** and **Belle Shannahan** and my grand daughter **Esther Raske** ... all my real estate ... to each of them an undivided 1/3 interest."
- **3rd:** each of them is to pay "my daughter **Thea Darrah**" $266.66⅔, a total of $800, as a lien on the real estate.
- **4th:** personal property to Michael, Belle, Thea and Esther, share and share alike.
- **5th:** "my son Michael as the sole executor". She signed by mark.
- **Significance:** this gives Michael an undivided third of the parents' **Medo Twp (Blue Earth Co.)** land from 1906. The land description is not in the will.
- The **"Mamie Shannahan"** heir named in the Court/probate notes fits Belle Shannahan's family.
- Thor Tweit's 1901 estate (Blue Earth Will Record C p.568, per Court/probate) is on the page just before; not read here.
