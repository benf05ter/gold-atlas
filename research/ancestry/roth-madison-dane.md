# Mathew Roth, Dane Co. WI (d. 1907; "$3000 miser hid"): probate (Ancestry, 2026-11-01)

**Collection:** *Wisconsin, U.S., Wills and Probate Records, 1800-1987* (coll. 9088), Dane Co., "Probate Records, Box 303 ... Box 305".
- **File no. 304** (on the inventory jacket); Ancestry record 282697; 61 images, starting at image 007704372_01327. Viewed only.
- **Will dated 10 Jan 1900; probate 9 Oct 1907.**
- Persons listed: sons Stephen, Mathew and Charles J. (deceased by 1907); daughters Anna and Mary; son-in-law **Lawrence G. Schlinger (executor)**.

## Inventory (images 01352-01353)
- Inventory and appraisal by **L. G. Schlinger, executor**, with appraisers A. Roth and H. Krugman, 6 Dec 1907. Filed 6 Dec 1907 (A. G. Zimmerman, judge).
- **Jacket totals: Real (blank), Personal $83.50.** So the estate inventoried only **$83.50** in personal property and no real estate.
- **Reading:** the $3,000 the 1907 papers said the recluse hid does **not** appear in the estate. That fits the story that it was never found, or that it was found and not reported to the court.
- **Guardianship** (image 01354, 5 Nov 1907): Rufus B. Smith, of Madison, was guardian ad litem for Mary, Eleanora, Amelia and Josephine Roth, **children of Charles J. Roth, a deceased son of Mathew Roth**.

## Not read
- The will text (images 16-19), the accounts and the land description, if any. The inventory has no real estate, so he likely owned no land at death.
