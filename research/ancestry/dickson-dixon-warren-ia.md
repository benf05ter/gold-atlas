# Andrew J. Dixon (Dickson), Palmyra Twp, Warren Co. IA (d. 20 Aug 1909): probate (Ancestry, 2026-11-01)

**Collection:** *Iowa, U.S., Wills and Probate Records, 1758-1997* (coll. 9064), Warren County, "Probate Case Files, Box 13x ... 1876-1909". Viewed only.
- **Case no. 2574**, probate date 30 Aug 1909. Ancestry record 581863; 83 images, starting at image 007597040_00978.

## Petition for letters of administration (image 980)
- **Petitioner:** Harriet E. Dixon, widow, P.O. Carlisle, Iowa (R.F.D.).
- **Andrew J. Dixon died in Palmyra Township, Warren Co., on or about 20 Aug 1909, leaving no last will** "so far as known".
- **Heirs:**
  - Young Dixon, brother, about 75, Tar River, North Carolina
  - Margaret Rhew, niece (daughter of Washington Dixon, a brother of Andrew J., deceased), North Carolina
  - Henry Dixon, son of ___ Dixon, a brother (deceased)
  - heirs of Caroline Garrett, deceased, a sister ("names and addresses I am now unable to give")
- **Real estate:**
  - "100 a. of land in" sec 23, T77, R23 ("a better description I cannot now give")
  - W½ SW¼ sec 25, T77 R23
  - NE SE sec 26
  - NW NW sec 36
  - Value about $2,000.
- **Administrator:** F. P. Smith.

## SUPPLEMENTAL INVENTORY: the buried gold was found (images 986-989)
- "(Supplemental) Inventory" of the personal effects of Andrew J. Dixon, by F. P. Smith, Administrator, case 2574.
- **Schedule "B"** (property mentioned as general assets of the deceased; cash on hand at time of decease):
  - **"1909 Sept 18 Gold found on premises of deceased and now in bank to my credit as Admr. ... $10,005.00"**
- Sworn by F. P. Smith on 22 Sept 1909 before A. V. Proudfoot, Notary Public. Filed 23 Sept 1909 (clerk J. W. ___land).
- Schedules A and C are blank.

## Significance
- **F for the money.** About $10,005 in gold was recovered on the farm on 18 Sept 1909, a month after his death, and was banked by the administrator.
- The farm is in **T77N R23W (Palmyra Twp)**, secs 23, 25, 26 and 36. The house is most likely on the 100 acres in sec 23.
- The later file images (account, sale and other papers, images 17-83) were not read. They may show the final distribution and land sale.
