## Enrichments to existing pins
- **Davenport murder 1845 (tw-il-arsenal-island-davenport-1845 and Paw Paw Grove banditti):** Bonney, The Banditti of the Prairies (1850; archive.org bandittiofprairi00bonn, OCR read) says Fox, John and Aaron Long and Birch "buried the watch and money in the old fellow's [Grant Redden's] wheat field" near Nauvoo/Montrose after robbing Col. Davenport; and Fox kept "about two thousand dollars secreted in the bluffs on the Des Moines River, in Iowa, concealed in four separate bottles". Redden's farm and the Des Moines bluffs are below the Mahaska-Wapello line: add as card text, and the two caches go to held-leads.md (out of region).
- **Fort St. Joseph, Niles:** forum legend that settlers sealed their money in a cannon and sank it in the St. Joseph River off Bond Street, never recovered (https://www.treasurenet.com/threads/treasure-in-cannon-in-st-joe-river-michigan.13769/). Card line, D; river bottom = state bottomland, look-only.
- **Dillinger's suitcase (Little Bohemia):** a forum author cites an FBI supplemental report of 2-3 May 1935 placing Dillinger with "a suitcase full of money" in Fort Wayne ten days after the Little Bohemia fight, which argues the suitcase was not buried there (https://www.treasurenet.com/threads/new-info-on-dillingers-little-bohemia-cache.25431/). Card line: "an FBI report puts the suitcase in Indiana afterwards".
