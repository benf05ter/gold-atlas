# Noyes plat check: Eden Twp, Mason Co., MI (2026-10-31)

**Source:** 1904 *Standard Atlas of Mason County*, Eden Township (T17N R16W).
- archive.org 3927862.0001.001.umich.edu, **leaf 53** (printed p.49): https://archive.org/details/3927862.0001.001.umich.edu/page/n53/mode/1up

## S. J. Noyes: 80 acres straddling the sec 5 / sec 6 line, in the N part of the township
- **40 ac = SW¼ of NW¼, sec 5** (the third tier down, below the fractional N row of lots)
- **40 ac = SE¼ of NE¼, sec 6** (directly W, across the section line)
- **House mark: "Res"** at the NW corner of the sec 5 forty, right beside the 5/6 section line, about 0.3 mi S of the N township line (the Custer twp line).

**Neighbours:**
- E. Lambert 79.04 (N of the house, sec 5)
- Ella Griswold 40 (E)
- Sydney Baker 40 (S, sec 5)
- I. Barton 39.65 (N, sec 6)
- Paul Oman 80 (S, sec 6), which has two "Spg" (springs) marks
- Dockery Lake is in E sec 5

**Other names:**
- No other Noyes in secs 4, 5 or 6.
- No Zukas in 1904; he came later.

## Location (rough, from the PLSS)
- **House ≈ 43.904, -86.272** (±1 km, because of Michigan correction-line offsets).
- That is about 3.5 mi S of Scottville, just S of the Custer/Eden line.
- Check against a modern map: the house sits by a road on the sec 5/6 line.

## 1930s plat check (Treasure list item 3, 2026-11-01)
- No 1930s Mason Co. plat online: archive.org (Mason County plat/atlas: only other states' Mason counties) and HathiTrust catalog ("Mason County Michigan plat book": no Mason volume) came up empty. The 1904 Standard Atlas stays the only plat. Sunday list.
