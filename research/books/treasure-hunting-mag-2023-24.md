# Treasure Hunting magazine (UK), Sept 2023 - Dec 2024: Midwest sweep

**Source:** *Treasure Hunting* (UK monthly, est. 1977), Greenlight Publishing, Ipswich IP3 9QN; editor Julian Evan-Hart; ISSN 0140 4539; www.treasurehunting.co.uk. Ten issues uploaded by Neb 2026-11-02: Sep 2023, Nov 2023, Dec 2023, Jan 2024, Feb 2024, Mar 2024, Apr 2024, Jun 2024, Sep 2024, Dec 2024. (Mar 2024 and Nov 2023 were also uploaded twice as byte-identical "… 2.pdf" copies.)

**This is the British magazine, not the US *Lost Treasure* / *Western & Eastern Treasures*.** Content is UK detecting: Roman/Saxon/medieval/post-medieval finds, hoards under the Treasure Act, PAS recording, rally reports, detector field tests, dealer adverts.

**Method:** June 2024 had a text layer (pdftotext). The other nine were image-only scans; every page was OCR'd (pdftoppm 200 dpi gray + tesseract 5.3, eng) and searched for Wisconsin, Minnesota, Michigan, Iowa, Illinois, Chicago, Milwaukee, Detroit, Minneapolis, Midwest, Great Lakes, Superior, Mississippi, America(n), USA, U.S., United States, plus every US mention read in context.

## Result: no WI / MN / MI / IA / IL content in any issue

No story, letter, find report, legend or period-source citation touches the five states. **No new leads; nothing appended to leads.tsv (no "thmag" rows).** All US mentions found are listed below so nobody re-reads these issues.
