# WFMK post: "Michigan Mystery: Is There Treasure Still Buried on Higgins Lake?"

- URL: https://99wfmk.com/hermithiggins2018/ (sent by Neb, 2026-11-02)
- Author: John Robinson (WFMK radio, Lansing). Published March 15, 2018. Read view-only; no overlays needed closing.

## Story (summary)

- **Place:** **Flynn Island** in **Higgins Lake, Roscommon Co, MI**, also called "Hermit Island" or "Treasure Island". The post calls it a small island of about 30 acres, now covered with vacation homes. It names no shore point; the island itself is the site.
- **Hermit:** **Israel "Ezra" Porter Pritchard**, "the Hermit of Higgins Lake". He lived alone on the island in an old shack and dugout for about 20 years, from the 1880s to 1902.
- **Backstory:**
  - 1873: a shotgun he was handling accidentally went off and wounded his wife Susan, who was carrying twins. The twins were stillborn, her leg was amputated, and she died after the operation. Rumour said he hid on the island to escape a murder accusation.
  - 1885: his son Edwin was shot in the head by an ex-logger.
- **Treasure claim:** he was said to have made a small fortune as a Civil War **bounty jumper**, paid $300-$500 per enlistment for three years in seven states. Locals believe the unspent money is buried on the island. His grandson (on treasurenet.com) denies the bounty-jumping.
- **Amount:** not stated; only "a considerable amount" or "a small fortune".
- **Death:** body found in the dugout in **1902**; buried in the Potter's Field section of **Roscommon Cemetery**. "Only had stubs for fingers" (frostbite).
