# True Tales of Buried Treasure: Midwest titles

Read 2026-11-02, look-only. No cart, no sign-up, no purchase.

- Main site: https://truetalesofburiedtreasure.com/. It is a shop and archive of old treasure magazines: Long John Latham's Lost Treasure, Treasure World and True Treasure; Lost Treasure; Saga's Treasure specials; Treasure; Treasure Facts; Treasure Search/Found; Treasure Trails of the Old West; Western & Eastern Treasures.
- The site has **no free story posts**. "Search stories by region" leads to a Sellfy store (https://true-tales-of-buried-treasure.sellfy.store/) that sells single-article PDFs, $1-10 each.
- The coordinator's brief named W. C. Jameson. No author is shown on the listing pages; they give titles and prices only.
- Store pages show **titles only**, so there's no place, amount or cited source to log. Nothing went to leads.tsv. The titles marked ★ are named legends worth checking against our files, and only the PDF (paid) would give details.
