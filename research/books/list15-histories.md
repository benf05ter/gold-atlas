# List 15 D: county histories (PC b, 2026-11-02; archive.org OCR, read only)

## 18. Dickinson Co IA: R. A. Smith, *A History of Dickinson County, Iowa* (1902; archive.org historyofdickins00smit)
- **Marble cabin site:** "In September [1856] a Mr. Marble, from Linn County, … settled upon the **west bank of Spirit Lake in the grove now owned by J. S. Polk**. This grove was for years known as **Marble Grove**" (about p.38). The cabin was "in the grove **on the ridge between Spirit Lake and Marble Lake**" (about p.41). On 11 Mar 1857 the Indians camped "some distance north of Marble's house"; Marble was shot after a trade and shooting match, and Mrs. Marble was taken (p.69-70). The carved "hieroglyphic" ash tree stood "a little way to the **southeast of the door of the Marble cabin**" (p.70).
- **No mention of Marble's gold, money or any cache** in the 1902 history. (Laird's Mrs. Bergman letter gives the $1,500 gold story, and the Sharp citation gives $1,000 taken from the body.)
- **Placement:** the 1902 owner of the Marble Grove was **J. S. Polk** (Laird's 1927 source says the "Birl Maish farm"). The site is on the isthmus/ridge between Spirit Lake (W shore) and Marble Lake. Grade B for the cabin area once a plat confirms Polk's tract.
- *History of Emmet and Dickinson Counties* (1917, vol.1): no Marble gold.
