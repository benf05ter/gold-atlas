**Source:** 1920s *Wisconsin Farmer's Plat Book of Brown County* (WHS W0175WJ), Eaton T23N R22E: https://explore.wishistory.org/AssetLink/lc1c7pcgg51052l051i51pnfs1gtq4vw.jpg
- This is a low-resolution scan (1956 px). It shows secs 13-36.

**Read by eye: no "Swantek".**
- The nearest spelling is **"Anton Swiontek 70"** in sec 29 (the NE part).
  - Swiontek / Świątek / Swantek may be the same family, but that is unproven.
  - BLM sec 29: -87.8669..-87.8465, 44.4292..44.4438.
  - Approx. centroid of the Swiontek 70 ≈ 44.441, -87.852.

**Hamlet:**
- A church + school + small-lot cluster sits at the S edge of secs 21/22, where a diagonal road from the NW meets the E-W 21/28 line road.
- This is likely the old Green Bay-Kewaunee road (old Hwy 29). Unverified.
- The cluster is ≈ 44.444, -87.826.
- "A block north of Highway 29" would put Frank Swantek on a small village lot here. Lots are not named on this plat.
