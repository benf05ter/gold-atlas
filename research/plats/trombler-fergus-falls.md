# Mill Street, Fergus Falls MN, c.1903 (Trombler) (2026-11-01)

Source: **Sanborn, Fergus Falls, January 1903** (LoC `sanborn04297_004`), key map and street index on sheet 1. Read view-only. Modern streets are from OSM/Nominatim.

## Mill Street in 1903
- A **N-S street in the downtown grid**, between Court St (W) and Cascade St (E). It crosses the Red River at the downtown dam and mill area, hence the name.
- **Index ranges:**
  - **N. Mill 100-319**, on sheets 7, 6, 3 and 2. It runs N from the river past Lincoln, Cavour and Summit to the Lakeside Drive / Lake Alice area.
  - **S. Mill 100-725**, on sheets 6, 9 and 13. It runs S from the river past Bismarck, Junius, Hampden, Vernon and Vasa to about Alcott Ave.
- **The name survives on the same alignment:** N Mill St and S Mill St (OSM, about -96.0742 along their length).

## Approximate ends (modern)
- **N end (1903, about no. 319, near Lake Alice):** about 46.2865, -96.0745. This is the N end of today's N Mill St.
- **River crossing (the 100 block N/S):** about 46.283, -96.0742.
- **S end (1903, about no. 725, near Alcott Ave):** about 46.270, -96.0742. Today's S Mill St continues S to about 46.265.
