# Lars Nielson (Danish recluse, d. 1916): Morken Twp, Clay Co., MN plat placement (2026-10-31)

**FOUND: "Lars Nelson Est 320", W½ of sec 14, Morken Twp.**

## Township check
- The plate header reads **"MORKEN Township, Township 141 North, Range 47 West of the 5th P.M."**
- The coordinator's T140N R46W guess was off. Flowing Twp, T141N R46W, adjoins it on the E.
- BLM PLSS id: MN051410N0470W0.

## Parcel
- **"Lars Nelson Est 320"**, printed vertically across the whole **W½ of sec 14** (NW¼ + SW¼). These are his two quarters (homestead + tree claim).
- **"Est"** in 1909 is odd for a man who died in 1916. Possibilities:
  - the abbreviation means "estate" in the sense of land holding;
  - the atlas maker erred;
  - a different Lars Nelson (spelled Nelson, not Nielson).
  - **Verify via a GLO patent** (BLM GLO Records search not done; the site needs interactive search).
- **One building dot**, near the SW corner of the SW¼ (on the plat).
- Neighbors in sec 14 E½: Joseph Huska 80, Eva R. Huska 80, Winnebago Co. Bank 160.
- Other neighbors: Chas. B. Miller, Edward Kelander and Trustee (sec 15 W); Thos. F. Conway, R. D. Kelsey and M. F. Crosby (sec 23 S).
- **No lake.** Flat Red River Valley prairie. A ditch runs along the sec 9/10 line to the NW. The Buffalo River is about 5 mi W, along the W edge of the township.

## Location
BLM sec 14 bounds: lon -96.6060 to -96.5847, lat 47.0206 to 47.0351.

**1939 air photo (MHAPO) bxr06057, 1939-07-10,** centred 47.0224,-96.6058, which is right on the SW corner of sec 14: https://geo.lib.umn.edu/dnr_historical_aerial_imagery/airphotos/usda/bxr/y1939/bxr06057.jpg
- It shows **one farmstead in the W½ of sec 14**: a building cluster with a few trees (a small shelterbelt), about 0.35 mi E of the W section line and about 0.1 mi N of the S section-line road. That is the **SE¼ of the SW¼ of sec 14**.
- Nothing else is built on the W½. The NW¼ is open field (the tree-claim quarter would have had planted trees; only scattered small trees show).
- **Farmstead estimate: 47.0221, -96.5986** (±150 m).
- The 1909 plat dot sits closer to the SW corner (about 47.0221, -96.6046). The two may be the original shanty site vs. the later (c.1912) house, or the plat dot may be imprecise. **Both are search candidates.**
- **Nearest road:** the E-W section-line road on the S line of sec 14 (sec 14/23 line), which meets the N-S road on the W line (sec 14/15) at the SW corner.
- **Other photos:** 1948 Clay Co. BXR-1E-171 (centre 47.0200,-96.5963) and BXR-1E-172.

| LARS NIELSON | 1895-03-12 | **Timber Culture** | 219 | TC-0211-260 | **NW¼ sec 14**, T141N R47W, 5th PM |

**Confirmed:**
- The name is spelled "Nielson" on both patents (the 1909 plat's "Lars Nelson" is the same man).
- His two quarters are the SW¼ (homestead, patented 1885) and the NW¼ (tree claim, patented 1895). They make up the W½ of sec 14, matching the plat's 320 acres.
- He entered about 1880, which fits "held ~36 yrs" to 1916.

**Implication for the search:** the homestead quarter is the **SW¼**, where both farmstead candidates sit (the 1939 air-photo farmstead in the SE¼ SW¼, and the plat dot near the SW corner). So the sod shanty and later house were on the SW¼. The NW¼ was the tree-claim quarter (1939: open field).

## 1939 air photo re-read, W½ sec 14 (MHAPO bxr06057, flown 1939-07-10)
https://geo.lib.umn.edu/dnr_historical_aerial_imagery/airphotos/usda/bxr/y1939/bxr06057.jpg

**Georeference:**
- The road grid frames sec 14 exactly: N-S roads on the 14/15 and 13/14 lines, E-W roads on the 11/14 and 14/23 lines. That's 1 mile ≈ 1,759 src px (≈0.91 m/px).
- Corners pinned to BLM PLSS bounds (-96.6060..-96.5847, 47.0206..47.0351).
- Accuracy ±40-60 m.

**NW¼ (1895 tree claim): a farmstead AND the tree-claim grove.**
- **Tree plantation:** a long N-S block of planted trees about 105 m × 520 m (~13-14 acres). That fits the Timber Culture requirement of 10 acres of trees. It runs from about **47.0281 to 47.0328 N, -96.6012 to -96.5998 W**, in the E-centre of the NW¼.
- **Farmstead on its W side:** the main building is at **≈47.0306, -96.6008**, with a smaller outbuilding ~60 m SW (≈47.0300,-96.6013).
- A farm lane runs N-S along the W edge of the grove, from the quarter line up to the N road.
- **A fenced square** (~130 × 160 m, about 5 acres; a paddock, garden or old yard) sits just W of the buildings, centred **≈47.0303, -96.6023**.
- In the 1st pass I wrote "open field" for the NW¼. That was wrong: this crop was not looked at closely then.

**SW¼ (1885 homestead): one farmstead.**
- A building cluster with scattered trees is at **≈47.0222, -96.5987** (±50 m). That is about 0.1 mi N of the S-line road, in the SE¼ of the SW¼.
- **This is the "1912 house" point in the notes (47.0221,-96.5986): it matches.**

**"Sod shanty" point (47.0221, -96.6046, from the 1909 plat dot):**
- **Nothing visible in 1939**: uniform cropland, about 120 m E of the 14/15 line road. No crop mark, grove or depression is seen at this scale.
- A sod shanty site would be expected to show nothing by 1939. The point stays a plat-derived guess only.

**Summary for pins:**
- **SW¼ farmstead 47.0222,-96.5987**: confirmed building site; the house c.1912.
- **NW¼ farmstead 47.0306,-96.6008** plus the tree-claim grove: a second building site on Nielson's land in 1939. It may be his barn or shack, or a later owner's.
- The **shanty point 47.0221,-96.6046**: no surface trace in 1939.
