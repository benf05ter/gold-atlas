# Bronson gold farm / C. N. Phillips, sec 5 T7S R8W, Branch Co. MI: plat job 4 (2026-11-01)

Follows research/plats/phillips-bronson.md (1894 atlas: "C. N. Phillips", a strip in the E part of sec 5, Bronson Twp).

Source (view-only): 1915 *Standard Atlas of Branch County* (archive.org 2911296.0001.001.umich.edu), Bronson Township plate, printed p.53 = **leaf 52** (in this volume leaf = page − 1 around the township plates; leaf 56 = p.57 Gilead, leaf 58 = p.59 Kinderhook).

## Parcel (1915)
- **"C. N. Phillips Est. 89"** (the Phillips estate, the same family as 1894) is a N-S strip across the N half of SEC 5, T7S R8W. It runs from the N township line (the Matteson line) down to about the E-W centre line of the section, roughly the E part of the NW¼ and the W part of the NE¼ (the "70.66" fractional N lot).
- **House mark:** a square dot near the N end of the strip, just S of the N line road (at the "70.66" label).
- A second dot to the E, near the NE¼ / sec 4 side, is outside the Phillips strip (probably a different owner).
- Neighbours: F. H. Rickards et al. 80 and F. Coward 24 (W, NW¼); Frank Coward 40 and 196 (S half, with a "Ditch" and a creek); Mrs. Shefler (W edge); Marie Burk 52 (SW).
- The same parcel as in 1894, now an estate. That is consistent with the "old Canary Phillips farm".

- Direction check: Bronson village is in secs 11-12 (≈41.872, -85.195), so the farm is about 3.5 mi WNW of the village, not "2 mi E". The 1939 article's direction stays unexplained (as in the 1894 note).
