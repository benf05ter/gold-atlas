## Sanborn check

- So the depot cannot be placed from a Sanborn.

## Fallback: rail layout (USGS US Topo basemap, view-only)
- Abbotsford grew up at the junction of the Wisconsin Central main line, which runs N-S through town along the Clark/Marathon line, and the Wisconsin Central branch that leaves NW toward Medford.
  - It was the junction town from 1880; it is the later Soo Line.
- Today's topo still shows the N-S main track. A wye or branch curves off it NW between W Sycamore St / N 1st Ave and Spruce St (Hwy 29), just W of City Hall.
- The depot of a junction town normally stood at the junction, by the main street (Spruce St / Hwy 29 crossing).
- **Junction / depot area: about 44.9471, -90.3202, r250.** That is the tracks between N 1st Ave and N 1st St, from Birch St S to Spruce St.
- **Confidence C-.** This is inferred from the track layout, not from a map that labels the depot.
