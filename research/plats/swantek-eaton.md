# Frank Swantek, Town of Eaton (T23N R22E), Brown Co. WI (2026-11-01)

Sources, read by eye and view-only:
- WHS c.1915 Hixson *Plat Book of the State of Wisconsin*, Brown plate "Town of Green Bay ... and Eaton" (AssetLink e105l5758310un57mylnt163sujb534q).
- 1920s *Wisconsin Farmer's Plat Book of Brown County* (W0175WJ), Eaton. See swantek-eaton-plat.md.
- No 1930s Brown plat is open. archive.org has only 1877 and 1903 Brown maps; WHS has the two above.

## Result
- **No Frank Swantek** on either plate.
- **The Swiontek family cluster at the Eaton crossroads, c.1915:**
  - **Martin Swiontek 70**: NE¼ of sec 29, at its NE corner. A "**P.O.**" label sits on this parcel at the 20/21/28/29 section corner.
  - **Martin Swiontek 80**: W-central sec 29.
  - **Jno. Swiontek 80**, plus a small J. Swiontek parcel: sec 32, by the Smith Bros lakes.
  - In the 1920s plat this is "Anton Swiontek 70", the same NE sec 29 parcel.
- The hamlet (church, school, small lots) and the diagonal Green Bay road (old Hwy 29) meet at the S edge of secs 21/22. The P.O. corner is about 0.5 mi W of that.

## Proposed pin
- **NE corner area of sec 29 (the Swiontek 70 / P.O. corner): about 44.442, -87.848, r300.** BLM sec 29 runs -87.8669 to -87.8465 and 44.4292 to 44.4438.
- Rural.
- **Confidence C.** Frank is not named; the pin assumes he was of the Swiontek family at the Eaton P.O. corner.
