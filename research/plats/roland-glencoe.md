# Patrick Roland (1893 miser murder), Town of Glencoe (T21N R10W), Buffalo Co. WI: plat job 2 (2026-11-01)

Lead: Patrick Roland, an old soldier and miser "in the town of Glencoe, six miles from" Arcadia, was killed with an axe in his HUT in Nov 1893; "no one knows where his money is hidden" (Milwaukee Sentinel 1893-11-19 p4). The current pin rests on BLM patents in secs 1-2.
