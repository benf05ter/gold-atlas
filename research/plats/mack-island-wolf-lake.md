# "Mack Island", Wolf Lake, Grass Lake Twp, Jackson Co. MI (2026-11-01)

Reads were view-only.

## Sources
- USGS National Map topo basemap (current US Topo).
- Esri World Imagery, both for bbox -84.262 to -84.222, 42.188 to 42.206.
- USGS GNIS (layers 5 and 7).
- Nominatim/OSM.
- archive.org has only the 1875 Shober county wall map (Rumsey `dr_`). The 1874/1911 Jackson Co. atlases are not on archive.org. Overpass was down (502), so no OSM island polygon could be pulled.

## Findings
- **GNIS has no "Mack Island"** (and no island at all) at Wolf Lake. Nearby GNIS lakes include Little Wolf Lake (42.1923, -84.2258) and Mellen Camp Lake.
- **OSM has "Mack Island Road"**, running E-W along about 42.198-42.199 from the E end of Wolf Lake (about -84.233) east to -84.226. So the name survives on the road.
- **There is no true island in Wolf Lake today**, on either the topo or the imagery.
- At the E end, a **wooded knoll peninsula** divides the main lake from its eastern basin.
  - The topo draws a closed hill reaching about 970 ft, against a lake level of about 940 ft.
  - It is joined to the N shore only by low wet ground. Mack Island Road and its short spur roads run onto it.
  - This is very likely the former "Mack Island": high ground that was once ringed by water or marsh, before the lake level changed or the land was filled.
- **Knoll centre: about 42.1945, -84.2318.** The S tip is about 42.1930, -84.2303. The coordinates come from the bbox georeference, ±30 m.
- No hotel or cottage history is recorded; there is no atlas to check. Today the knoll has houses along its spur roads.
