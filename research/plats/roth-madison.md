# Matthew Roth farm, Town of Madison, Dane Co. WI (c.1907): plat read (2026-11-01)

Source: WHS c.1915 Hixson *Plat Book of the State of Wisconsin*, Dane plate "Town of Madison, Medina, Verona, Pleasant Springs, Rutland, and Blooming Grove" (AssetLink 6256uihgwvx3w4jd785l63cdscu8ggoa). Read by eye, view-only. The 1904 and 1911 Dane plats were not reachable: the WHS search returned errors to scripts, and archive.org has only the 1877 county map.

## What the plate shows
- **"M. Roth 20"** is a narrow strip at the **extreme NE corner of the Town of Madison**, i.e. the N part of the **NE¼ NE¼ of sec 1, T7N R9E**.
- Immediately W are "Golf Links 57" (the Maple Bluff club) and R. M. La Follette 60. Immediately S are "L.S." and the Lakewood Land Co. 123.
- The railroad (the line toward Sun Prairie / Mendota) runs close by on the E.
- That fits "near the golf grounds".
- **Woodward** and **Schlimgen** are not on this plate. They would be E or N across the town line, in Burke or Westport (T8N R10E), which I did not read.
- No house mark is legible on the Roth strip at this scale.

## Location today
- Sec 1 T7N R9E (BLM) runs -89.3834 to -89.3638 and 43.1043 to 43.1190.
- **Proposed pin: about 43.118, -89.3652, r150.** OSM reverse-geocodes this to Fremont Ave, Sheridan Triangle, on Madison's North Side.
- **Built over:** yes. It is now city residential (the Sheridan Triangle / Maple Bluff edge), roughly along N Sherman Ave and the rail line.
- **Confidence C+.** Roth is identified next to the golf grounds, but Woodward and Schlimgen are not yet checked.

## Follow-up: Town of Burke sheet (read 2026-11-01)
Source: WHS W017VNM Dane plate "Town of Windsor, Oregon, Burke ..." (AssetLink 272m22oa14566u6e8amj164o5gcxrt1h).

**The Roth land extends E across the town line into Burke sec 31, T8N R10E.** That section adjoins Madison sec 1 directly on the E, at the same latitudes (BLM: -89.3641 to -89.3452 and 43.1067 to 43.1215).

The W tier of Burke sec 31, from N to S:
- the McKenna Plat (hatched);
- **T. Schlimgen**: a thin strip directly N of Roth;
- **M. Roth**: a larger parcel with a **house mark at its W edge**, on the town line road;
- **S. Roth 70**, S of M. Roth;
- A. Schultz 33.

The C.M.&St.P. railroad crosses the parcels diagonally. Darwin Sta. and Sunnyside are just NE.

- **No Woodward** appears in the SW part of Burke. I did not read Westport, which lies across Lake Mendota and is less likely.
- **Fit:** Schlimgen is directly adjacent to Roth along the old town-line road, which matches "lives across the road". The 1907 farm was clearly more than the 20-acre Madison strip.

**Revised pin: Roth house mark, about 43.118, -89.3635, r150** (W edge of Burke sec 31 at the Madison town line). The farm block centre is about 43.1165, -89.360. The area is now Madison North Side residential (the Sherman / Sheridan Triangle area). **Confidence B.**
