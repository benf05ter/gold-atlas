# Adolph Strolfer, Douglas Co WI, about 4 mi E of Superior (PC b, 2026-11-02)

## BLM GLO patents (glorecords.blm.gov, new site; view only)
- Full-text search, Patents, WI: "Strolfer" gives 0; "Stroelfer" gives 0. A "Strol*" wildcard returns only 11 unrelated WI patents (fuzzy matching; no Strolfer).
- Patents, WI, county **Douglas**: "Adolph" gives 3 (Adolph Blomst 1908, T43N R14W sec 12; Adolph Volker 1910, T46N R10W sec 36; Adolph A. Hetebrugge 1912, T43N R13W sec 10). "Adolf" gives 1 (Adolf Carlson 1893, T47N R11W sec 36). **No Strolfer patent in Douglas County.**
- Likely reason: land E of Superior (T49N R13-14W) mostly passed through railroad grants, state lands or speculators, so a 40 bought there about 1880-85 would be a private deed (Douglas Co Register of Deeds), not a federal patent.
- Search tip for the new GLO site: the URL `#/search?q=<word>&documenttype=Patent&geostatecodes=WI&counties=Douglas` works when the page is reloaded fresh (the s-name parameter is ignored).
