# F. Z. Wegemer gravel pit on the Bear River, south of Petoskey, Emmet Co. MI (1913) (2026-11-01)

Lead: Detroit Free Press 1913-10-26: "Franz Z. Wegemer gravel pit on the bank of Bear River, south of this city".

## Source
- **1902 *Plat Book of Emmet County*** (archive.org 2933895.0001.001.umich.edu), Bear Creek Twp plate (T34N R5W), leaf 11. Read by eye at full resolution, view-only.
- **No 1910s or 1919 Emmet atlas is on archive.org.** The only other Emmet item there is the 1873 Walling county map.

## Result: no Wegemer in 1902
- The atlas OCR has no "Wegemer" or "Wegmer".
- **The Bear River south of the city:** it leaves Petoskey at the S edge of sec 6/5 and runs S/SE through **secs 8, 17, 16, 21 and 28**, with the railroad alongside. Owners on or by the river in 1902:
  - **Sec 8:** T. Robinson 77.50, J. Chattaway 38, Siebert 120, C. E. Call
  - **Sec 17:** H. Brewer 40, H. C. Townsend 40, L. G. Grimes, T. A. Bremmeyer 67 (on the river)
  - **Sec 16:** Fred. Boening 60, Carpenter Est. 40
  - **Sec 21:** J. R. Wylie 190, the River Valley Stock Farm (C. Hankins 170), Filer & Burch 40
  - **Sec 28:** Cobb & Mitchell
- No gravel-pit symbol (the atlas does not use one).
- Wegemer evidently bought river-bank land after 1902. The likeliest stretch for a gravel pit "south of the city" on the bank is **secs 8/17**, the first mile S of the 1902 city limit. That is about 45.355-45.365, -84.955 to -84.965, but the exact sections were not checked against BLM.
- **No pin proposed** under the no-guessing rule. A 1910s Emmet plat or the 1913-14 tax roll would place it.
