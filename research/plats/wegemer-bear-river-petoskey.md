# F. Z. Wegemer gravel pit on the Bear River, south of Petoskey, Emmet Co. MI (1913) (2026-11-01)

Lead: Detroit Free Press 1913-10-26: "Franz Z. Wegemer gravel pit on the bank of Bear River, south of this city".

## Source
- **1902 *Plat Book of Emmet County*** (archive.org 2933895.0001.001.umich.edu), Bear Creek Twp plate (T34N R5W), leaf 11. Read by eye at full resolution, view-only.
- **No 1910s or 1919 Emmet atlas is on archive.org.** The only other Emmet item there is the 1873 Walling county map.

## Result: no Wegemer in 1902
- The atlas OCR has no "Wegemer" or "Wegmer".
- **The Bear River south of the city:** it leaves Petoskey at the S edge of sec 6/5 and runs S/SE through **secs 8, 17, 16, 21 and 28**, with the railroad alongside. Owners on or by the river in 1902:
  - **Sec 8:** T. Robinson 77.50, J. Chattaway 38, Siebert 120, C. E. Call
  - **Sec 17:** H. Brewer 40, H. C. Townsend 40, L. G. Grimes, T. A. Bremmeyer 67 (on the river)
  - **Sec 16:** Fred. Boening 60, Carpenter Est. 40
  - **Sec 21:** J. R. Wylie 190, the River Valley Stock Farm (C. Hankins 170), Filer & Burch 40
  - **Sec 28:** Cobb & Mitchell
- No gravel-pit symbol (the atlas does not use one).
- Wegemer evidently bought river-bank land after 1902. The likeliest stretch for a gravel pit "south of the city" on the bank is **secs 8/17**, the first mile S of the 1902 city limit. That is about 45.355-45.365, -84.955 to -84.965, but the exact sections were not checked against BLM.
- **No pin proposed** under the no-guessing rule. A 1910s Emmet plat or the 1913-14 tax roll would place it.

## Sanborn and newspaper check (Treasure list item 1, 2026-11-01)
- **Owner per Detroit News 1913-11-02 p59: "Frank A. Wegemere"**; the pit is "on the bank of Bear river, in the southern part of the city" (see pc-a-batch3-results.md).
- newspapers.com: "Wegemer OR Wegemere" gravel MI 1905-25 gives 1 hit (that same Detroit News page). The Emmet Co. filter for 1900-30 gives **0 (no Petoskey papers indexed)**, so the street can't be found there.
- **LoC Sanborn, Petoskey, Oct 1913** (sanborn04149_006, 25 sheets; viewed in browser via tile.loc.gov IIIF):
  - Key sheet 1: the Bear River runs S from the bay through sheets 13/14, 17 and 18, past Mitchell Rd/Sheridan/Ann, to the S corporation line. Below sheet 18 the river corridor is **unmapped** (no sheets), with only streets (Willis Av., Philip B. Wachtel Add'n, Curtis & Wylie's Add'n) shown on the key.
  - **Sheet 18** (the river in S Petoskey): W.L. McManus Lumber Co. saw mill and tail race on the Bear River; G.R.&I. R.R. round house and turntable; **De Arment Brick Yard with a "CLAY PIT"** (E of the tracks, W of Buckley Av.); Standard Oil Co.; Petoskey Block Mfg. Co.; Antrim Lime Co. (inset). **No gravel or sand pit is labelled.**
- Result: **the Wegemere gravel pit is not on the 1913 Sanborn.** It probably lay on the open river bank S of sheet 18 (between Mitchell Rd/Sheridan and the S corporation line), which Sanborn didn't map. The 1919 edition (sanborn04149_007) was not read; it likely covers the same area.
- A Petoskey city plat (c.1910s) or the 1913-14 city tax roll would fix the parcel. Treasure's pin 45.3620,-84.9510 r1000 (south Petoskey) covers the sheet-18 reach and the unmapped river S of it.
