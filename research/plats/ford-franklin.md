# Ford parcels, Franklin twp (T5N R21E), Milwaukee Co.: 1876 atlas re-read (2026-11-01)

Source: 1876 *Illustrated Historical Atlas of Milwaukee County*, Map of Franklin Township, p. 35 (WHS AssetLink f06454bmigwv672gj30baqm84aj6ru5q, 4287x5217). The plate was re-read by eye at full resolution, view-only.

## James or John?
- **Not determinable from the plate.** It prints only "**J. Ford 160**" on the SW¼ of sec 29, with a house dot in the S part, W-centre.
- No patrons' directory page was available alongside the WHS plate image to expand the initial.

## Other Ford parcels: yes, two more
Both are held by **"Mrs. M. Ford"**:
1. **Mrs. M. Ford 80**: the **E½ NW¼ of sec 29**, directly N of the J. Ford quarter.
   - The creek crosses it.
   - There is no house dot inside it. The nearest dot is at its NW corner, on the C. Lynch 40.
2. **Mrs. M. Ford 40**: the **SE¼ SE¼ of sec 19**, at the 19/20/29/30 corner area, NW of the J. Ford land.
   - A dot sits just W of it (on J. Lynch Jr. 80), and another sits at its SE corner on the sec 20 line.

So the Ford family held about 280 acres in 1876: the SW¼ 29 + E½ NW¼ 29 + SE¼ SE¼ 19. These form a contiguous block on and around the N-S section road between 29/30 and 19/20.

The house group stays the one on the J. Ford quarter: the earlier 1938 check put it at 42.8586, -88.0496.
