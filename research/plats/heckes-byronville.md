# Philbrook / Heckes farm, Byron Twp, Waseca Co. MN (1905 grove dig) (2026-11-01)

## Sources (UMedia, view-only)
- **1896 Ogle *Standard Atlas of Waseca County*** (coll231:1150), Byron Twp T105N R23W, p. 29 (ptr 1111).
  - Directory p. 59 (ptr 1126): "Philbrook, Roscoe, Farming, S. 14, T. Byron, P.O. New Richland."
- **1914 Webb *Atlas and Farm Directory*** (coll231:10902), directory p. 26 (ptr 10887): "Heckes, Herman, R3, sec 14, ac 320."

## Parcels on the 1896 plate
- **Roscoe Philbrook 160 = SE¼ of sec 14.**
  - House mark near the NW part of the quarter, i.e. just SE of the section centre.
  - A schoolhouse sits on the S line of the quarter.
  - Philbrook also holds the NE¼ of sec 11.
- **H. Heckes 160 = NE¼ of sec 14.** House mark at the W edge of the quarter.
- **By 1914, Herman Heckes holds 320 acres in sec 14.** That is presumably the whole E½: his own NE¼ plus the old Philbrook SE¼. This fits "the Philbrook farm" being Heckes land by 1905.
- The 1896 Ogle plat does not show groves.

## 1938 aerial (MHAPO we07367, 12 Aug 1938)
- A farmstead with a **tree grove** sits in the SE¼ area of sec 14, near the section centre. A second small grove is near the N part of the section.
- The georeference is approximate: centre point plus the road grid, ±300 m.

## Pin
- Sec 14 T105N R23W (BLM) runs -93.5673 to -93.5467 and 43.8918 to 43.9063.
- **1896 Philbrook house mark: about 43.8984, -93.5554, r200.**
- The 1938 grove/farmstead reads at about 43.897, -93.552 (±300 m), consistent with the house mark.
- **Rural.**
