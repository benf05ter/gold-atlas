# 541 Third Street, Milwaukee (1902 numbering): Hille / Schuerman bottle money (2026-11-01)

Source: LoC Sanborn **Milwaukee 1894 Vol. 1** (`sanborn09622_001`), street index and **sheet 64**. Read view-only.

## What the sheets show
- The Vol. 1 index lists Third St only from **560** upward: 560-641 on sheet 64, 642-720 on sheet 68, and so on.
- **Sheet 64:** Third St (N side of the river) runs N from **Walnut St** (the bottom of the sheet) past Sherman St to Reservoir Ave.
  - Lot numbers begin at **559/560 at Walnut** and rise to the N.
  - The W side is odd (559-639); the E side is even (560-640).
  - Below Walnut the sheet is marked "**See Volume Two**".
- **So 541 Third St (pre-1930 numbering) is on the W (odd) side of Third St, in the block just S of Walnut St.** That block is shown in Vol. 2.
- **Vol. 2 sheets were not reachable.** The LoC JSON lists no page resources for that volume, and direct IIIF requests were refused. So the exact lot and the house outline are not read.
