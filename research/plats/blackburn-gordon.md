## 1938 aerial: WHAIFinder Douglas BRS 8-66 (flown 8-18-38), centre 46.1671,-91.6765
https://web.s3.wisc.edu/rml-aerial-imagery/counties/WI/Douglas/1938/Douglas_WI_BRS_8_66_19380818.jpg
- Centre-point georeference at ~4.4 m per view px. **Accuracy only about ±200-300 m.** The plat lake vs the photo lake suggests the photo positions run ~200 m N.

**At the pin:** the Totagatic(?) River meanders through dense timber/brush. The pin sits on a wooded river bend. **No building or clearing at the pin itself.**

**Within ~500 m:**
- **Farmstead** (2-3 buildings with a cleared yard and lane) on the **NE shore of the lake**, at a road bend W of the river. **≈46.162, -91.694** (±250 m), ~400 m W of the pin. On the SW¼ "56.75" lot / W edge of sec 36.
- **Cleared field** between river loops ~250 m N: ≈46.165, -91.688 (W. F. Graham land).
- **Road:** a N-S road on the W side (~sec 35/36 line), with a branch curving E to the lake-shore farmstead. Faint tracks continue toward the river.
- **Other:** larger cleared fields ~1 km NE, and the main E-W road ~1.5 km N.

**Reading:**
- The only 1938 building site near the pin is the lake-shore farmstead ~400 m W.
- Whether that was the 1897 Blackburn homestead is **unproven**; the plat names no owner there.
- **Suggest:** keep the pin, but widen to r ~500 m to include the farmstead (≈46.162,-91.694).
