# Hession and "Glen" land, SW of Humboldt, Humboldt Co. IA (2026-10-31)

Reads were view-only, with no login or human-check clicks.

## Atlas used
- **1896 township plats, "Plat of Corinth", Township 91 N, Range 29 W, p. 6.**
  - Hosted on IAGenWeb Humboldt: https://iagenweb.org/humboldt/maps/images/Corinth_Township.jpg (4415x5249 scan).
  - Index page: https://iagenweb.org/humboldt/maps/1896townshipmaps.htm
  - Corinth in 1896 = the former Spring Vale twp, T91N R29W. It lies immediately SW of Humboldt and Dakota City.
- Also checked: the 1875 Andreas county map (Rumsey, archive.org `dr_map-of-humboldt-county-...-00724039`). It names only a few owners and shows no Hession.
- No other 1875-1915 Humboldt atlas is open. archive.org has none, and HathiTrust full-text search did not load.

## Hession land, 1896
- **"Dennis & Michael Hession 320"**: the **N½ of sec 15**, with a house dot near the NW part.
- **"Dennis Hession 80"**: the **W½ SE¼ of sec 14**, with a house dot at its S edge, near the sec 14/23 line.
- **"Michael Hession 80"**: the **E½ SE¼ of sec 14**, with a house dot at its SE.
- The NW¼ of sec 22 (the 1934 legal notice for Dennis Hession) was in other hands in 1896: Elizabeth Stoebe, the August Stoebe estate and Martha Mason are on the N part of sec 22. So the Hessions acquired it later.

## "Glen" land, 1896
- **"GLEN FARM"** is printed across the S part of **sec 24**, on the holding of **Lucy E. Jones, 512.82 acres** (secs 24, 13 and part of 25). Jones Siding on the railroad is there too.
  - Indian Creek meanders through the S half of sec 24 into the Des Moines River.
  - So "Glen" is the farm's name, not an owner surname.
- The 1896 plat does not show woodland symbols.
- **1930s aerial (Iowa ortho_1930; extent checked, -94.270 to -94.204 / 42.6699 to 42.7051):**
  - **Heavy timber** fills the NW part of sec 24 and the E edge of sec 23 along Indian Creek, at the Des Moines River bottom. Timber also runs along Indian Creek across the S parts of secs 23 and 24.
  - The surrounding upland is open cropland.
  - So the Glen Farm is the timbered tract in this township.

## Do they line up?
Yes.
- **Dennis Hession's 1934 quarter (NW¼ sec 22):** the 1930s aerial shows a farmstead with a grove inside it, at about **42.6850, -94.2596**.
- **From there:** the E-W road on the sec 15/22 line runs straight E about 2 mi to the N-S highway on the sec 23/24 line. The Glen Farm timber begins right there in NW sec 24, at about **42.6842, -94.2238**.
- **The 1896 Dennis Hession home (SE¼ sec 14):** a 1930s farmstead sits just N of the 14/23 line at about **42.6885, -94.2348**. That is under a mile W of the Glen timber along the same road grid.
- So the Glen woods are literally "down the road" from both Hession places.

## Proposed pins
- **Hession farmstead (1934 quarter):** 42.6850, -94.2596, **r150**. Confidence B, from the plat-confirmed section plus the farmstead on the 1930s photo.
- **Glen Farm timber:** 42.6842, -94.2238, **r600** (NW sec 24 woods along Indian Creek and the river). Confidence B for the identity of the Glen Farm. The exact spot in the woods is not known.
- **Alternate Hession home (1896):** 42.6885, -94.2348, r150.

Georeference: the photo's road lines matched the BLM section lines (sec 22 runs -94.2659 to -94.2462 and 42.6736 to 42.6881; sec 24 runs -94.2266 to -94.2073), ±100 m.
