## Parcel (1902)
- **"ANDREW DIXON 123"**: a N-S strip along the **E edge of SEC 23, T77N R23W**, directly W of the sec 23/24 line, with a **house dot** in the strip's centre.
- At the NE corner of sec 23, just N of the Dixon strip: a **School**, a church and cemetery ("ch, cem", J.K. Deaton 36), and a "P" (post office) lot. A second "Church" is in the SE part of sec 23.
- Sec 24 (E, across the line road): Wm. H. Gardner 152.50, Martha Miller 50, Tillman Gibbons 97½, Jno. Gildow 135, J.T. Lacy, H. Clark, A. Spence, H. James 40.
