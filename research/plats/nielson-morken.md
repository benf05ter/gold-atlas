# Nielson, Morken Twp, Clay Co. MN: pins vs plat and 1939 aerial (2026-10-31)

Summary of work done earlier today. Full details are in research/plats/nielson-morken-plat.md.

**Plat:** 1909 Ogle Clay Co. atlas (UMedia coll231:10304, Morken T141N R47W, page ptr 10248).
- "Lars Nelson Est 320" = the W½ of sec 14 (NW¼ + SW¼), with one building dot near the SW corner.
- GLO patents to LARS NIELSON: SW¼ sec 14, homestead, 1885-05-20; NW¼ sec 14, timber culture, 1895-03-12.
- The 1918 atlas was not opened. Lars died in 1916.

**Aerial:** MHAPO bxr06057 (1939-07-10). Sec 14 is road-framed on BLM bounds (-96.6060..-96.5847, 47.0206..47.0351), ±40-60 m.

**Which site the aerial favours:**
- Both carry buildings in 1939, so the aerial alone doesn't choose.
- The **homestead (primary)** is the documented original farm (sod shanty / house c.1912 per the lead).
- The tree-claim farmstead may be a later owner's or tenant's.
- Suggest keeping the primary. Optionally shift its centre ~150 m E (to ≈47.0222,-96.6000) so r300 covers both the 1939 farmstead and the 1909 plat dot evenly.
