# Andrew Olesen (maple box, Nov 1900): Lynn area, Clark Co., WI plat check (2026-10-31)

**Result: no Olesen / Olsen / Oleson parcel found in the Lynn-Romadka corridor.**

## Geography (corrected)
- **Lynn Twp is T24N R1E**, not R1W. Lynn village is at the sec 5/8 line.
- **Romadka is in York Twp (T25N R1W), sec 14**: the patrons list has "Kintzele, John P., Real Estate... at Romadka, S. 14, T. York".
- The "Milwaukee/St. Paul" railroad runs from Lynn village NW through Lynn sec 6, then Fremont Twp (T25N R1E) secs 31/30/19 ("Harris Station" in sec 19), then York secs 24 ("Nast Sta.") / 13 to Romadka in sec 14.
- So **"between Lynn and Romadka" = Lynn sec 6, the W column of Fremont (secs 19/30/31), and the E part of York (secs 13/14/23/24/25)**. That's about a 6-mile corridor NW of Lynn, not due W. (Due W is Grant Twp, T24N R1W.)

## Conclusion / candidates
- Andrew Olesen probably bought from a land company or rented, and may have left before 1906.
- **Best substitute placement (unproven):** the **James Olson 320 in secs 22/23 of Lynn**. "Olesen" and "Olson" are often confused, and it's a large holding on Cunningham Creek.
  - But that is SE of Lynn village, not toward Romadka. Only use it as a weak candidate.
- **Corridor placement:** NW of Lynn along the railroad toward Harris Station (Fremont sec 30/19). **CORRECTED (coordinator, 2026-10-31):** GNIS Lynn village is 44.5825,-90.4099 and the Town of York centroid is 44.640,-90.497. So the Lynn-Romadka corridor is about **44.58-44.65 N, -90.41 to -90.47 W**. The earlier 44.835,-90.33 was ~17 mi too far north.
- A pre-1900 plat (an 1893 Clark Co. atlas) would be needed to find him. None was found on WHS / UMedia in this session.
