# Schaeffermeyer (Schaefermeyer) miser farm, La Crosse Co. WI (d. 1 Mar 1903; settled 1904) (2026-11-01)

Background (research/newspapers-com/pc-b-asks-results.md, batch 3):
- C. Henry Schaefermeyer, a wealthy La Crosse county farmer aged 80, died 1 Mar 1903.
- His sister Mrs. Hahn took "a large farm in La Crosse county", said to hide "something over $10,000".

## Sources checked (view-only)
- **BLM GLO patents, WI:**
  - Schaffermeyer, Schaeffermeyer and Scheffermeyer: **0 results each.**
  - A Schaefermeyer/Schafermeyer search was sent but its result screen was not captured, so that is unconfirmed.
  - He probably bought the farm second-hand.
- **No c.1890-1906 La Crosse Co. plat is open.**
  - archive.org has none.
  - WHS has only the c.1915 Hixson *Plat Book of the State of Wisconsin* La Crosse plates:
    - "Town of Hamilton, Shelby, Onalaska, and Burns" (AssetLink hnt06a3356n544w81h3w67gi306ue1a2)
    - "Town of Washington, Farmington, Bangor, and Campbell" (AssetLink t45ya2256mb07si657lok632h2ujrgqt)
    - an outline map
  - No Hixson plate for Barre, Greenfield or Holland turned up in the plate list.

## c.1915 Hixson reads (by eye, full resolution)
- **Town of Shelby (T15N R6-7W)**, all sections read: **no Schaefermeyer (any spelling) and no Hahn.**
- **"Campbell" plate (T16N R7W: Campbell, Medary, Onalaska city to La Crosse), secs 1-3 and 8-36 read: no Schaefermeyer.**
  - One Hahn: **"Theresa Hahn"**, a small tract in the **NW¼ of sec 14 T16N R7W**.
    - It lies between the La Crosse River (N) and the C.M.&St.P. railway (S), next to L. M. Goldfish et al 52 (W) and A. Wich 31.25 (E).
    - No acreage figure is legible (it looks like roughly 20-30 acres); no house mark.
    - It is about 2 mi NE of La Crosse.
  - This is **not a "large farm"**, so it is a weak candidate only.
- **Not yet read:** Hamilton, Onalaska, Burns, Washington, Farmington and Bangor (on the two plates), plus Barre, Greenfield and Holland (no plate found).

## Coordinates (candidate only)

- **Theresa Hahn tract: about 43.8658, -91.1874, r200.** Confidence D. "Hahn" is a common name, and the parcel is small with no house.
- **No pin proposed for the Schaefermeyer farm.**

## Follow-up: the six remaining Hixson towns (2026-11-01)
I read the Towns of Hamilton, Onalaska, Burns, Washington, Farmington and Bangor in full, by eye at full resolution, on both plates.
- **No Schaefermeyer (any spelling) and no Bothenfelt** anywhere.
- **No Hahn** in any of the six towns.
- One more small Hahn turned up on the Campbell plate: **"J. Hahn 13.44"**. It is a riverside lot near the Mississippi, at the sec 14/23 area of the Campbell/French Island strip. It is too small to be the "large farm".
- **Result:** the farm does not appear under the family names on the c.1915 plat.
  - By 1915 the farm had probably passed out of the Hahn name.
  - Mrs. Hahn may have sold it, or it may have gone to a married daughter under another surname.
  - It may also lie in Barre, Greenfield or Holland. No Hixson plate was found for Barre or Greenfield. Holland appears only partly, on the Shelby plate margin, with no Hahn there.
- The pin stays as above: weak candidate only (Theresa Hahn, sec 14 T16N R7W, D). **No farm pin.** The census and probate items are on Neb's Sunday list.

## FOUND: 1874 Bliss "Map of the County of La Crosse" (LoC 2012593190, IIIF full res, view-only) (2026-11-01)
Campbell Twp, T16N R7W, by the La Crosse River, about 2-3 mi NE of La Crosse near "Winona Junction" and the "Fourmile House". These coordinates come from position relative to the BLM section bounds, ±150 m.

| Name on map | Parcel | Approx. coords |
|---|---|---|
| **C. H. Schaefermeyer** | Long N-S strip at the **W edge of sec 14**, running the full section from the N line south across the railway; river to the N | **43.863, -91.1876** |
| H. Schaefermeyer | Strip along the La Crosse River in **E sec 16 / W of Winona Junction** | ~43.856, -91.2134 |
| H. Schaefermeyer | **NW sec 22**, beside the Fourmile House | ~43.853, -91.2080 |

- Neighbour: **John Hahn**, sec 21 (W of the Fourmile House).
- **C. H. = C. Henry Schaefermeyer, d. 1903** (Find a Grave 67784436).
- **Continuity:** the c.1915 Hixson plate shows **Theresa Hahn** on the same ground in NW sec 14 T16N R7W, between the river and the railway (earlier pin 43.8658, -91.1874). That fits the 1904 settlement, where his sister **Mrs. Hahn took the farm**.
- So the Theresa Hahn tract is the old C. H. Schaefermeyer farm, or its core. The candidate is **upgraded**.

## Pin (revised)
- **Schaefermeyer/Hahn farm: 43.864, -91.1876, r300.** That is the W strip of sec 14 T16N R7W, from the La Crosse River S to the railway.
- **Confidence B-.** The 1874 owner and the c.1915 Hahn successor sit on the same strip. No house mark is legible on either map.
- The other H. Schaefermeyer parcels (E sec 16, NW sec 22) are secondary holdings of the same family.
