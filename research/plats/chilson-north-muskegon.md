# Chilson, block 17 lots 7-8, North Muskegon MI: 1900 plat read (2026-11-01)

Source: 1900 *Standard Atlas of Muskegon County*, North Muskegon plat, pp. 60-61 (archive.org `3927917.0001.001.umich.edu`, leaf 62). Read by eye, view-only.

## Which block 17
- The plat has several additions: Mills', Mills' 2nd, Storrs & Co., and the "Torrent & Arms Plat Corrected & Enlarged".
- The only block 17 is in the **Torrent & Arms Plat**, in the NW part of the village.
- **Bounds:**
  - Center St on the W and Nelson St on the E.
  - The street N of it is the Pine St line, with block 22 beyond.
  - Block 16 lies E across Nelson, and block 18 W across Center.
- **Lot layout:** lots 1-6 run along the N side and 7-12 along the S side, numbered from the W.
  - So **lots 7 and 8 are the two westernmost S-side lots**, next to Center St.
- **Road:** a double-dashed diagonal road (SW to NE) cuts across the W end of block 17 and on through block 23 toward the Bear Lake shore. That matches the June 1897 county road through the NW half of lots 7-8, the old Whitehall Road line.
- **Owner names:** none printed on these lots, which carry lot numbers only.
