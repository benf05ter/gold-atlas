# Mulchrone (Patrick, found dead 1899), Franklin Twp, Vernon Co. WI: aerial check (2026-10-31)

**Plat** (coordinator, from the 1896 Vernon plat): "Patk Mulchrone 113 ac", **sec 2 T11N R5W** (Franklin), with Fred Zitzner/Fitzner in the same section.
- The newspaper puts the house "three miles west of Liberty Pole", "only a short distance from the road", and close to Fred Zitzner's.
- BLM sec 2: -90.9481..-90.9281, 43.4529..43.4669. The pin 43.4610,-90.9347 is inside, in the SE-centre.
- I did not re-open the 1896 plat this pass (no WHS link in my notes), so the quarter isn't re-checked.

**1938 aerial: WHAIFinder Vernon BHP 3-26, flown 10-13-38 (centre 43.4661,-90.9428).**
- https://web.s3.wisc.edu/rml-aerial-imagery/counties/WI/Vernon/1938/Vernon_WI_BHP_3_26_19381013.jpg
- Driftless terrain; the roads are irregular. The georeference uses the straight E-W road taken as the sec 2 S line, plus a N-S field/road line taken as the E line, at about 343 px/mile. **Accuracy only about ±150-200 m.**
- **No building at the pin.** The pin area is fields with a few tree clumps, N of a curving E-W road (the main road, with a wooded strip along it).

**Reading:**
- An 1899 "humble cottage" of a recluse may not survive to 1938.
- "Short distance from the road" fits sites b or a, but the georeference is too loose to pick one.
- **Suggest:** keep the pin at 43.4610,-90.9347 and widen the radius to ~450 m to take in a and b. Confidence: low-moderate.
- To tighten it: re-read the 1896 plat for the quarter, and use the 1947 frame BII 1D-124 (centre 43.4600,-90.9302), which is better centred.

## Resolution (coordinator / court-probate thread, 2026-10-31)
- On the 1896 plat (WHS W0170V6, Franklin plate: AssetLink/sh854tv8o74uqvehw2i7fp144bq0nu3r.jpg), **Pat. Mulchrone 113.02 = the N½ of sec 2**. The house dot is at the current pin.
- **Site (a)** (43.465,-90.935) is on Mulchrone land.
- Site (b) is Fitzner/Henderson land; site (c) is Gorrell/Pettygrove land.
- Pin kept, radius widened to 450 m. The 1947 frame was not checked (stopped on request).
