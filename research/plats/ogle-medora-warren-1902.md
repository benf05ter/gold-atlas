## Ogle land, 1902 (read by eye)
- **"F. M. OGLE 80"**: the **S half, W-central part of SEC 10**, with a **house dot**. This is F. Marion Ogle himself, alive in 1902.
- "HARRISON OGLE 229.10": N part of sec 4. "HARRISON OGLE 172": NW/central sec 9.
- "H. H. OGLE 73.01": NW corner of sec 3.
- "H. OGLE 40" and "HENRY OGLE 40": W part of sec 10, N of F. M. Ogle. "H. OGLE 20": E edge of sec 9.
- Neighbours: Ira Judkins 79, Nancy Roach 61, L. M. Lacy 85 (secs 9/10), J. C. Clarke 80 (sec 10), Geo. D. Hayworth 234 (sec 4), Valentine Clark (sec 3). A small platted lot group sits at the E edge of sec 10.
