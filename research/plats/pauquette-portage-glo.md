# Pierre Pauquette, the Portage: GLO original survey plat, T12N R9E, 4th PM, Columbia Co WI (PC b, 2026-11-02)

Source: glorecords.blm.gov (new site), Surveys, WI, Columbia, T012N R009E: **6 survey records**. Viewed in the site's built-in viewer only (not downloaded).

- Others not opened: a supplemental diagram (approved 02-09-1835) covering T12N and T13N R9E; John Mullett 08-13-1836; John Brink original survey 05-20-1846; Henry S. Howell 11-08-1851; C. E. Corning.

## What the 1835 plat shows
- "Ft. Winnebago" with the fort symbol and a "Bridge" at the top (N edge, sec 2 area), on the Fox River bend.
- **"Claim No. 21, Acres 648.82, A. Grignon"**: a large private claim drawn diagonally across the W part of the township between the fort and the Wisconsin River (the portage strip).
- The **portage road** (double yellow line) runs SW from the fort area through **secs 4 and 9** to the **Wisconsin River bank in sec 9** (lot 3, "55.60"; lot 4, "39.15"). There is a **cluster of small building dots where the road meets the river** at the sec 9 lots.
- Note on the plat: "Let Sections 2, 3, 4, 9 in Township No. 12 N, Range No. 9 East, and Sections 33, 34, 35 in Township 13 N, Range 9 East, be reserved for Military Purposes. ... Andrew Jackson" (the Fort Winnebago military reservation).
- **No "Pauquette" or "Paquette" label** anywhere I could read at 60% zoom (NW quarter, the Grignon claim and the Wisconsin River bank).
- **Best reading:** the building cluster at the river end of the portage road, in **sec 9 (lots 3-4) T12N R9E**, is the Wisconsin River landing where Pauquette's post or house stood, but the plat does not name it. It is inside the 1835 military reservation (secs 2, 3, 4, 9), next to the Grignon claim. Grade C, unlabelled.
- **1835 supplemental diagram (approved 02-09-1835, GLO doc 17181342478110995064), read:** covers T13N R9E secs 33-36 and T12N R9E secs 1-4/9. It shows Ft. Winnebago, the "Bridge" over the Fox, "Claim No. 21, Acres 648.82, A. Grignon", the sec 9 lots (3: 55.60 ac; 4: 30.15 ac) along the Wisconsin River, and Swan Lake. Note: "Sections 2, 3, 4 and 9 Tp 12 N R 9 E, and Sections 33, 34 and 35 in Tp 13 N R 9 E Reserved from Sale for Military Purposes by order of the President February 9th 1835." **No Pauquette or Paquette label; no buildings drawn at the sec 9 landing.**
- **1846 John Brink original survey (approved 05-20-1846, survey office Dubuque, 5,860.71 acres, GLO doc 12828731810836347660; 2 sheets), glanced:** sheet 1 shows the rest of T12N R9E (the Wisconsin River's W bend, the Baraboo River, secs 19-31 etc.) with a meander table. The military-reserve sections in the NE are not subdivided. **No name labels seen** at overview zoom; not read in detail.
