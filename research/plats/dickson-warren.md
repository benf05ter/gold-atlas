- URL pattern: ortho.gis.iastate.edu/arcgis/rest/services/ortho/ortho_1930/ImageServer/exportImage, bbox ±0.012° lon / ±0.009° lat, EPSG:4326.
- Accuracy: the mosaic's own georeference, about ±30-50 m.
