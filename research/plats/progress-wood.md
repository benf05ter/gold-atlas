## Result: FOUND
- **"PROGRESS"** is printed as a platted village (a hatched block of lots) in the **SW¼ of SEC 33, T24N R2E** (Town of Rock), at the S edge of the town.
- A **railroad** runs NW-SE diagonally through the plat; the line continues NW through secs 29 and 20 to the village at the N line of secs 19/20. That village's name is partly cut off ("...NDSEY", probably LINDSEY).
- Neighbours (c.1915): Geo. Gibson 309 (sec 32, W), J. Fagan 73.75, R.D. Evans 78.7 and R.W. Muller 80 (N part sec 33), O.C. Wettelson 160 (NE sec 33), Fred Boushon 40, Northwestern Land Co. 120 (SE sec 33).
