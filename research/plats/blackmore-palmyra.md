# Blackmore brothers' shack, about 3 mi SW of Palmyra, Jefferson Co. WI (1927) (2026-11-01)

Source: WHS c.1915 Hixson *Plat Book of the State of Wisconsin*, Jefferson plate "Town of Hebron, Coldspring, Palmyra, Sumner, and Aztalan" (AssetLink n3raqv7bjn8y03w62xe768q568eoiu77). Read by eye, view-only. No c.1919 or 1930 Jefferson plat was found open on WHS.

## Result: no Blackmore
- The SW quarter of the Town of Palmyra (secs 19-21, 27-34, around and SW of the village in secs 22-23) was read in full. There is **no Blackmore**.
- The only near-match is **"B. Moore 18"**, a small strip in the **E part of sec 33** near the 33/34 line, among other small tracts (W.L.K., E.C., P.P., Fulton).
  - It is about 1.5 mi S of Palmyra village, not 3 mi SW.
  - "B. Moore" could be a different person, or a mis-engraving of Blackmore. Unconfirmed.
- Not read: the S tier of Sullivan (beyond the plate) and Sumner, which lies W, around Lake Koshkonong.
- **Note:** Palmyra twp is on the Jefferson/Walworth county line. "3 mi SW of Palmyra" may fall in Walworth Co (Town of Whitewater or La Grange). Those towns are not checked for Blackmore here; walworth-1880s.md is a county history, not a plat.
