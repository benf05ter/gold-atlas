**Which pin the aerials favour:**
- Both quarters had an occupied farmstead in 1938, so the aerials alone don't choose.
- The documents favour **Medo**: Mamie Shanahan was "brought to the Tweit farm in Medo" (Mankato Free Press 1914-09-04), the funeral was at Medo, and the Medo tract had assessed buildings in 1895.
- Vivian sec 14 is Michael's own later quarter (1914), and it's the subject of the 1927 auction.

## JACOBSON (Mons, d. July 1932)

| **B**: NW¼ sec 4 T135N R41W (1888 homestead patent area) | patent; not Jacobson on the 1933 Star Lake plate | Working farmstead (several buildings, lanes) at the S edge of a large grove, just E of road D | **46.5385, -95.8630 (r ~80 m)** |

**Which pin the aerials favour:**
- **A for 1932.** It was the land he still held in 1933, and the cleared yard is consistent with a barn that was gone after the killing.
- B was probably occupied by another family by 1939, though it may hold the original buildings.
- A and B are ~520 m apart.
