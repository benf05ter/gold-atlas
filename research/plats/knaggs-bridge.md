# Knaggs Bridge / "Chief Knaggs' gully", Shiawassee Co. MI: placement (2026-10-31)

## 1. Knaggs Bridge location
- **OSM:** "Knaggs Bridge Area" (a memorial / historical-marker feature) at **42.8609, -84.0314**, on Cole Road, Burns Twp. Nominatim lists the type as "memorial".
- **USGS Corunna SE 7.5' (1972)** (prd-tnm GeoTIFF MI_Corunna SE_275903_1972_24000_geo.tif): the only river crossing at that spot is where the diagonal road (the red-dashed "Road", the old Indian/Treaty trail line) crosses the Shiawassee in **sec 6 (Burns Twp, T5N R4E)**, just W of **Gaylord Cem.** and BM 848.
  - Georeferenced from the quad neatlines: **crossing ≈ 42.8610, -84.0321**. This matches the OSM point within about 60 m.
- **USGS Corunna 15' (1926)** (MI_Corunna_278067_1926_62500_geo.tif): the same crossing, with **"Burns Sch"** on the E bank. No "Knaggs" label appears on either topo.
- This is about 10-11 mi SE of Owosso, not "a few miles". The 1937 phrasing is loose, or a different crossing is meant. **Treat this as the best available fix, not proven.**

## 2. Hill and ravine on the right (E) bank
- The river flows N toward Owosso here, so the **right (downstream-facing) bank is the EAST bank**.
- **E-bank high ground** (1972 contours):
  - Gaylord Cem. knoll ≈ 42.8620, -84.0256;
  - the 848-ft BM rise ≈ 42.8593, -84.0245.
  - Both are about 25-30 ft above the river (820 ft).
  - The bank between the crossing and the cemetery is the steepest slope close to the bridge, so it is the best candidate for "Knaggs' hill".
- **Gully:** the 1972 sheet shows small reentrant contours (short drainages) cutting the E bank just N and S of the crossing. At 1:24,000 none is named.
- **Ravine pin (best guess): 42.8615, -84.0295, r ≈ 350 m** (covers the E-bank slope from the crossing to the cemetery knoll). **Confidence: low-moderate.**
- Modern imagery: not checked this pass. A LiDAR hillshade (MI DTMB) would show the actual gully.

## Summary
- **Bridge:** ≈42.8610,-84.0321 (high confidence, from the marker and topo).
- **Ravine pin:** 42.8615,-84.0295 r350 (low-moderate).
- **Caveat:** the Knaggs post/bridge may instead have been nearer Owosso/Corunna. No 1870s-1890s atlas names Knaggs.

## 1875 atlas, Burns Twp plate (read by eye)
Source: *County Atlas of Shiawassee Co.* (1875), Burns T5N R4E, printed p.95 = archive.org leaf 81.
- https://archive.org/details/3928007.0001.001.umich.edu/page/n81/mode/1up
- **The crossing in sec 6 is "BURNS P.O." in 1875:** a small hamlet at the bridge, with a **"G. Mill" (grist mill) and "S. Mill" (saw mill)** on the river at the bridge, and a cemetery ("CEM.") just NE in the NW corner of sec 5. That is today's Gaylord Cem.
- **Owners around the crossing:**
  - **Sec 6:** N. G. Phillips (44.10 and 32.63 a, N edge on both banks); L. Bingham 77.80; C. Drumm 44; T. H. Reeves (79.60, plus 20.80 on the E bank by the cemetery); L. A. Warren (16½ a, E bank at the mills); N. A. Van Tuyl 6 a; J. Gardner 69.90; J. L. Case 48; G. Golden; Frac. Dist. No. 9 school land.
  - **E bank, S of the road:** J. J. Gaylord 75.85 (the "Gaylord" of the cemetery name) and C. Van Ben Schoten 77.70. These are on the E bank of the crossing, where the hill/ravine candidate lies.
  - **Sec 5:** A. G. Warren 81.20 and 76; J. E. Miller 124.22; J. Gulick 160; W. P. Parsons 160; S. Stone; J. A. Burgess.
  - **Sec 7:** Wilcox 74.20; A. Stone 43.20; W. E. Taylor 138.05; N. K. Potter; G. G. Golden; H. H. Lanckton.
- **No "Knaggs", no "Knaggs' Place", and no trading-post or store symbol** other than the mills and the P.O. cluster.

## Takeaway
- The Knaggs Bridge crossing was the **Burns P.O. mill hamlet** by 1875.
- The E-bank land next to the bridge belonged to **J. J. Gaylord** and **C. Van Ben Schoten** (and T. H. Reeves by the cemetery).
- The ravine pin (42.8615,-84.0295, r350) stays. It lies on the Gaylord / Van Ben Schoten land.
- The old mill sites at the bridge (≈ the crossing point 42.8610,-84.0321) are a secondary point of interest.

## 1873 Walling state-atlas Shiawassee map (archive.org dr_map-of-shiawassee-county-michigan-drawn-compiled-and-edited-by-hf-wa-00734140; David Rumsey copy)
- Small scan (1248×1536). Read zoomed on N Burns twp.
- Shows **"Burns P.O."** at the Shiawassee crossing in sec 6 (the bridge spot). Right below it, along the river, is the label **"Indian Reservation"**, spanning the N part of Burns twp along the Shiawassee.
  - This is the old treaty reserve tract. The 1819 Saginaw treaty reserves on the Shiawassee are the setting for Knaggs the trader / interpreter.
  - The atlas thus ties the crossing to the reservation lands.
- **No "Knaggs" name, mill or post symbol** at this scale beyond "Burns P.O.".
- **Net:** this supports Burns P.O. = the Knaggs Bridge locale. Nothing finer.

## Newspaper corroboration (newspapers.com, 2026-11-01)
- **Owosso Times 1892-02-12 p1**, Bancroft column: "Mrs. Chas. DRUMM, of KNAGGS' BRIDGE, died on Sunday... an old pioneer of this county". https://www.newspapers.com/image/174207471/
  - The 1875 Burns plate shows **C. Drumm 44 a in sec 6**, at the crossing. So "Knaggs' Bridge" = the sec 6 Burns P.O. crossing.
- **Owosso Times 1897-12-24 p8**, Bancroft column (from the Commercial): "Roberts Bros. have traded their MILLING PROPERTY AT KNAGGS BRIDGE to a J. Cole for 260 acres... near Crystal Lake". https://www.newspapers.com/image/174813034/
  - This matches the 1875 G. Mill / S. Mill at the sec 6 crossing.
- **Net:** two independent local mentions put Knaggs Bridge at the Burns P.O. mill hamlet (≈42.8610,-84.0321).
  - The bridge pin moves from "best available, not proven" to **confirmed**. The ravine pin is unchanged.
  - "Knaggs buried" / "Knaggs treasure" in Shiawassee 1870-1960 found no treasure article (the hits are Knaggs-family locals and these two items).
