# Irvin Meyer "Gold Mine" farm, W of McGregor, Clayton Co. IA (1937) (2026-11-01)

Reads were view-only.

## Sources
- **No 1920s-40s Clayton Co. plat is open online.** IAGenWeb Clayton has only Grand Meadow plats and the 1965 farm directory. archive.org has only the 1872 and 1875 Andreas maps.
- **1965 Clayton Co. Farm Directory** (IAGenWeb, land/1965_m.htm, plus the Giard twp map giard_65.jpg):
  - "Meyer, Irvin, McGregor 1, **Giard 26**, O(wner)".
  - Also: "Meyer, Maurice, McGregor 1, Giard 26, R(enter)" and "Meyer, G.N., McGregor 1, Giard 26, O".
  - The map labels the parcel "**Maurice & Irvin Meyer**", NW-central **sec 26, Giard Twp (T95N R4W)**. G. N. Meyer is on the adjoining E part of sec 26. A house square is shown.
- **Note:** this is **Giard Twp**, which lies W of Mendon Twp (McGregor). It is about 6 mi W of McGregor, which fits "west of McGregor" but not "Mendon Twp". It is a 1965 owner listing; the 1937 owner is not confirmed by a contemporary plat.
