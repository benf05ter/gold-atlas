## What the plate shows along the Wolf River near Lily
- The riverbank is almost all **corporate timber land**: Menasha Wooden Ware Co., Wallrich & Anderson, Hatten Lumber Co., C&NW and St.P.E.G.T. RR, Wolf River Paper & Fiber Co., and Shawano Lumber Co.
- Individual owners near Lily: Wm. Priem, Geo. Baldwin Est., A. Smith 119.5 (sec 27, E of the village), Jesse/Louis/Archie Pendleton, P. M. Jenski, M. J. Wallrich, Jos. Goldberg, Chas. Walsh, Gus Berndt, Albano, Jas. McHale.
- **"A. Smith 119.5"** sits in sec 27, just NE of Lily village. That is a different initial; the 1939 victim used the alias "John Smith". It's noted, but probably unrelated.
- **No Warnick. No J./John Smith.**
