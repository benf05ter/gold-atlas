# kmr-flemming-road-yellow-banks: Empire Lumber "Flemming Road" landing at Yellow Banks on the St. Croix (2026-10-31)

**Text:** King leaf 62-63. Empire's line ran "a few miles to the southeast of the O'Neal Brothers' logging railroad", from Crooked and Sand creeks to "the landing at Yellow Banks on the St. Croix River"; gear came via Grantsburg WI.

**Place-name hits (OSM / Nominatim), all in Crosby Twp, Pine Co. MN, across the river from Burnett Co. WI:**
- **"Little Yellow Banks"** canoe landing/slipway on the St. Croix, ≈ **45.9754, -92.5416**.
- **"Yellow Banks Drive"** and **"Yellow Banks CCC Camp"** (ruins) in St. Croix State Park, ≈ 45.9415, -92.6040.
- **GNIS** has no MN or St. Croix "Yellow Banks"; only the WI Juneau, Price and Portage entries.
- The **1916 Pine Co. plat** (UMedia) shows no "Yellow Banks" label (earlier check, research/crossref/umedia-plats.md).
