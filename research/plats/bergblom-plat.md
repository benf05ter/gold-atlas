# Bergblom (Carl G.), Redwood Co. MN: plat and 1938 aerial (2026-10-31)

**Plat** (earlier, research/crossref/umedia-plats.md, "Redwood County" section):
- **Carl G. Bergblom 140** = the W part of the SW¼ of **sec 11 T109N R39W**, with a house dot near the S line.
- Family land: S. J. and Aug. Bergblom in sec 14 to the S.
- UMedia also has the 1914 atlas (14052) and the 1930 *Atlas & farmers' directory of Redwood Co.* (8424); both carry the Bergblom name.
- The pin 44.2591,-95.5079 falls inside sec 11 (BLM: -95.5130..-95.4927, 44.2536..44.2684).

**1938 aerial: MHAPO bse01038 (8-4-38, indexed centre 44.2390,-95.5014).**
- https://geo.lib.umn.edu/dnr_historical_aerial_imagery/airphotos/usda/bse/y1938/bse01038.jpg
- Sec 11's S edge is at the top of the frame. The E-W road is the S line; the N-S roads are the W and E lines (about 455 px/mile in my view). Accuracy ±60 m.
- **Farmstead 1:** a grove-ringed yard with house, barn and outbuildings. It is in the **W part of the SW¼, about 0.15 mi E of the W-line road and about 0.08 mi N of the S-line road**, with a drive S to the road. This is **≈ 44.2547, -95.5099**, and it matches the plat's house dot on the Carl G. Bergblom 140.
- **Farmstead 2:** just E of the N-S half line, near the S road (≈44.2543,-95.5023). This is the SW corner of the SE¼; not Bergblom per the plat.
- **The pin 44.2591,-95.5079 is on open cropland** about 500 m NNE of farmstead 1. No buildings are at the pin.

**Suggested pin:** 44.2547, -95.5099, r ≈ 100 m. Confidence: good. The plat dot and the 1938 farmstead agree.
