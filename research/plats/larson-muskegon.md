## Follow-up: 1911 sheet scan (2026-11-01)
I scanned sheets 1-8 (the W. Western Ave mill district) and 63-64.

- **Sheets 2/64 (Michigan Ave, between Franklin and Hudson):** Muskegon Traction & Lighting Co. **car barns and repair shop** only. No generating plant.
- **Sheet 8:** this shows the **Muskegon Traction & Lighting Co. power and gas plant**:
  - boiler and engine houses, a gas holder and a coal pile;
  - **between the P.M. RR / W. Western Ave on the N and Morris St on the S, at about N. 2nd-3rd Sts, just W of the Indian Cemetery.**
  - Across Morris St to the S are small shops and buildings (Walker Candy Co. and others).
- **Best candidate for "the electric lighting plant" (as of 1911):** the plant site at about **43.2340, -86.2550**, r150. That is W of the Muskegon Indian Cemetery (W. Morris Ave at 2nd St).
  - The Larson shop would be "across the street", most likely on the S side of Morris St or along N. 2nd/3rd St.
- **Caveat:**
  - This is a 1911 map. A 1902 municipal or separate light plant may have stood elsewhere, and the article's "outskirts" does not fit a downtown site well.
  - The Lakeside sheets (83-90) were not scanned within the cap.
- **Confidence C-.** The 1902-03 city directory (Sunday list) would settle it.
