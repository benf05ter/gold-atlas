## Job 21: Ross shop on N. Terrace St, the light station opposite, gas main (2026-11-01)
Sources: LoC Sanborn Muskegon **Nov. 1887 sheet 7** (index: "Electric Light Works ... 7") and **1911 Vol. 1 sheet 9**. Read by eye, view-only.

**1887 sheet 7 (N. Terrace between Market St and Lake St, block 4 / block 6):**
- **(b) Electric light station:** "ELECTRIC LIGHT WKS., 3 dynamos", a brick building with an engine/boiler room, on the **E side of N. Terrace, mid-block between Market St (S) and Lake St (N)**, in block 4. It is about 300 ft N of Market St and about 430 ft N of W. Western Ave.
- **(a) Woodworking shops facing it:**
  - **Directly opposite, W side of N. Terrace (block 6): "Heap's Patent Earth Closet Co."**, a frame woodworking factory (wood-working, sand-papering, varnishing; dry kiln; brick boiler/engine room). This is the building literally "across the street" from the light works.
  - Also on the E side, **next N of the light works: "Kelly Bros Mfg Co., woodworking machinery"** and a "Sash, Door & Blind Fac.", with a lumber yard behind.
  - G. R. Ross is not named in 1887 (the shop changed hands later). The Heap's building is the best match for a woodworking shop opposite the electric plant; Kelly Bros is the alternative.
- **1911 sheet 9:** "Ross Mfg Co., Mfg Card Cases & Filing Devices" (woodworking, finishing, painting, dry kiln, engine room) is at **120 Water St, N side, at the head of N. Terrace**, with Moon Desk Co. warehouse at 122. That is the later Ross plant at the N end of the street, a block or so N of the 1887 pair. It may be the 1902 boat-works site rather than the c.1901 turning shop.
- **(c) Gas main:** **none shown.** Sanborns draw water pipes only ("6 in. W.P." along Market and N. Terrace, also on Water St). The City Gas Works is on 1887 sheet 16, elsewhere in town; gas mains are not mapped on these sheets.

**Coordinates.** N. Terrace was later realigned N of Western Ave. Today Terrace St crosses W. Western at **43.2383, -86.2480** and curves N to Shoreline Dr. The historic street ran straight on from the S-of-Western alignment (bearing about 327°). Measuring along it with the sheet's 150 ft scale bar:
- **Electric Light Works (1887 sh. 7): 43.2394, -86.2487, r50.** Confidence C+.
- **Heap's woodworking shop, opposite (1887 sh. 7): 43.2392, -86.2492, r50.** Confidence C.
- **Pin for the pair (Larson/Ross shop + light station): 43.2393, -86.2489, r75.** Confidence C+. It is about 50 m from the modern Terrace St curve.
- **Ross Mfg Co. at the head of N. Terrace, 120 Water St (1911 sh. 9): about 43.2405, -86.2500, r100.** Confidence C, because the street was realigned and Water St is now Shoreline Dr.

**Note:** this downtown pair is a better fit than the 1911 Traction & Lighting plant (43.2340, -86.2550) above, because the 1887 light works stands on N. Terrace itself. The 1902 newspaper "outskirts" wording still does not fit well. The 1902-03 city directory would confirm the address.
