# Bertrand bushel of gold: old Bertrand village, Bertrand Twp, Berrien Co. MI (plat queue job 6, 2026-11-01)

Reads were view-only.

Sources:
- **1873 *Atlas of Berrien Co.*** (archive.org 2911281.0001.001.umich.edu), "Plan of Bertrand", pp. 82-83 = leaf 76.
- **1887 *Atlas of Berrien County*** (2911279.0001.001.umich.edu), Bertrand Twp plate, leaf 59 (T8S R17W and R18W).

## 1873 village plan
- A full town grid of about 80 numbered blocks lies on the N bank of the St. Joseph River, which makes an S-bend along the S edge of the plat.
  - E-W streets: Congress, Jefferson, Adams, Washington, Kosciusko, Pulaski.
  - N-S streets: Michigan, Huron, Hudson, Madeline, Bertrand, Chicago, Garney, Silsbe, Berris, Erie, Ontario.
- Most blocks are empty. Buildings cluster in blocks 8, 16-17, 20-22, 29 and 32 along Bertrand and Chicago Sts; a store is in block 21.
  - Lot owners include J. Lowrain, D. L. Stoneman, Hoffman, D. C. Higbee, Pratt, Bennett, W. Batson, Mrs. Gardner and others.
- **Bertrand family:**
  - **"J. Bertrand" in block 32** (between Silsbe and Berris, Jefferson-Adams), with a house mark.
  - **"Est. of J. Bertrand 52a"**: the land inside the river's S-bend directly S of the village grid (S of Kosciusko St, W of the river loop). This is the riverside tract.
- A **Catholic church and cemetery** occupy block 13 (W end, near the river). "Jas. Hilton 15a" lies across the river to the SE.
- There is **no orchard symbol**. The atlas does not draw orchards, so "the old orchard" cannot be placed from this plan.

## 1887 plate
- The village is no longer platted; the grid area is farmland (Christian Ditmer 89, M. B. Gillette, John F. Vetter, Geo. F. Basmer 30, Wm. Copp). This lies on the E edge of the township, in **secs 10, 14 and 15 T8S R17W**, at the river meander.
- There is no Bertrand name on the 1887 plate.

## Coordinates
- Today's "Bertrand" place node (Nominatim) is at **41.7745, -86.2622**, on the river at the sec 14/15 line. That is the village site.
- Sec 15 T8S R17W (BLM) runs -86.2843 to -86.2644 and 41.7692 to 41.7837.
- **Pins:**
  - **Village site: 41.7745, -86.2622, r300.** Confidence B. The place node matches the plan's river S-bend.
  - **Est. of J. Bertrand 52a (riverside land in the bend, S of the village): about 41.7720, -86.2640, r300.** Confidence C. Its position is relative to the bend; the 1873 plan has no scale tie to the section lines.
- The 1898 dig in "the old orchard" stays unresolved below the village and estate. The likeliest ground is the J. Bertrand estate land by the river, but that is not confirmed.
