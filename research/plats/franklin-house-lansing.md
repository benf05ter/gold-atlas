# Franklin House hotel, North Lansing (Old Town), Lansing MI: placement (2026-11-01)

Source: **Sanborn-Perris, Lansing, June 1898, sheet 21** (LoC `sanborn04071_003`). The index lists "Franklin Hotel ... 21". Read view-only from the LoC IIIF image.

## What the sheet shows
- **"FRANKLIN HO."** is a two-storey frame hotel. The ground-floor "Barber B." is at the N end.
- Address: **414 Franklin St. E.**
- Position: the **SW corner of E. Franklin St. and Center St.**, at the E edge of **block 13** (North Lansing).
- It is a long building running about 110-120 ft S down the W side of Center St, which is marked "80 ft wide".
- A "bridge" links the rear of the hotel to an outbuilding on the W. The rest of the block runs S to Wall St.
- On the same block frontage, from the W: 402 jewelry/photo, then the 404-410 brick row (tailor, B&S, vacant).
- No proprietor name is printed on the sheet, so George Lovely is not confirmed from it. The 1892 sheets were not read.

## Today
- Franklin St. E. later became E. Grand River Ave, and is now **E. César E. Chávez Ave**.
- Center St is still N. Center St.
- From OSM reverse-geocoding along Center St, the Chávez Ave crossing is at about 42.7469, -84.548.
- **Proposed pin: 42.7467, -84.5487, r40.** That is the SW corner of E. César E. Chávez Ave and N. Center St, running S along the W side of Center.
- **Confidence B+.** The building, corner and street address come straight from the Sanborn sheet. The modern coordinate is from street geocoding, ±30 m.
- **Note:** the 1896 "south cellar" would be under the S part of the long Center St wing.
