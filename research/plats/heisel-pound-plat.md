## Reading
- Two Heisel parcels have 1938 farmsteads:
  - **Gus Heisel 50** (sites A/B/C, W of the highway);
  - **Ludwig Heisel 30** (site D, just E of the highway).
- "West of Pound" (1906) favours the **W-of-highway cluster (Gus Heisel 50: A/B/C)**. The highway runs S out of the village, so "west" of the road from Pound fits.
- **Suggested primary pin:** site B, ≈45.0860,-88.0343, r~120 m to cover A-C.
- **Alternate:** site D, 45.0851,-88.0329.
- **Confidence:** moderate. The 1912 parcel boundaries are approximate. B and C may belong to one farm or to two neighbours, and the 1906 home site isn't proven to be one of these.
