## County Road 6 / the 1927 ad
- "3 mi S and 3 mi E of Waldorf; 8 mi W of New Richland" puts the point at about **43.890-43.894, -93.636 to -93.654**: the S edge of sec 13 or just E of it.
- The **E-W road on the S line of secs 13/14 (lat ≈ 43.8915)** runs straight E toward New Richland in the 1938 photo. It is **most likely County Road 6**, but that is **unverified** (no 1930s county road map was checked).
- On that reading, the two farmsteads facing it near the ad point are:
  - the **SW-corner cluster of sec 13** (43.8920,-93.6668), and
  - the **Twite SE¼ sec 14 farmstead** (43.8946,-93.6769), about 0.6 mi further W.
- Neither is a full "3 E" from Waldorf. The ad distances are rounded; "3 mi E" measured from Waldorf village (about -93.696) lands nearer the sec 13/18 township line.

## Reading
- In 1914 the Twite land was only the SE¼ of sec 14, and that quarter had a farmstead in 1938.
- If the 1927 auction was on sec 13 land, Tweit must have bought into sec 13 after 1914 (from McArdle, Gadient or Tellett). A 1920s Waseca plat would be needed to check; none was found on UMedia (only 1896, 1914 and 1937). The 1937 plate showed "Mamie Hanson 160" on the S½ sec 14 tract.
- **Recommendation:** keep the SE¼ sec 14 farmstead (43.8946,-93.6769) as the Vivian pin. Note the sec 13 SW-corner cluster as an alternate if the auction ad's location is taken literally.
