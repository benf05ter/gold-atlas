# Superior post-office loot (Nov 1925), Arcklet farm 1.5 mi E of Bennett, Douglas Co. WI (2026-10-31)

## Plat
**Source:** *Plat Book of Douglas County* (WHS W01B8D6, c.1920-23), sheet "Townships 46 North".
- https://explore.wishistory.org/AssetLink/qj811r5hk0o25iipq553h772y3bg7i4k.jpg
- **Correction:** Bennett village is on the Soo/StP RR in **sec 22 T46N R12W** (the plate is labelled "BENNETT"), not R13W.
- BLM: sec 22 -91.8615..-91.8406; sec 23 -91.8407..-91.8200; sec 24 -91.8201..-91.7994; all three span lat 46.4458..46.4600.
- **1.5 mi E of the village** lands about **-91.817**, i.e. the W part of **sec 24** (or the E edge of sec 23).
- **No Arcklet / Areklet / Arckle** anywhere in secs 22-26. The 45-acre purchase probably postdates this plat.

**Swamp:**
- A dark conifer (swamp-type) timber block lies N of the road **between B and C**, covering the SW-central part of sec 24.
- **Nearest swamp edge to B ≈ 46.4466, -91.8178** (~100 m E of B).
- More swamp/wet timber extends N across secs 23-24.

## Reading
- "1.5 mi E on STH 11" plus "a deserted farmhouse next to her farm, near a swamp" fits **B (deserted) beside C or A**, with the swamp block between B and C.
- Agnes Arcklet's 45 acres were likely one of the sec 23/24 parcels along the S road. The plat predates her purchase, so the owner isn't proven.
- **Suggested pins:**
  - Arcklet farm: C, 46.4467,-91.8134, r250 (or A as the alternate).
  - Deserted farmhouse: B, 46.4463,-91.8191, r100.
  - Swamp edge: 46.4466,-91.8178, r150.
- **Confidence: low-moderate.** The STH 11 routing and the names are unverified.
