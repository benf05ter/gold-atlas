# Conrad May farm, Louisburg, Town of Jamestown, Grant Co. WI (2026-11-01)

Lead: Conrad May admitted in a Dubuque divorce/alimony case (1897) that he had buried his money on his Wisconsin farm. A squirrel-hunting neighbour's dog was said to have dug $40,000-$50,000, mostly gold, out of a gopher hole (Grant County Witness 1897-10-06 p1, from the Dubuque Times: "the reigning sensation in Dickeyville, and all the other little towns in Grant County"). Treasure: HELD D. The Cuba City News denied the account (Grant County Herald 1897-10-14). See the leads.tsv Conrad May row.

Source (view-only): WHS c.1915 Hixson *Plat Book of the State of Wisconsin* (W017VNM), Grant Co. plates:
- Page 92, "Town of Paris, Smelzer, Ellenboro, Platteville, Little Grant, and Fennimore" (Paris = T2N R2W). Read in full. **No May.** Dickeyville ("Dickeysville") is in sec 22 of Paris.
- Page 94, "Town of Wyalusing, Jamestown, Potosi, Patch Grove, and Glenhaven": https://explore.wishistory.org/AssetLink/675727714463bo43l4kvmb5cccr5sgtj.jpg (7970 x 5775). Jamestown NE quarter read.

## Parcel (c.1915)
- **"CONRAD MAY 361"**: the large S and W part of **SEC 12, T1N R2W (Town of Jamestown)**.
- **"ELMER MAY 160"**: the NE¼ of sec 12 (a family member, adjoining).
- **LOUISBURG** village sits on the N edge of sec 12 at the 11/12 line, with a church (cross) on the N line. Jamestown village is in sec 1 just N.
- Neighbours: Wm. Dreesen 51 (W, by Louisburg), Fred Klaas 120 and Jno. Weiderholt (sec 11), Maurice Quinlan, Frank Plocke, Jno. C. Splinter (sec 13 S).
- **House mark:** a dot just W of the N-S road, N of the "12" label, inside the May land and about 400 m S of Louisburg. That's the probable May farmstead.
