  - Owners near the village (secs 29-33) include W. L. Hall, Hiram Clark, W. A. Thomas, Geo. Stark, J. W. Thomas, Matt Olson and Bert Emerson.

- **Confidence: LOW** (town-level only). Settle with an 1890s Jackson plat (Foote 1898?) or Black River Falls papers on WHS film.

## Pin 4: Zitzner, "3 mi W of Liberty Pole" (Vernon Co. WI), murder 1899
- **Plat:** Hixson c.1915, Vernon sheet p278, Town of Franklin W part (T12N R5W). Liberty Pole village is printed in sec 25 (E edge of T12N R5W, next to the R4W line).
- **Zitzner family cluster, c.1915:**
  - **Sec 35:** F. G. Zitzner 135 (N part) and Geo. Zitzner 140 (centre), each with building dots.
  - **Sec 2 (T11N, S):** Geo. Zitzner 186.42 and Fred Zitzner 160.
  - **Sec 3:** Ed. Zitzner.
  - This is the only Zitzner land in the W part of Franklin. It lies about 1.5-2 mi SW of Liberty Pole, not a full 3 mi W, but newspaper distances were loose.
- **BLM bounds:**
  - sec 35 T12N R5W: -90.9482..-90.9281, 43.4667..43.4813;
  - sec 25: -90.9282..-90.9084, 43.4813..43.4957.
- **Proposed pin:** 43.4745, -90.9385 (centre of the sec 35 Zitzner parcels), r600. It covers both sec 35 farmsteads; the sec 2 tracts lie ~0.5-1 mi S.
- **Confidence: MEDIUM-LOW.** The family land is located, but the 1899 victim's own parcel and house aren't identified. Next check: the 1896 Vernon plat (if found) or the 1899 article text naming the farm.
- **Aerial:** the WHAIFinder 1938-39 Vernon frame is not yet checked.
- **Coordinator decision (17:07Z):** Zitzner is the neighbour named in the 1899 Mulchrone report, not a separate lead. The Mulchrone pin stays at 43.4610,-90.9347 r450 (1896 plat house dot, sec 2 T11N R5W). The c.1915 cluster corroborates it, so no move.
- **Randall:** stays town-level.

## Pin 8: Horrigan brothers, 1930 treasure-hunter murders (Crawford Co. WI)
- **Placement from the newspapers:** "near Mt. Zion" and "between Mt. Zion and Soldiers Grove".
- **Plat:** Hixson c.1915, Crawford sheet p45 (WHS W017VNM, AssetLink url index 135), Town of Scott (T10N R3W).
  - **"Jno. Horrigan 167.68"** across the N tier of **sec 2** (W and centre of the N half). That is the only Horrigan found in Scott's N half.
  - Neighbours: Floyd Owens 42.84 and 32.92 (NE), Henry Lightfoot 60 and Chas. Owens 100 (S), Ellen Miller 80 (sec 1). Wheatville is in sec 9.
  - "Jno." is probably the father of Cornelius (60 in 1930) and Stephen.
- **BLM sec 2 T10N R3W:** -90.7061..-90.6861, 43.3645..43.3796.
- **Proposed pin:** **43.376, -90.700**, r450 (the N half of sec 2, W of centre).
  - This is about 4.5 mi SE of Soldiers Grove, which fits "between Mt. Zion and Soldiers Grove". The Mt. Zion position was not independently checked.
- **Aerial:** the WHAIFinder query at this spot returned only a 2023 frame; no 1937-41 frame is indexed here.
- **Confidence: MEDIUM.** The family name is on the right quarter-section area 15 years earlier. The 1930 owner and house site are unconfirmed.

## Pin 9: Rudolph "gold" prospect: Jesse Waters 80 and Herman Bates 40 (Wood Co. WI), 1884-94
- **Placement from the newspaper:** "a farm about a mile east of the Rudolph post office".
- **Plat:** Hixson c.1915, Wood sheet p316 (url index 948), Town of Rudolph (T23N R6E).
  - The "RUDOLPH P.O." label is in S sec 4.
  - Bates family land in 1915: **A. Bates 40 = SW¼ NE¼ sec 10**, J. Bates 60 (NW sec 3) and J. Bates 20 (E edge sec 9).
  - A. Bates 40 in sec 10 is about 1.1 mi E-SE of the P.O., which matches "a mile east".
  - The adjoining Waters 80 is not named in 1915. The candidates are the unnamed 40s in sec 10 (W of Bates: E. J. Duncan 40, plus blank 40s) and C. Waterman 40 / A. Zimmerman 40 just N.
- **BLM sec 10 T23N R6E:** -89.7875..-89.7670, 44.4816..44.4961.
- **Proposed pin:** **44.4907, -89.7746** (SW¼ NE¼ sec 10, the A. Bates 40), r400 to cover the adjoining 80.
- **Confidence: MEDIUM-LOW.** The 1894 Herman Bates 40 is likely the same family tract; Waters is not on the 1915 plat.
- The c.1884 prospect pits would be on the Waters 80 next to Bates. Next check: an 1890s Wood Co. plat, or the 1894 Wood County Reporter follow-ups.
