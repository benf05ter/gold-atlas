# Dexfield Park, Union Twp (T78N R29W), Dallas Co IA (PC b, 2026-11-02)

- Ancestry 1127 has **Dallas 1901** (31617_072141-00882) and **Dallas 1916** (31617_072141-00829). There is no 1920s-30s Dallas plat book in 1127.
- **Dallas 1916 (52 images):** after the county outline map (viewer p.3), the images are farmers' directories and gazetteer pages: Beaver p.6, Dallas p.10, Washington p.14, Grant p.18, Adel p.22, Linn p.26, Adams p.30, Boone p.34, "Parts of Union, Adams, Van Meter and Boone Townships" p.36, then tables and portraits. **I found no township plates in the pages sampled**, so no "Dexfield Park" label can be read from this item. The Union Twp directory gives owners by section (e.g. Dexter P.O. entries), but I don't know the park owner's name to match against.
- The 1901 atlas predates the park (it opened about 1915), so it would only show the earlier owner of the tract. Not opened.
- **Correction (second pass):** the 1916 item **does** have plates, on odd viewer pages (directories are on even pages). **Union Twp T78N R29W = viewer p.29, image 31617_072141-00858** (Anderson Publishing Co., Mason City). Redfield is in secs 9-10; Scotia is SE of it.
  - Secs 8, 9, 16 and 17 (W and SW of Redfield, by the river) show owners **H. L. Massare** (sec 9), **Lafayette De Ford** (secs 16-17), A. Cooper 100 and **W. O. Leeper** (sec 16 by the river), Jacob Feller, T. H. Quigley, and the "Redfield No. 1" school.
  - **No "Dexfield", "Marshall", "Marshall Springs" or park label** and no Valley Junction-owned tract are seen in those sections (the park was only being funded in 1915, so the 1916 plat may predate the sale).
- **Not placed** (owner of record about 1916 on the likely tract: W. O. Leeper / Lafayette De Ford, sec 16; unconfirmed).
