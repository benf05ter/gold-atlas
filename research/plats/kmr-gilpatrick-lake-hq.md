# kmr-gilpatrick-lake-hq: Gull Lake & Northern HQ at Gilpatrick Lake (2026-10-31)

**Identity of the lake:** King, *Minnesota Logging Railroads*, leaf 51 says: "the log dump at Gilpatrick Lake (now known as Lake Margaret)". The logs were rafted across Gull Lake and down the Gull River to the mill. Notes: research/books/locator-recheck-2026-10-31.md.

**Lake Margaret:** OSM water feature, Lake Shore, Cass Co. MN, ≈ **46.4953, -94.3640**. It lies immediately N of Gull Lake, which fits the rafting route.
- The GNIS "Lake Margaret" in Pine Co. (46.348,-92.586) is a different lake.
- GNIS has no Gilpatrick in MN.

**HQ / landing:** not drawn on any map found. King's own maps don't show it.
