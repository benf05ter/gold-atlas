# "Grohe swamp" near the C. Dolph farm, Bellevue Twp, Eaton Co. MI (2026-11-01)

Reads were view-only.

## Sources
- 1913 Ogle *Standard Atlas of Eaton County*, Bellevue plate (archive.org fl-3657716-756450, leaf 44, printed p.47).
- USGS US Topo basemap (current), bbox -85.022 to -84.985, 42.428 to 42.455. Pixel positions were converted to lat/long with a linear georeference, ±40 m.

## 1913 plate
- There are **no swamp or marsh symbols** on the plate. Ogle does not draw them here.
- **J. S. Grohe** owns:
  - land in the **W/central part of sec 27, S of the Battle Creek River**, directly W of C. Dolph 20 (SE¼ sec 27);
  - **"J. S. Grohe (Allen & Sharkey) 160"** in **N sec 34**, directly S across the 27/34 line road (today Butterfield Hwy / M-78).
- So any "Grohe swamp" must lie on one of these two blocks, which flank the Dolph 20 on the W and S.

## Wetlands on the USGS topo (marsh symbols)
1. **Directly N/NE of the Dolph parcel** (S half of sec 27, between the farm and the Battle Creek River): a marsh with two small ponds. Its nearest edge to Dolph is at about **42.4381, -84.9994**, only ~50 m N of the Dolph pin (42.4376, -84.9993). Most of it lies on 1913 Davis/Anson land, with the W part toward the Grohe sec 27 land.
2. **S of Butterfield Hwy in N sec 34, on the Grohe (Allen & Sharkey) 160:** a large wooded wetland. Its nearest edge to Dolph is at about **42.4353, -84.9989**, ~250 m S across the road. **This is the wetland actually on Grohe land, and the best fit for "Grohe swamp".**

## Suggestion
- Keep the Dolph pin 42.4376, -84.9993. Tighten the radius toward the swamp edge:
  - **Grohe sec 34 swamp edge 42.4353, -84.9989** (best fit by owner name); or
  - the N marsh edge 42.4381, -84.9994, if the story's swamp adjoined the farm on the N.
- A radius of **r200** covers the Dolph 20 plus both swamp edges. Confidence C+: the owner name fits, but the swamp's extent has changed since 1913.
