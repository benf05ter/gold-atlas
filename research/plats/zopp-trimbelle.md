# Zopp farm, Trimbelle Twp T26N R18W, Pierce Co. WI

## Plat + 1939 aerial check (2026-10-31)

### Which 80? The answer is **S½ NE¼ sec 30**, not S½ NW¼
**Source:** 1909 Ogle *Standard Atlas of Pierce County* (WHS W0171UC), Trimbelle plate p.37: https://explore.wishistory.org/AssetLink/332mn7v38vsn2oabbi776c3l7o5ur2y2.jpg

Sec 30 is fractional on the W (range-line correction). The plate shows it as two columns.

**W column (fractional W½), four ~78-acre rows, N to S:**
- **Thos. Beddall 78.3 Est**: N½ NW¼.
- **John Huppert 78.1**: S½ NW¼.
- Jas. Grady 77.9: N½ SW¼.
- Mat Huppert Est 77.7: S½ SW¼.

**E column, separated by a N-S road on the quarter line:**
- an unnamed "80": N½ NE¼.
- **"Michl Zopp 80": S½ NE¼.**
- Ed Smith and Matt Huppert 40s: SE¼.

**Building marks:**
- The **Zopp house mark is at the W edge of his 80, just E of the N-S quarter-line road**, about mid-height of the S½ NE¼.
- John Huppert's mark sits just W of the same road.

**So the current pin (S½ NW¼) is on JOHN HUPPERT's 80. Move it E across the road.**
- This matches Neb's upload ("E of Jno Huppert 78.1 (W fractional strip), Thos Beddall Est 78.3 above"); Beddall is above Huppert, not above Zopp.

### 1939 aerial (WHAIFinder Pierce BRW 5-15, flown 6-20-39)
https://web.s3.wisc.edu/rml-aerial-imagery/counties/WI/Pierce/1939/Pierce_WI_BRW_5_15_19390620.jpg

**Georeference:**
- Road grid: the range-line road (W edge of sec 30), the N-S quarter-line road, the E-W road on the N line of sec 30, and the E-W quarter-line road. Spacing ≈ 362 px per mile on my 1100-px view.
- Anchored to BLM sec 30 bounds: **-92.6190..-92.5989, 44.7031..44.7176**.
- Accuracy is about ±80 m.

**Farmsteads in the N½ of sec 30:**
- **One farmstead with a grove**, just **W** of the N-S quarter-line road, about 0.36 mi S of the N line. **≈ 44.7124, -92.6095.** That is on the Huppert side (S½ NW¼), at the road.
- **No separate farmstead E of the road in the S½ NE¼ (Zopp)** in 1939. The tract is open fields. By 1939 the Zopp 80 was likely farmed from across the road.
- A farmstead just N of the N line (sec 19) sits at the quarter-road junction. Others are in sec 29 to the E.

### Pin advice
- **Zopp 80 = S½ NE¼ sec 30.**
  - Centroid ≈ **44.7121, -92.6040**.
  - 1909 house-mark spot (W edge by the quarter road) ≈ **44.7112, -92.6085** (±100 m).
- Move the pin from the S½ NW¼ to the house-mark spot, with a radius covering the W part of the S½ NE¼.
- **Michael Zopp died c. mid-1907** (River Falls Journal 1907-07-11), so the 1909 "Michl Zopp" label is the estate holding.

## Pin check vs 1939 aerial (2026-10-31): live pin 44.7112,-92.6085 r150
- On WHAIFinder Pierce BRW 5-15 (6-20-39), **the S½ NE¼ sec 30 has no buildings**; it is open fields.
- The **only farmstead in the N½** is just **W** of the N-S quarter-line road, at **≈44.7124, -92.6095** (the John Huppert side, S½ NW¼).
  - That is ~150 m NW of the pin, at the edge of r150.
- The pin sits on the 1909 Zopp house-mark spot (W edge of the Zopp 80, just E of the road).
  - By 1939 that spot was field. Either the Zopp house was gone, or the plat mark and the 1939 farmstead across the road are the same building group, displaced by the engraver.
- **Suggest:** keep the pin. r150 already touches the 1939 farmstead; r ~200 m would cover both cleanly.
- **Date note:** River Falls Journal 1907-07-11 implies Michael Zopp died in mid-1907, not 1912.
