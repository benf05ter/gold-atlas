## Follow-up: 1937 aerial (WHAIFinder, Washington Co. XH 5-475, 7 Aug 1937)
- The frame covers Pike Lake (top) S to the Hartford-Erin town-line road (bottom). Its scale is about 170 display px per mile at the 1000 px preview, i.e. about 1.6 m per original pixel.
- **Woods seen:**
  1. A long, irregular **wooded creek valley** running SW to NE through the centre-left of the frame, from the town-line road N toward Pike Lake's S shore. This fits the creek the plat shows crossing secs 34-35 and running up past the E. L. Phillips land.
  2. A **large rectangular woodlot** just N of the town-line road, W of the first N-S section road. If that road is the 35/36 line, the woodlot is in the SE part of sec 35.
  3. Smaller woodlots scattered to the E (secs 25-36).
- **Georeference is not good enough to name one woodlot at half a mile.** WHAIFinder gives only the frame centre (43.2930, -88.3277, ±200-400 m). I could not tie the road grid to the plat section lines with confidence: the diagonal Hartford-Holy Hill road is not clearly traceable on this frame, and frame 5-434 to the W was not fetched.
- **Best qualitative read:** the Schauer 80 (N½ SW¼ sec 35) sits just E of the wooded creek valley. Woods in that valley, about ¼-¾ mi NW of the farm in secs 34/27, are the most likely "woods nearby".
- **Not proposed as a pin** under the no-guessing rule. To do this properly: georeference 5-475 plus 5-434 against BLM section lines (sec 35 runs -88.3400 to -88.3199 and 43.2814 to 43.2959).
