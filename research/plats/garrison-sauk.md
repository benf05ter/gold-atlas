# Garrison (vanished village), Lower Narrows of the Baraboo River, Town of Greenfield, Sauk Co. WI (2026-11-01)

Ask: cr-garrison-village-sauk (grade C). A vanished village at the Lower Narrows of the Baraboo River (source: Baraboo Weekly News 1912).

## 1877 map
- **"GARRISON"** is printed beside a small platted block (hatched lots) on the **W bank of the Baraboo River where it turns N through the narrows**. The block sits on the **sec 27/26 line in the N part of the sections**, in the top (N) tier of the Town of Greenfield, which is T12N R7E (secs 25-30 on the S edge of Fairfield's tier).
- A **"Saw Mill"** is labelled just E/NE of the village in sec 26, on the river (the mill dam site).
- The river then runs N into Fairfield (secs 22-23), past the marsh, toward the Columbia Co. line.

## c.1915 plat (same spot)
- No village name (gone by 1915). The river runs N along the sec 26/27 line. There is a church and cemetery ("Ch", "Cem") at the S line of the N part of sec 26 (F. Luebke 76.10), and a road crossing at the narrows. Owners: C. Sigglekow 136 and Wm. Brown 100.80 (sec 26), F. Karg and A. Weiland (secs 35/34), F. Luebke.
