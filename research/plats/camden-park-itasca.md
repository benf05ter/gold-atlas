# Camden Park bank loot, Itasca Co. MN: trapper's 80 (plat queue job 9, 2026-11-01)

## Lead (from research/newspapers-com/ia-il-second-source.md and sweep-2-misers-loot.md)
- The robbers were seen driving to the edge of an 80 owned by "an old farmer and trapper", then going into the brush for about 1½ hours with an ax and a package.
- Printed distances east of Grand Rapids vary: 2 mi (probably a misprint for 12), 12 mi and 16 mi.
- **The trapper is never named** in any item read.

## Plat sources checked (UMedia coll231 title search "Itasca")
- Only these turned up:
  - "Itasca 31" and "Itasca County" sheet series (16 sheets each; undated survey or road sheets)
  - Road map 1910-19
  - Sectional map 1907
  - Standard maps 1914 and 1929
  - A 1919 peat map and promotional booklets
- **No 1930s Itasca plat book with owner names** is in UMedia, and none is on archive.org.

## Result
- **Not placed.** Under the no-guessing rule, an unnamed 80 cannot be picked out of the townships 12-16 mi E of Grand Rapids along US-2 (Blackberry, Wawina and Ball Club area).
- Needs:
  - the trapper's name (Grand Rapids Herald-Review, Jan 1934; not on newspapers.com), then a c.1930s Itasca plat or 1930s tax rolls; or
  - a later plat (for example the 1950s Rockford Map Pub. farm plat book) if one turns up open.
- No pin change.
