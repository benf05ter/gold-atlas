# kmr-paine-carlton-rr: J. M. Paine mill, Carlton MN (2026-10-31)

**Sanborn, Carlton, Sept 1892** (LoC sanborn04270_001, 3 sheets; viewed in full, not saved to the repo).
- **Sheet 3: "PAINE & CO'S STEAM SAW & PLAN'G MILLS"**: a saw mill (1 gang and 1 circular saw, 70,000 ft/day capacity, 150 HP engine) on a **"Mill Pond"** formed on **Otter Creek**, with log slide, slab conveyor, lumber sheds and "Paine and Company's Lumber Yard (average about 15 million ft)" served by switch tracks.
- **Sheet 2:** the "Paine & Co. Planing Mill". The **Mill pond** borders the **Court House / Jail block** (block 13, between N. 3rd and N. 4th St.).
- Note: these sheets are drawn with north to the left.

**Position:**
- The Carlton County Courthouse is still on 3rd St at ≈ 46.6651, -92.4244 (OSM).
- The 1892 mill pond lay beside that block, and the saw mill on the pond beyond the planing mill, roughly 200-400 m from the courthouse.
- The 1939 MHAPO frame (BXP-1-168) shows no surviving pond I could match with certainty.
- **Best estimate: 46.667, -92.425, r ~400 m.** That is **not within the requested 300 m**. Confidence **C**.
