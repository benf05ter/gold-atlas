# Olson brothers (Sever and Knute, murdered Dec 2, 1936), Bristol Twp, Fillmore Co. MN (2026-10-31)

**Township correction:** Bristol is **T101N R11W**, not R10W. The plate header reads "Township 101 North, Range 11 West of 5th P.M."

**Source:** 1928 *Atlas and Farmers' Directory of Fillmore County* (UMedia coll231:8714).
- Bristol plate p.45 (page ptr 8701): https://cdm16022.contentdm.oclc.org/iiif/2/p16022coll231:8701/pct:14,57,50,13/1600,/0/default.jpg
- Bristol farmers' directory (ptr 8700, 8713).

**Directory (1928):** "Olson, Knute A., Rt 3, sec 20, O 80 ac, sec 19, O 40 ac, (58)". The 58 is years in the county.
- **No Sever Olson entry.** Brothers often listed under one name.

**Plate (1928):**
- **"Knute A. Olson 80"**: the W½ NW¼ of **sec 20**, with a house mark near its SW part, about 0.3 of the section S of the N line.
- **"Knute A. Olson 40"**: the SE¼ NE¼ of **sec 19**, adjoining W across the 19/20 line.
- **Neighbours:** Torger Torgerson 79.50 and Anders Torgerson et al 80 (sec 19 NE); Wesley Jones 40 and F. A. Berning 100 (sec 20 N); John J. William 40 and John J. Williams 280 (sec 20 S/W); David I. Davies and R. J. Owens (sec 19 W).
- **Bristol village** (store, church, school) is at the N line of secs 20/21. The **Bristol Centre Stock Farm** (Wm. H. H. Broadwater 160) is in sec 22.

**Other Olsons:**
- "Olson Bros., sec 21, R 180 ac, O Jno. T. Healy": in the directory of another township (page 35), not Bristol. The coordinator may want to check that it isn't Bristol sec 21 (which on the plate shows Hugh Jones / Dornink / Meinen, not Olson).
- "Olson Bros., Rt 2, sec 11 / sec 10, O N. H. Olson": in another township (page 56).
- **No other S. or K. Olson on the Bristol plate.**

**Location (PLSS from BLM, sec 20 T101N R11W: -92.1893..-92.1691, 43.5297..43.5443):**
- **Knute Olson house mark ≈ 43.5400, -92.1884** (±150 m), at the W edge of sec 20, ~0.3 mi S of the Bristol village road.
- That is ~1.7 km WSW of the Bristol village crossroads, and ~5 mi WNW of Harmony. It fits "near the Bristol Center store, near Harmony".
