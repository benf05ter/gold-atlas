# Delaney "boiling spring" money, sec 22 T16N R6W (Coldwater Twp), Isabella Co. MI: plat job 16 (2026-11-01)

Lead: money (said to be 1812-era) buried at the Delaney boiling spring, Coldwater Twp. Newspapers gave nothing usable (Isabella has no papers on newspapers.com; see newspapers-com/pc-a-asks-results.md B2-4). The section comes from the coordinator's job card.

Sources (view-only):
- BLM PLSS CadNSDI: PLSSID MI190160N0060W0, sec 22 = lon -85.02857..-85.00855, lat 43.75541..43.76987.
- USGS 1:62,500 *Lake, Mich.* 15' quadrangle, 1959 edition (TNM item 278264; from 1957 aerial photographs). Sec 22 sits in the far SE corner of the quad. GeoTIFF read in the scratchpad for viewing only.
- No 1930s-40s Michigan aerial source on this PC (as in job 4). The 1959 topo is the closest thing to that era I could reach.

## What the 1959 topo shows in sec 22
- **Brinton** (hamlet) is on the N line at the NE corner; most of the buildings are just N of the line, in sec 15. BM 1038.
- **Odd Fellows Cemetery** is in the NW¼, just S of the N line (≈ 43.7686, -85.0218). Spot height 1054 at the NW corner.
- A **creek** cuts the SW corner of the section, flowing SE through a marshy valley, and crosses the S line at spot height 988 (≈ 43.7556, -85.0282). The creek name is cut off on the crop ("...Creek").
- **Marsh/wet ground**: a patch in the W centre (≈ 43.7620, -85.0238) beside a steep 1000 ft contour bench, and small marsh spots across the S half.
- **Farmsteads** (building dots): one on the W line (≈ 43.7658, -85.0284); three along the E-line road (≈ 43.7635, 43.7602 and 43.7559, all at -85.0092); one near the S line with small closed depressions next to it (≈ 43.7560, -85.0210).
- **No spring symbol or named pond** anywhere in the section.

## Verdict
- **Not placed.** The 1959 map shows no spring. The best guess for a "boiling" spring is the creek valley in the SW¼, where the land drops about 60 ft from the 1054 upland to the 988 creek crossing and the wet ground sits at the foot of the slope.
- The SW¼ farmstead next to the depressions (≈ 43.7560, -85.0210) and the W-line farmstead (≈ 43.7658, -85.0284) are the two buildings nearest that wet ground.
- Suggested pin: SW¼ creek valley ≈ 43.7600, -85.0250, r600 (covers the creek, the marsh and both buildings). Confidence D: the section comes from a secondary source and no feature was identified.
