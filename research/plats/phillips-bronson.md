# "Old Canary Phillips farm", 2 mi E of Bronson, Branch Co. MI (2026-11-01)

Source: 1894 *Illustrated Atlas and Columbian Souvenir of Branch County* (archive.org `2911314.0001.001.umich.edu`). Read by eye, view-only:
- Bronson Twp T7S R8W, p. 21 (leaf 25);
- Bethel Twp T7S R7W (leaf 27), N half.

## Result: not placed 2 mi E
- **Bronson village** sits in secs 11-12 at the E edge of Bronson Twp, so "2 mi E" falls in **Bethel Twp, about secs 8-9**.
- The **Bethel N half (secs 1-18) has no Phillips.** Sec 8 is Barzilla Short and H. Fowler; sec 9 is H. H. Smith, P. A. Buck and others.
- **The only Phillips in Bronson Twp is "C. N. Phillips"**, a long strip in the **E part of sec 5, T7S R8W**, NW of the village. That is about 2 mi **W/NW** of the village, not E.
  - The OCR patrons list has a "C. W. Phillips, Bronson P.O." in it; the initial may be the same man.
  - A Canary/C. Phillips at sec 5 would mean the 1939 article's "east" is wrong, or the farm changed hands. This is unconfirmed.
- Batavia (N) and Bethel S half were not read. The 1915 atlas OCR has no Branch Twp Phillips, and Kregger was not checked.

**No pin change proposed.** Candidate if the direction is wrong: C. N. Phillips, E part of sec 5, T7S R8W, about 41.90, -85.22 (not georeferenced). Confidence D.
