# Mons Jacobson (recluse, killed July 1932): plat placement (2026-10-31)

**FOUND: "Mons Jacobson" in DORA Twp (T136N R41W), not Star Lake Twp.** His land runs right along the Dora/Star Lake township line.

## Source

- **Dora plate, printed p.41** (UMedia page ptr 10590): https://cdm16022.contentdm.oclc.org/iiif/2/p16022coll231:10590/pct:12,64,40,13/full/0/default.jpg

- **Dunn** (T137N R42W, ptr 10598): not needed.

## Location

- That is about 21 mi NNE of Fergus Falls, which fits "~20 mi N".

## Newspaper sources (Chronicling America, free; AP wire)
- **Bismarck Tribune 1932-07-16 p6**, "Recluse Found Dead" (Fergus Falls, July 16): Mons Jacobson, 75, "recluse of Star Lake township", was found dead, bound and gagged with head wounds, in a BARN on his farm on Friday night (July 15, 1932). "Apparently the victim of a robber." https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn85042243/1932-07-16/ed-1/?sp=6
- **Bismarck Tribune 1932-07-18 p3**, "St. Paul Man Is Held In Murder of Hermit":
  - H. Williams, alias "Blackie", alias George H. Johnson, was arrested at Fargo.
  - Jacobson "was beaten brutally and shot to death apparently by a robber who attempted to make him REVEAL WHERE HE HID HIS MONEY".
  - Coroner's verdict: gunshot, suffocation and strangulation. The body was tied to a horse's stall in the barn.
  - https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn85042243/1932-07-18/ed-1/?sp=3
- **Bismarck Tribune 1932-07-25 p1** and **Sauk Centre Herald 1932-07-28 p5** (August Grage sniper killing): "Mons Jacobson, aged recluse who lived on a farm 20 MILES NORTH OF FERGUS FALLS... tortured before he was slain... motive apparently robbery."
- **Bismarck Tribune 1932-08-16 p3**, "Ask Expert Probe Of Mystery Bones":
  - Human(?) bones were found in a BURNED BARN on the farm of HENRY ABBOTT, near the Jacobson farm.
  - Theories: the Jacobson killer committed suicide there, or (since two men probably killed Jacobson) one killer murdered his accomplice and burned the barn.
  - https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn85042243/1932-08-16/ed-1/?sp=3
- **Bismarck Tribune 1933-06-09 p7** and **Sauk Centre Herald 1933-06-15 p2**: the Charles Kaikomen blackjacking in Paddock twp is listed as the fourth mysterious assault in a year.
- **Bismarck Tribune 1933-12-04 p1**: a Silronen/Holman was arrested in the Kaikomen case. Jacobson's and Grage's "slayers never had been apprehended".

## Takeaways
- **Second sources:** Star Lake twp area; 20 mi N of Fergus Falls; tortured to reveal his hidden money; died July 15, 1932; never solved.
- **Nothing found on whether the robbers got the money,** or whether any was found later. The "Star Lake township" in the wire copy vs. the Dora sec 32 plat parcel is explained by the land lying on the township line; his post office/community was likely Star Lake.
- **Henry Abbott's farm** (burned barn, mystery bones, Aug 1932) is "near" the Jacobson farm. Look for Abbott on the Dora or Star Lake plates; he was not seen on Star Lake p.40, so check Dora.

## Henry Abbott farm (burned barn, bones, Aug 1932): NOT PLACED
- **Sauk Centre Herald 1932-08-18 p4**:
  - The barn burned on a Thursday night while Abbott and his sons were in North Dakota; only Mrs. Abbott was home.
  - Abbott found the bones on his return. Dog bones were also in the ruins.
  - "The Abbott farm is HALF WAY BETWEEN the farms of Henry Gjage [August Grage, shot 3 mi N of Fergus Falls on the Otter Tail lake road] and the farm of Mons Jacobson."
  - https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn89064489/1932-08-18/ed-1/?sp=4
- **So the target is about 10-11 mi N/NE of Fergus Falls**, between Grage (~3 mi N of FF) and Jacobson (46.545,-95.871).
  - Midpoint ≈ 46.44, -95.97: the Maine / Friberg / Elizabeth twp area.

## 1939 air photo check of both candidate quarters (MHAPO da04084, flown 7-15-39, centre 46.5284,-95.8633)
https://geo.lib.umn.edu/dnr_historical_aerial_imagery/airphotos/usda/da/y1939/da04084.jpg

**Geometry used:**
- The photo shows the township-line road (Dora/Star Lake) crossing the N-S road (county road D) at the shared corner of secs 32/33/4/5, **46.5427,-95.8647**.
- Section-line roads are about 1,910 source px per mile, so about 0.84 m per px.
- Silent Lake lies NW, and a large marshy lake lies SW of the corner.

**A. SE¼ sec 32 T136N R41W (the 1933 "Mons Jacobson" tract): a farmstead site right at the corner.**
- A small square yard enclosed by a tree windbreak (on the N and W sides) sits **immediately NW of the corner**, between the two roads.
- It is about 85 × 80 m, and contains 2 small light objects (small buildings or ruins). **No large barn is visible.**
- Centre ≈ src px (2856,725): **≈ 46.5431, -95.8653** (±40 m).
- The rest of the SE¼ is open fields running N to the Silent Lake shore, with timber W of the fields.

**B. NW¼ sec 4 T135N R41W (the 1888 homestead: S½ NW¼ + lots 3-4): an active farmstead with a grove.**
- There is a building cluster (several light rectangles: house, barn and outbuildings, with lanes) at the S edge of a large tree grove. It is just E of road D where the road bends SW along the marsh/lake edge.
- Centre ≈ src px (3070,1333): **≈ 46.5385, -95.8630** (±60 m). That is about 470 m S and 130 m E of the corner, **inside the S½ of the NW¼**, which matches the 1888 homestead patent.

**Reading:**
- The 1933 plat shows **no Jacobson on the Star Lake plate** (sec 4 had passed to others by 1933). So the sec 4 farmstead in 1939 was probably someone else's occupied farm. It may still be **the original 1888 Jacobson homestead buildings**.
- The small yard at the sec 32 corner is the best fit for **a 1932 recluse's own house/barn on the land he still held**.
- The 1932 reports say the body was found in his **barn**. No large barn is visible at site A in 1939; it may have been removed after 1932.
- **Recommendation:** two tight targets in place of the 900 m circle:
  - **(A) 46.5431,-95.8653**, the sec 32 corner yard (primary for 1932);
  - **(B) 46.5385,-95.8630**, the sec 4 homestead cluster (original 1888 farmstead).
  - They are about 520 m apart.
