# Tweit family farm, MEDO Twp, Blue Earth Co., MN (2026-10-31)

**FOUND: the Tweit home farm is in Medo secs 34-35.** It is separate from Michael "Twite"'s Vivian sec 14 tract in Waseca Co.

## Township
- **Medo is T106N R25W.** The 1895 header reads "Township 106 North, Range XXV West"; the 1921 header reads "T106N R25W".
- It is **not T105N**. Danville is T105N R25W, S of Medo.
- **Medo's E edge is the Waseca Co. line** (Freedom twp, T106N R24W). It does **not** touch Vivian (T105N R24W) except at the corner past sec 36.
- So the Medo farm does not touch Vivian.

## 1895 *Standard Historical Atlas of Blue Earth County* (UMedia coll231:4046)
Medo list continued on printed p.19 (page ptr 4014): https://cdm16022.contentdm.oclc.org/iiif/2/p16022coll231:4014/pct:5,15,47,70/1400,/0/default.jpg
- **Sec 34, sub-div 3: "T. T. Tweet": N½ of SE¼ & SE¼ of NE¼, 120 acres**, land assessment $1,572, **structural assessment $218**. That means buildings stood on it.
- **Sec 35, sub-div 1: "T. T. Tweet": N½ of SW¼, 80 acres** (no structure listed).
- Total about 200 acres. The secs 34 and 35 parts adjoin across the 34/35 line.
- Neighbors: Barney Olson, Herm. J. Plageman, Fred Plageman, John Germo, A. Knudson (sec 34); O. G. Lindland, A. P. Tonstad, S. Quamm (sec 35).

## 1921 *Blue Earth County Atlas* (coll231:13517)
Medo plate, printed p.45 (ptr 13484): https://cdm16022.contentdm.oclc.org/iiif/2/p16022coll231:13484/pct:40,62,32,13/1400,/0/default.jpg
- **"Thos. T. Tweit Est. 120"**: the E part of **sec 34**, the same tract as 1895 (E½ of the SE¼ / SE¼ of the NE¼ area).
  - The estate label shows that Thomas T. Tweit was dead by 1921.
  - **A building mark ("1") is printed just S of the label**, inside the tract.
- **Sec 35, W part (where the 1895 "N½ of SW¼" was):** an unnamed "80" (no owner name printed) next to Walt. Lentz 80. It may still be family land, but that's unconfirmed.
- Neighbors in 1921: J. O. Lindeland 40 (N), B. Strom / C. N. Wright (S), Peter E. Olson and F. & B. Olson (W), O. E. Plageman (sec 35 S).

## Location
BLM PLSS sec 34 T106N R25W: lon -93.8282 to -93.8081, lat 43.9351 to 43.9498.
- **Tweit tract (E part of sec 34): about 43.942, -93.811.**
- **Building mark (1921): about 43.941, -93.812** (±200 m).
- That is about 4 mi E of Mapleton and about 5 mi W of the Waseca Co. line. It is about 9 mi WNW of the Vivian sec 14 "Twite" tract (43.896,-93.667).

## Reading
- **T. T. (Thomas T.) Tweit** owned the Medo home farm from at least 1895 until his death (estate by 1921).
- **Michael Tweit** (d. 1927) also owned the Vivian quarter in Waseca Co. (1914).
- **Mamie Shanahan** was "brought from Chicago as an infant to the Tweit farm in Medo" (Mankato Free Press 1914-09-04), so **the Medo farm with buildings is the family home site** and the strongest search spot. The Vivian tract is secondary.

**Georeference:**
- Road intersection = the NE corner of sec 34 (the N-S road on the 34/35 line, with the E-W road on the N line); it bends around a slough just N of the corner.
- Mile ≈ 1,377 src px (≈1.17 m/px).

**The only farmstead in the E½ of sec 34:**
- A building cluster (house, barn and several outbuildings) with a windbreak grove on its W/N side.
- It is reached by a lane running ~180 m W from the 34/35 line road.
- It lies in the **N part of the SE¼, E half**, i.e. inside the 1895 "T. T. Tweet N½ of SE¼" parcel that carried the $218 structural assessment.
- **Tighter coordinate: ≈ 43.9394, -93.8113** (±60 m).
- This is about 180 m S of my plat-based estimate (43.941,-93.812), and it supersedes it.
- The rest of the tract is cropland. A small woodlot (tree square) at the W edge of the SE¼ (~43.944,-93.8185) has no buildings.

**Caveat:** the georeference rests on the road grid, not ground control. Check against a modern aerial: the lane from the 34/35 line road and the farm grove may still exist.
