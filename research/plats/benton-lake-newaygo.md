# Benton Lake, Newaygo Co. MI: is there a second one "a few miles NE of Fremont"? (2026-11-01)

The 1926 article places a Benton Lake a few miles NE of Fremont. Checked view-only.

## Sources checked
- **USGS GNIS** (carto.nationalmap.gov geonames service):
  - Layer 7 (hydrographic) was searched across the box -86.3 to -85.4, 43.2 to 43.9.
  - Layers 3, 12 and 13 (populated places and historical features) were searched for any name containing "Benton".
- **OSM / Nominatim** water features named "Benton" in the same box.
- **USGS historical topos covering NE of Fremont** (TNM product list):
  - 1:62,500: Fremont 1958 and White Cloud 1959.
  - 1:24,000: Fremont 1985, Newaygo 1985, Dayton Center 1985, White Cloud SW/SE/NW/NE 1976, Walkup Lake 1985, Woodland Park 1985.
  - 1:250,000: Midland 1954-66.
  - No pre-1950 large-scale USGS sheet covers this area.

## 1900 Ogle *Standard Atlas of Newaygo County* (archive.org 3927906.0001.001.umich.edu) (2026-11-01)
No 1920s Newaygo plat is on archive.org. The 1900 atlas and the 1880 atlas are the only ones there.

- **BLM PLSS:** Benton Lake (43.6714, -85.8884) is in **sec 20 T15N R13W**. In 1900 that was **Monroe Twp**; Merrill Twp was organised later. The Monroe plate is leaf 54.
- **On the 1900 plate the lake in sec 20 is labelled "BLUE L."**, so today's Benton Lake was Blue Lake in 1900. Nothing on the plate is called "Benton".
- **Owners around the lake (1900):**
  - C. E. Brown (N half sec 20)
  - **Baker Lumber Co.** (strip on the E side of sec 20 and in sec 21; also "Baker Lumber Co." at the 20/21 line)
  - G. H. Kimmel, F. M. Brown, H. Van Assen, M. Crawford, F. F. Watson, P. Rhynard and J. Mason (S part)
  - D. K. Pierson and C. Eaton (N edge)
  - The "Northwestern Loan & Trust Co." holds land in sec 19.
- **No C. E. Fish.** C. E. Brown is the nearest name.
- **No village on the lake.** Otia P.O. (sec 13/24 of the W half of the plate), Brookings, and the Pere Marquette line are about 3-5 mi W.
- The OCR of the whole atlas has **no "Benton Lake"**. The only Benton hit is a farmer, C. G. Benton, sec 20 T. Home, P.O. Lilley; Home Twp is directly N (T16N R13W). The lake may have been renamed after him or his family.
- **No "Benton" lake near Fremont** in 1900 either. The lakes NE of Fremont in Sherman Twp are Robinson/Big, Crystal, Bass, Mud, Long, Peck and others (outline map, leaf 14).

## Result: only one Benton Lake
- **Benton Lake (GNIS id 1619168), Merrill Twp, Newaygo Co.: 43.6714, -85.8884.**
  - It is on the **White Cloud NW 1:24k (1976)** sheet and the **White Cloud 1:62,500 (1959)** sheet.
  - It is about **23 km (14 mi) N by NE of Fremont** (Fremont centre 43.4675, -85.9420), not "a few miles".
- **No second Benton Lake** appears in GNIS (current or historical), in OSM, or near Fremont.
- The 1926 "few miles NE of Fremont" either means this lake with a loose distance, or a local name for a smaller lake that the USGS never recorded.
  - I did not read the topo sheets' small-lake labels by eye. A 1920s Newaygo plat could show a local "Benton" lake name.
