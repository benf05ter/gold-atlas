## Settler named in the 1887 atlas history (leaf 14)
- The 1887 atlas history says: "Among other early settlers were, William Strong, Henry Searls, in Section 3; Hiram Wells, in Section 11. **Matthew Paddock, in Section 11**; Ephraim Murdoch, in the same section ..."
- So a Paddock was an early settler (about the 1840s-50s) in **sec 11**. He no longer appears on the 1873 or 1887 plates.
- The 1897 finder could be a descendant, or a later owner of the old Paddock place.
