# Gottlieb Mattrich (Mattrick) farm, Town of Beaver, Marinette Co. WI (1925 buried money) (2026-11-01)

Source: WHS 1912 Ogle *Standard Atlas of Marinette County* (W0174RP), plate "Township 31 North, Range 19 East" (AssetLink 16b76c60j58672on665j2frowbu0xain). Read by eye, view-only.

## Parcels (spelled "Mattrich" on the plate)
- **Gottlieb Mattrich 120: sec 17, T31N R19E, S-central part.** That is roughly the SE¼ NW¼ plus the NE¼ SW¼ area, between Wm. Mattrich's land on the W and Kannenwerfe & Glaube 80 on the E.
  - There is a **house mark labelled "Res."** at the NW part of the parcel, just S of the E-W road along the centre of sec 17.
  - "Little Pelican Lake" and "Big Pelican Lake" lie just SE.
- **Wm. Mattrich 80:** sec 17, W of Gottlieb's parcel, next to the Saw Mill at the SE shore of the lake that straddles secs 17/18.
- **Wm. Mattrich 40:** NE part of sec 18.
- Beaver village (sec 13) is about 5 mi E. In 1912 sec 13 was all Wm. Brett 640.

## 1938 aerial (WHAIFinder Marinette BIA 8-33, 22 Sep 1938; frame centre 45.1592, -88.1713, ±300 m)
- The frame shows the lake, a N-S road along its E side, and the two small Pelican ponds to the SE.
- **Farmstead candidate:** a cluster of buildings with yard, N of the pond pair, about **45.1588, -88.1614**. Its position matches the plat's "Res." mark relative to the lake and ponds.
- A second, smaller building group sits just E of the lake road, at about 45.160, -88.166. That is probably the Wm. Mattrich / saw-mill area.
- Sec 17 (BLM) runs -88.1665 to -88.1457 and 45.1539 to 45.1688.

## Pin
- **Gottlieb Mattrich farmyard: 45.1590, -88.1612, r200.**
- **Confidence C+.** The parcel and house mark are firm from the 1912 plat; the aerial match is relative to the lake and pond pair, with a frame-centre georeference of about ±200 m.
- Rural. The 1925 burial spot (20 ft W of a birch, 30 paces from the $3,000 recovery) cannot be resolved below the farmyard.
