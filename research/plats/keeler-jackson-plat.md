# Keeler, Liberty Twp, Jackson Co. MI: pin check (2026-10-31)

**Pin 42.1290,-84.4023 falls in sec 14 T4S R1W** (BLM: -84.4029..-84.3831, 42.1167..42.1314). It is at the W edge, in the N part.

**1874 *Combination Atlas Map of Jackson County*** (archive.org 3927884.0001.001.umich.edu, leaf 48, printed p.34). Read earlier; details in research/keeler-plat.md.
- **"Noah Keeler": the NW¼ of sec 14**. The label spills W into the NE corner of sec 15.
- **Two house marks at the W edge of sec 14**, on the N-S road along the 14/15 line, about ⅓ of the way down the quarter: **≈42.129, -84.4025**. The pin sits on them.
- **Neighbours:** S. H. Holmes (NE¼ 14); Jas. Lewis (N sec 15); M. Kerr (S sec 15). A small lake lies just S (S sec 15 / N sec 22).
- **No other Keeler in the township.** ("T. Keeling", sec 17, is a different name.)

**Later plats:** no 1894 or 1911 Jackson Co. atlas was opened this pass. Noah Keeler died in 1892, so a later plat would show his heirs or buyers.

**Verdict:** the pin is correct as placed (tier B holds). Optional radius ~150 m.
