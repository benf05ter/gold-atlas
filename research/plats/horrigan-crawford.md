- **Relation to the Scott sec 2 pin:** this block is in Clayton's SE corner. Clayton sec 36 adjoins the **SW** corner of the Town of Scott (T10N R3W sec 31), not sec 2. Scott sec 2 ("Jno. Horrigan 167.68", 43.376, -90.700) is about 7 mi NE, on the N edge of Scott.
- **So there are two Horrigan holdings:** Jno. in Scott sec 2, and the larger C.M./S. "Horgen" block in Clayton secs 26/27/35/36.
