# Rechenberg, West St. Paul / Dodd Road, Dakota Co MN (PC b, 2026-11-02)

Source: Ancestry 1127, **Dakota 1916** atlas (50 images; no 1920s Dakota atlas in 1127). Viewed only.
- Outline map (viewer p.3): West St. Paul is at the N tip of the county, against St. Paul.
- **Mendota Twp plate, viewer p.5 (image 31617_072133-00789):** the N part is Mendota village, Pike Island, and owners Small, Burns, Dehrer, Wagenknecht, Franzmeier, Martinson, Pommerening, Darsaw, Roeller and others. The E edge is marked "WEST SIDE" (the West St. Paul line), with "Outlots" and "Glenford" in the NE corner. **No Rechenberg seen** on the read part of the plate (secs 22-36 in the N half).
- **Farmers' directory, viewer p.4:** the "West St. Paul Post Office" sections (Brown, Bruder, Chapdelain, Erickson, Fee, Heuer, Larson, Motz, Oberg, O'Neil, Pagel, Polzin, Schindeldecker, Timm, Wescott; then Frammeir, Frammier, Larson, Roeller, Sell) have **no Rechenberg**. The Mendota P.O. and Rosemount P.O. sections also have none.
