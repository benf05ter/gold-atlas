# Eliza Wilson home: "up Big Elk Creek, where old No. 12 crosses, site of the old mill" (2026-10-31)

**Result: no Wilson parcel found on the plat.** There is a strong mill-site candidate on Elk Creek near the Eau Claire County line.

## Plat reads (by eye)
- **Elk Mound twp:** W half and Big Elk Creek corridor (secs 4-8, 17-20, 29-32), plus the Elk Mound village area. **No Wilson.**
- **Red Cedar twp:** E side (secs 1-2, 11-14, 23-26). **No Wilson.**
- **Spring Brook twp:** E column (secs 1, 12-13, 24-25, 36), along the Eau Claire Co. line. **No Wilson.** But:
  - **Elk Creek is drawn with a MILL POND at the S edge of sec 13 T27N R11W**, right at the county line, where a road crosses at the 13/24 line.
  - Owners around it: W. F. Gibson 238 (sec 13) and A. M. Anderson 240 (sec 24); Joseph Langdell 160 and C. L. Ford 160 (sec 12) upstream.
  - This is the old mill dam site of today's **Elk Creek Lake** (hamlet ≈44.8138,-91.6503; reservoir ≈44.8187,-91.6484, per OSM).
  - It is about **7.5 mi W of downtown Eau Claire**, which fits "seven miles west of Eau Claire".
- **Big Elk Creek** proper (OSM) runs through **N Elk Mound / Colfax / Wheaton towns** (≈44.93-44.96 N). The 1915 Elk Mound plate shows no mill pond on it in the S part.

## Reading / caveats
- The 1925 phrases ("a mile and a half up Big Elk Creek", "where the old route of No. 12 crosses", "site of the old mill", a planned dam and power house) fit the **Elk Creek mill pond (sec 13 Spring Brook / Elk Creek Lake)** better than anything on Big Elk Creek, on the plat evidence. But the creek name doesn't match exactly, and the route of old No. 12 there is **unverified**.
- Eliza Wilson likely held a small lot, or her land had passed to others by 1915. W. F. Gibson and A. M. Anderson held the land by the pond in 1915.
- **Candidate pin:** the mill-pond dam at the S edge of sec 13 T27N R11W ≈ **44.813, -91.650** (±300 m). This is plat- and OSM-based; I didn't georeference it on a 1938 photo this pass.

## Follow-up (2026-10-31): 1938 aerial and the Eau Claire Co. 1910 Union plate

### (1) 1938 aerial: a DAM and a large POND are present
WHAIFinder **Dunn Co. BRU 1-6, flown 7-28-38** (centre 44.8118,-91.6500): https://web.s3.wisc.edu/rml-aerial-imagery/counties/WI/Dunn/1938/Dunn_WI_BRU_1_6_19380728.jpg
- A **large impoundment**, far bigger than the small 1915 mill pond. It fills the Elk Creek valley from the county line N about 1.2 mi, straddling Spring Brook sec 13 (Dunn) and Union secs 18/7 (Eau Claire).
- The surveyor's pencil notes on the print mark "Dunn / Spring Brook T27N R12W[sic]" and "Eau Claire / Union T27N R10W" on either side.
- **The dam is at the S end of the pond**, where the E-W road crosses the valley on the county-line / sec 13-24 (Dunn) = sec 18-19 (Union) line.
  - **Dam ≈ 44.8137, -91.6515** (±100 m).
  - Small buildings and a lane sit just below the dam on the W bank (likely the power house or mill site). There is also a farmstead cluster on the SE side of the pond and resort-type lanes on the E shore.
- The E-W road over the dam is pencilled **"C.T.-E"** (County Trunk E) on the print. There is no "12" label.
- So the 1938 picture matches the 1925 plan for "a dam and power house" at "the site of the old mill".

### (2) Eau Claire Co. 1910 Ogle atlas: Union Twp T27N R10W (WHS W01BA1H, plate p.79)
https://explore.wishistory.org/AssetLink/i054580yoqac3bafdp5215f4e3q8c7n2.jpg

**WILSON PARCEL AT THE DAM SITE:**
- **"E. T. Wilson 148.40"** holds the **W (fractional) part of the NW¼ of sec 19**, against the Dunn county line. That is the quarter whose **NW corner is the dam crossing**.
- In sec 18 just N: S. S. Wethern 171.48; Elk Creek (drawn) enters at the NW. Also in sec 19: Jas. Brennan 148.80 (SW¼) and **Eliza Duhig** 80 (an "Eliza", but not Wilson).
- **BLM sec 19 T27N R10W:** -91.6498..-91.6301, 44.7994..44.8140.
  - E. T. Wilson's tract centroid ≈ **44.810, -91.646**.
  - Its NW corner at the dam ≈ 44.8139,-91.6498.
- Other Wilsons in Union twp (1910): R. Wilson 120 (sec 5), R. Wilson (sec 4), E & S Wilson (sec 9), all 3-4 mi NE. The sec 19 one is the only Wilson at Elk Creek.
- **About 7 mi W of downtown Eau Claire.** This fits the 1925 "seven miles west of Eau Claire".

### (3) Old US-12 routing: NOT confirmed
- I found no 1920s Wisconsin state highway map online in WHS search.
- The 1915 Webb plates don't carry route numbers (pre-1918 numbering).
- The 1938 photo labels the dam road "C.T.-E", but a 1925 "old route of No. 12" could have been demoted to a county trunk once 12 was rerouted. That is plausible, but unverified.
- To confirm: a 1918-1925 Wisconsin State Highway Commission map showing STH 12 west of Eau Claire.

### Conclusion
**Best placement:** the E. T. Wilson tract, NW¼ (W frac.) of sec 19 T27N R10W (Union), at the Elk Creek dam / old mill crossing.
- **Dam/old mill:** 44.8137,-91.6515.
- **Wilson tract centroid:** 44.810,-91.646.
- The 1925 home on "the site of the old mill" would be near the dam crossing at the tract's NW corner.
- **Still unverified:** that E. T. Wilson = Eliza's family, and the old No. 12 route.
