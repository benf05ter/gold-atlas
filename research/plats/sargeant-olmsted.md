# Sargeant (Sargent) farm between Dover and Eyota, Olmsted Co. MN (1913 express silver) (2026-11-01)

Sources (view-only):
- UMedia 1914 *Atlas and Farmers' Directory of Olmsted County* (Webb; coll231:11406).
  - Dover Twp plate, T106N R11W, p. 21 (ptr 11377).
  - Dover directory p. 22 (ptr 11376).
- MHAPO/UMN 1940 Olmsted air photo WB-2A-184, 7 Aug 1940 (frame centre 43.979, -92.186).

## Parcel
- **Directory:** "Sargent, Louis, R1, sec 18, ac 342½, wf Carrie, ch Clayton, Arnold" (Dover Twp).
- **Plate:** **sec 18 T106N R11W**, the NW section of Dover, against the Eyota town line, is drawn as **"Anthony Hein 342.00"**. The acreage is the same, so the plate shows the previous owner and Sargent held or ran the same farm by 1914.
- **House mark:** one dot at the **E edge of sec 18, just N of centre** (NE¼/SE¼ line area), W of the 17/18 section road.
- **Rail:** the railroad crosses sec 18 diagonally, NW to SE, then runs E into Dover village (sec 16). The plate labels it **"Chicago & North Western"**, not Chicago Great Western.
- **Road:** the Dover-Eyota wagon road runs **E-W along the S line of sec 18**, just S of and roughly parallel to the rail. It continues W into Eyota Twp and E to Dover.

## 1940 aerial
- The road grid matches the BLM bounds: sec 18 runs -92.1991 to -92.1791 and 43.9775 to 43.9919.
- A **farmstead with a tree grove sits on the W side of the 17/18 road, N of the railroad**, at about **43.9844, -92.1800**. This matches the plat house mark.
- **Woods:** sec 18 is mostly open cropland. There is a dense woodlot just E across the road in W sec 17 (about 43.985, -92.176), and scattered trees along the creek and ravines S of the rail.
- A second farmstead sits on the W line of sec 18 near the Eyota line, at about 43.986, -92.199 (a neighbour).
