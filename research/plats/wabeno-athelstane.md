# Wabeno 1921 bank-loot car: pin 45.424,-88.16 (W of Athelstane), plat + 1938 aerial (2026-10-31)

**PLSS:**
- The pin falls in **sec 18 T34N R19E** (BLM: -88.1808..-88.1596, 45.4147..45.4298).
- It is right at the E edge (the 17/18 line), about mid-section.
- Athelstane village is in sec 14 of the same township, ~4.5 km E.

**1912 Ogle *Standard Atlas of Marinette County*** (WHS W0174RP), T34N R19E plate p.46: https://explore.wishistory.org/AssetLink/ce73d2xw8w1hx0k25hl0mi571q0x0dg8.jpg

Mostly lumber-company and speculator (Rollins) land. The Wausaukee River and the railroad run through the township's N/E part.

**1938 aerial: WHAIFinder Marinette BIA 8-12, flown 9-22-38** (centre 45.4274,-88.1584).
- https://web.s3.wisc.edu/rml-aerial-imagery/counties/WI/Marinette/1938/Marinette_WI_BIA_8_12_19380922.jpg
- Scaled from the frame centre (~4.2 m/px in my view), ±150 m.
- **Pin area:** cut-over brush and second growth with darker swamp/conifer strips running NNW-SSE. An **E-W road** crosses the frame ~300-400 m N of the pin (likely the 7/18 or quarter line). A **N-S road** runs ~1.2 km E.
- **Small clearings** near the pin:
  - a rectangular clearing ~350 m NE (≈45.426,-88.157), S of the E-W road;
  - another ~1.4 km W on the same road (≈45.426,-88.178).
- **Faint two-track/trail lines** wander through the brush S and W of the pin. These are old logging trails, typical of 1920s cut-over. None stands out as a specific camp.
- No buildings at the pin.

**Reading:**
- Consistent with "an abandoned logging trail several miles W of Athelstane".
- The aerial can't single out the car site.
- **Suggest:** keep the pin, r ~2-3 km (tier C).
- The two clearings on the E-W road are the only identifiable features in the box.
