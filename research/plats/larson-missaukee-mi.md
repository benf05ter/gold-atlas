# "J. Larson", Lake Twp around Jennings, Missaukee Co MI (PC b, 2026-11-02)

Source: Ancestry 1127, **Missaukee 1906** (57 images, 31617_072123-00332 …). Viewed only.
- TOC is viewer p.3. Township plates on viewer pages 6-21 (atlas pp.11-39: Bloomfield 15, Forest 19, West Branch 21, Butterfield 25, Reeder 29, Richland 33, Clam Union 38-39). Village plats: Lake City (pp.22-23), Moorestown / **Jennings** / Falmouth / Lucas (p.24), McBain (p.25).
- **The Lake Twp plate was not found** in the viewer pages sampled (4-30). It may be a split spread I skipped; still to finish.
- **Jennings village plat (viewer p.24):** the mill town on the W shore of Crooked Lake (Lake Twp). Lots are shown, with streets Laurel, Aspen, Poplar, Elm, Walnut and Cedar and the railway spurs and mill by the lake. **No owner names on village lots.**
- **Patrons' Reference Directory (viewer pp.32-33):** the L section runs Laarman, LaChonce, Lamb, Lampman, Lauffer, Lewis, Lincoln, Locke, Loveless, Lucas, Lutke, Lowery, Lux, and has **no Larson**. It does include Jennings-area people (e.g. LaChonce, "Coal and Wood, Jennings 1897"; Gunnerson, "Farmer and Laborer, Jennings"; Jorgensen, Jordell).
- **1900 census (Ancestry 7602): Joe Larson, b. Jun 1869 Sweden, head, Lake Twp, Missaukee**, with wife Emma (b. May 1876, Sweden) and son Arthur (b. Sep 1898, MI). This is the best match for "J. Larson" in Lake Twp. (Other Missaukee Larsons in 1900 are in Norwich, Richland and Reeder.) The street or section is not in the index; the census image was not opened.
