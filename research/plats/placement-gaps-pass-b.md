## #24 Iwers farm near Sunbury (Cedar Co. IA)

**Proposed pin: 41.6882, -90.9190, r150. Confidence B.**

- **Known from newspapers:** The Iwers homestead was 160 acres, SE¼ sec 35, T80N, "range one". The township is Inland (Cedar Co.), between Sunbury and Bennett.
- **Section bounds (BLM PLSS):** T80N R1W 5th PM, sec 35 runs -90.9379 to -90.9183 and 41.6842 to 41.6989. Sunbury village (41.670) is just S.
- **1885 plat:** H. R. Page *Illustrated Atlas of Cedar County*, Inland twp, p. 82 (archive.org `dr_map-of-inland-township-80-north-range-1-west-of-5th-principal-meridian--2871051`, Rumsey).
  - "**Hans Ives**" holds a large tract straddling the E part of sec 35 and the W part of sec 36. The figure under the name looks like "320".
  - A house dot sits on the E line of sec 35, about two-thirds of the way down the section, which is the SE¼.
  - The 1885 "Ives" is very likely the Iwers father, mis-spelled by the engraver. The three bachelor brothers inherited the land.
  - Also on the plate: "Hans Ives" in sec 27 (S), Lorenz Miller in the N½ SE¼ of sec 36, and Gabriel Struck in the S part of sec 35 W.
- **1930s aerial (Iowa ortho_1930):** a farmstead with grove on the W side of the N-S road on the E line of sec 35, in the SE¼. A second building group sits further S near the SE corner.
  - The plat dot and the photo farmstead agree.
  - The aerial georeference is approximate. I took the section lines as the photo's road lines, ±150 m.
- **Note:** this is a recovered-find pin. The $21,000 in bonds was dug from under an old Mitchell car near the machine shed in July 1936, so the pin marks the farmstead only.

## #22 Ogle farm and dugout near Medora (Warren Co. IA)

**Proposed pin: 41.2273, -93.6260, r800 (centre of sec 9, T74N R24W). Confidence C.**

- **Township correction:** Medora village is at 41.187, -93.603, in **T74N R24W** (Virginia twp). The earlier note's "≈41.236, -93.618" lands in the same sec 9, but the township label there was wrong.
- **1940 plat (warren-ia-plat.md):** an Ogle-family block lies in secs 9, 10 and 15, T74N R24W. Bert Ogle holds 194.5 acres in the NW/W-central part of sec 9.
- **Section bounds (BLM):**
  - sec 9: -93.6357 to -93.6160, 41.2199 to 41.2347
  - sec 10: -93.6161 to -93.5968, same latitudes
  - sec 15: -93.6160 to -93.5968, 41.2052 to 41.2200

## #19 Scott's mill, S of Bloomingdale (Van Buren Co. MI)

**Proposed pin: 42.3578, -85.9385, r250. Confidence C (the mill is not named "Scott" on the plat).**

- **1873 plat:** *Atlas of Van Buren County* (Lake, 1873), Bloomingdale twp T1S R14W, printed p. 13 (archive.org `3928034.0001.001.umich.edu`, leaf 17).
  - The only mill S of Bloomingdale village is an "**S.S. Mill**" (steam saw-mill), marked with two building dots.
  - It sits in the **NE¼ of sec 28, against the sec 27 line**, about ¼ mi N of the section's E-W centre line. It stands on the W shore of a small lake in sec 27, next to the "Isman & Martin 46" parcel.
  - That is about 1.7 mi S of the village.
- **Fit:** a steam saw-mill has a furnace, which matches Veeley's 1898 confession (body burned "in the mill furnace", c.1868).
- **Not named:** the word "Scott" does not appear in the 1873 or 1895 atlas text (full-text search of both items).
- Also on the plate: a Cider Mill in N sec 32 (T. Loehr), not a fit. A sawmill in NW sec 7 (Lane & Cooley) lies NW of the village, not S.
- **Section bounds (BLM, MI190010S0140W0):** sec 28 runs -85.9571 to -85.9373 and 42.3467 to 42.3612.
- **Still to do:** a second source naming Scott, e.g. the 1880 Van Buren history.
