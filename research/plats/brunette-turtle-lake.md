# Hugh C. Brunette cache, Turtle Lake islands near Marcell, Itasca Co. MN: plat job 10 (2026-11-01)

Lead: Hugh C. Brunette, a convicted murderer, asked in July 1935 for a temporary release to dig up $15,000 in gold and securities in a thermos bottle set in concrete on "an island in Turtle lake" (Duluth News-Tribune 1935-07-23 p6, https://www.newspapers.com/image/1131089454/). No island name was printed and no follow-up was found (see newspapers-com/pc-a-asks-results.md ask 18 and ia-il-second-source.md).

Source (view-only): OpenStreetMap, Turtle Lake multipolygon relation 538294 (lake centre ≈ 47.6274, -93.7114). The islands are the relation's inner rings. Overpass query `relation(538294);way(r:'inner');out center bb;`, run 2026-11-01. None of the islands carries a name or `place=island` tag in OSM.

## Verdict
- **Not placed to one island.** Ten islands, none named in OSM. The 1935 print gives no clue to which one.
- The four islands over about 130 m across (53688423, 53688424, 53687894, 53687900) are the likeliest spots for a buried concrete block. The two tiny islets in the SE arm are rock-sized.
- Suggested pin: keep the lake-level pin at 47.6274, -93.7114 with a radius that takes in all ten (about r3000), and list the ten island centres in the stop's notes.
- Next options (not done): local island names from a USGS 1:24,000 topo (Marcell quad) or a USFS Chippewa NF map; any Itasca plat or lake survey that names "Treasure" or "Brunette" island.
