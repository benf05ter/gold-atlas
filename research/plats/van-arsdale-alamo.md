# Van Arsdale hermit lot, Alamo Twp, Kalamazoo Co. MI (died Mar 1907; estate sale 1908) (2026-11-01)

Lead: Kalamazoo Gazette 1908-12-18 p8, County Records: "Frank Van Arsdale to John M. Selkig, land in Alamo, $50".

Sources, all on archive.org and read by eye (view-only):
- **1890** *Illustrated Atlas of Kalamazoo County, Michigan* (2990422.0001.001.umich.edu), Alamo plate, leaf 27 (T1S R12W).
- **1910** *Standard Atlas of Kalamazoo County* (Ogle; 3927871.0001.001.umich.edu), Alamo Township p. 67, leaf 69.
- **1919** *Plat Book and Rural Directory* (3927844.0001.001.umich.edu): OCR text only.

The OCR text of all three books has no Van Arsdale or Selkig; the plates are not OCR'd.

## 1890 plate: Van Arsdale holdings
- **"G. Van Arsdale 20.00": a narrow E-W strip across the E half of sec 19**, at the S edge of the central block. It is directly S of and touching **"J. Selkrig 100.00"**. No house mark is on the strip.
- **"G. Van Arsdale 79.37": SW part of sec 20** (N part of the SW¼), next to the 19/20 line. It has a **house mark** and a **schoolhouse** at its SW corner. This looks like the home place.
- Neighbours: L. D. Bender to the S in sec 19; H. A. Tallman, S. Rector and P. Keller in sec 20. The South Haven RR crosses to the SE, and Alamo Centre station is about 1.5 mi E in secs 21/22.

## 1910 plate (after the 1907 death and 1908 sale)
- **"John Selkreg 120"** in N-central sec 19, with a house mark near the E line (19/20 road). That is up from 100 acres in 1890, consistent with adding the 20-acre Van Arsdale strip. The plat engraver's "Selkreg/Selkrig" is the newspaper's "Selkig".
  - The 1910 subdivision lines in sec 19 differ from 1890, though. Two small "F. Ames 20" parcels appear: one inside the W end of the Selkreg block, and one strip along its S edge. So the exact 20 acres cannot be pinned below the quarter.
- The **sec 20 Van Arsdale 79.37 is "Warner Williams 80"** in 1910, with the schoolhouse still at its SW corner. **Geo. Williams 120** is just N. That Williams cluster in secs 19-20 may be the "Williams" of the lead, though no station by that name is shown on either plate.
