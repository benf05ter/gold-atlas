# Carr gold / Lewis Potter farm, Delhi Twp (T3N R2W), Ingham Co. MI: plat job 1 (2026-11-01)

Lead: 1850 "Carr" (an Englishman back from California) poisoned on the place, then owned by JOSEPH JESSOP; he buried gold before dying. The 1895 find (box of nuggets) was on LEWIS POTTER's farm, "side hill", "4 mi E of Dimondale" (Lansing State Journal 1895-08-20 p1). Potter had bought the farm about 1890. Earlier note: research/potter-delhi-plat.md.

## Parcel (1895)
- "L. POTTER 40" = the NE¼ of the NE¼ of SEC 30, T3N R2W (Delhi). It is the ONLY Potter anywhere on the 1895 Delhi plate.
- House mark: a square dot at the E edge of the forty, on the sec 29/30 line road.
- Neighbours: Amos Frink 128 and F. E. Durfee 80 (W, sec 30); E. Wilson Est. 80 (E, across the road, sec 29); Mrs. S. Markham 20, with a School and a Church at the 29/30 line just S; W. Fisher 60/20 (S).
- Terrain: the GRAND RIVER meanders across the S-centre of sec 30 below the parcel, so the river-valley slope gives the "side hill".

## Parcel (1874)
- The same forty (NE¼ NE¼ sec 30) = "A. J. DOOLITTLE 40 a", with a house mark at the E edge; a SCHOOL is at its SE corner (as in 1895). The Fisher Est. is S; Wilson 80 is across the road in sec 29.
- NO JESSOP appears on the 1874 Delhi plate. The 1874 patrons list has a George Jessop, farmer, in INGHAM TOWNSHIP (not Delhi). The 1850 Jessop ownership is therefore unconfirmed by plat. Jessop may have sold before 1874, or the 1850 tract may be a different parcel.

## Verdict
- Parcel and house mark firm for 1895 (Potter) and 1874 (Doolittle). The Grand River side-hill fits the 1895 find.
- The 1850 Jessop link is not shown on the plats. Confidence B for the Potter farm.
