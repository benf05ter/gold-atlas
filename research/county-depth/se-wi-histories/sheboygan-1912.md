## Treasure, loot and hoard stories

1. **Harrison's buried whiskey barrel, Sheboygan, c.1835-36 (ch. III, p. 59).** A trader named Harrison came to make a claim on the S side of the Sheboygan River with two barrels of whiskey to trade. The first was taken, so he buried the second in the bushes at night. The Indians found it and made him dig it up. This is a comic anecdote; nothing was left buried.
