# The History of Jefferson County (Western Historical Co., 1879): vanished and early sites

Source: archive.org `historyofjeffers00west`. I read the OCR text view-only and kept it in scratch only. Page numbers come from the running heads, ±1.

Coverage:
- Early-settlement chapter: pp. 336-351.
- Watertown: pp. 400-465.
- Jefferson: pp. 466-497.
- Fort Atkinson: pp. 498-519.
- Town chapters: pp. 519-567 (Sullivan, Farmington, Palmyra, Cold Spring, Lake Mills, Hebron, Aztalan, Waterloo, Milford, Ixonia, Oakland, Sumner).

The town chapters are short. Concord, Koshkonong and the remaining towns get little or no separate treatment, and the biographies were not swept.

## Treasure, loot and hoard stories

None in the chapters read.

The nearest items:
1. **Mormon raiders, 1852 (p. 502).** Converts camped on the NW side of Lake Koshkonong robbed Joseph Horan's house at gunpoint, taking household goods. Preston Downing and Heth were jailed.
2. **Horse thief John Conway, 1855 (p. 502).** He stole Rufus Dodge's horse. The horse was recovered.
