## Treasure, loot and hoard stories

Dodge has no buried-money, miser or lost-loot story in the chapters read.

The nearest items:
1. **Seal ring and coins, Beaver Dam (p. 370).** A brass seal ring marked "Gr. B. 1575" and a brass pipe-tomahawk were found by Mr. Shafer about 1860, 8 in. below the sod near the dam that holds Beaver Lake. He also had a Prussian coin of 1690 and a French one dated "1392". These are trade relics, not a cache.
2. **Waupun Bank (p. 508).** When the safe was opened after the bank failed in 1859, it held only "a pack of cards and two dollars in counterfeit money".
3. **Treasurer Traynor embezzlement case (pp. 359ff).** $5,000/$1,000 counts and a claimed deficiency of $21-27k, still in court in 1880. This is a paper loss, not a cache.
4. **1858 Beaver Dam murder (p. 456).** Malone shot Conklin and fled to the woods S of the city. No money involved.
