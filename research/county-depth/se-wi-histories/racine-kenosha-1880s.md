# The History of Racine and Kenosha Counties (Western Historical Co., 1879): vanished and early sites

Source: archive.org `historyracinean00cogoog`. I read the OCR text view-only and kept it in scratch only.

Page numbers come from the running heads, ±1. The exceptions:
- Burlington, Union Grove, Waterford and Rochester (book pp. 471-492) have no readable running heads in the OCR. They are cited by the table-of-contents span, marked "TOC".
- Items found only in the biographies are cited as "bio".

Coverage:
- Racine Co early settlement: pp. 285-330.
- Kenosha Co pioneers: pp. 331-351.
- Racine city manufactories: pp. 459-470.
- Racine town histories: Burlington 471, Union Grove 477, Waterford 482, Rochester 485.
- Kenosha city: pp. 493-552.
- Kenosha town histories: Wilmot 553, Salem Station 556, Bristol 557, Pleasant Prairie 557, Somers 561. These are very short.

The book has no chapters for the other towns (Caledonia, Mount Pleasant, Yorkville, Dover, Norway, Raymond, Paris, Brighton, Randall, Wheatland). They appear only in the early-settlement chapter and the biographies.

## Treasure, loot and hoard stories

1. **Koch farm, Burlington, sec 31, Dec 3 1865 (bio, Burlington Township sketches).** While the family was at church, hired man Peter Frohm tried to rob the house and murdered three children. He "did not succeed in finding the money that was fortunately hidden away". The money stayed with the family; this is not a lost cache. **Sensitive: a child-murder site on private land.**
2. **No buried or lost-loot story was found.** The only other hits were Indians taking belongings in the 1830s (p. 469 area) and a bank's specie table.
