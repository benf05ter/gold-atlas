# History of Walworth County (Western Historical Co., 1882): vanished and early sites

Source: archive.org `historyofwalwort00west`. I read the OCR text view-only and kept it in scratch only.

Page numbers come from the running heads, ±1. The town is assigned by the town chapter the page falls in, using the headings found:
- East Troy 525-551, Troy 552-570, Whitewater 571-656, Delavan 657-731, Darien 732-753, Sharon 754-774.
- Richmond 775-~790, Walworth ~790-807, Lyons 808-819, La Grange 820-832, Bloomfield 833-849, Geneva 850-889.
- Spring Prairie ~890-921, La Fayette ~922-936, Sugar Creek 937-947, Linn 948-967.

Elkhorn and Hudson were not swept separately. Each town chapter has biographies mixed in, and site mentions from them are included.

## Treasure, loot and hoard stories

1. **Geneva bank burglary, Nov 1871 (p. 874).** E. D. Richardson's private bank at Geneva was robbed "of its entire means" the week after the Chicago fire. The OCR reads "130,000", probably $30,000, of which $17,000 was Richardson's own. **"The home plunder was never recovered."** The robbers were later traced to penitentiaries, where they confessed. No hiding place is mentioned.
2. **Voree "golden plates", Spring Prairie, Sep 13 1845 (pp. 902-903).** James Strang sent four men to an oak tree on the E line of Spring Prairie, S of the White River bridge. They dug up three brass plates, 3 ft down, in a baked-clay case under a flat stone. This is a dig site rather than a treasure, but it is a famous, locatable spot (the Hill of Promise).
3. **Elkhorn bank robbery (p. 515, bio).** Robbery of "Mr. R."'s Elkhorn bank before 1869. No amount or recovery is given.
4. **Adam Payne, May 1832 (p. 854, bio).** Robbed of his horse and **$2,300 in gold** and killed on the Naperville-Ottawa road. That is in **Illinois**, out of area. His head was never found.
5. **East Troy area, 1830s (p. 555, bio).** A settler was robbed of all his money by a fellow lodger at Southport (Kenosha).
